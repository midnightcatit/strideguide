# Gym equipment coverage: what a club floor has, and what the app now covers

A wave-6 note so the app can name every piece of equipment a member sees on the
floor, taken from a real club page (Planet Fitness, Worcester MA, the
"Work Out With Us" section) and checked against what the app already carried.

## The floor, as the club lists it

- **Strength**: selectorized (pin-loaded) machines, cable towers, plate-loaded
  (hack squat, leg press, calf raise, supine bench press), free weights
  (dumbbells and barbells), abs and stretching, a dual adjustable pulley, Smith
  machines, and a 30-minute circuit.
- **Cardio**: treadmill, elliptical, rower, indoor cycle, upright bike, stepmill,
  recumbent stepper, recumbent bike, arc trainer, upper body ergometer.
- **Functional**: functional stations (battle ropes, cable tower, kettlebells,
  strength bands, medicine balls) and a TRX station (TRX, medicine balls,
  kettlebells, Bosu).

## What was already covered

The strength floor is fully covered by the machine catalog (selectorized and
cable machines, hack squat, leg press, calf raise, Smith machine, the abs and
stretch work). The cardio is mostly covered as cross-training sessions
(treadmill through the incline walk, elliptical, rower, indoor and road bike,
stair climber, ski erg, swim, aqua run).

## What this batch adds

- **Cardio**: recumbent bike, recumbent stepper, upper body ergometer and arc
  trainer as cross-training sessions, the low-impact aerobic work the plan
  otherwise gets from the bike and elliptical.
- **Functional**: a functional gym circuit session (battle ropes, medicine-ball
  throws, TRX rows, kettlebell swings), plus battle ropes and the two
  medicine-ball moves as catalog entries with demos.

## What is left text-only, and why

- **TRX / suspension training.** Supported by evidence (Marquina et al. 2021,
  systematic review of balls and suspension devices; TRX's own military work), but
  the open frame library has no suspension rows, so it appears in the circuit as
  a step with cues and no demo, rather than a wrong picture.
- **Bosu / unstable-surface work.** The evidence is genuinely mixed: unstable
  surface training can raise trunk muscle activity but has also been linked to a
  drop in force output for the lower body, so it is left out rather than sold as a
  benefit.

## Sources

- Marquina M et al. Effects on strength, power and speed using exercise balls,
  semi-sphere balance balls and suspension training devices: a systematic review.
  2021. PMC7908215.
- TRX Suspension Training: scientific foundations (white paper).
- General low-impact aerobic work: the walking-groups meta-analysis (Hanson and
  Jones 2015) and the load-carriage work in 16a.
