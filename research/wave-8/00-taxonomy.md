# Wave 8 taxonomy: the Calistree exercise library

Calistree (calistree.app) is a bodyweight-first fitness app with a large public exercise
library. Its own mission statement says the exercise library is freely available for
everyone to use in their training. The owner approved using Calistree as a reference for
wave 8: its names and organization are cited facts here, and every description in these
wave-8 documents is written in our own words, never copied.

## What was harvested and how

- Sitemaps listed every datasheet: 4 exercise sitemaps, one muscle sitemap, one
  equipment sitemap. robots.txt allows all crawlers on everything except account pages.
- All 1,961 exercise datasheet pages were fetched into raw/pages/ (about 17 MB of HTML,
  no videos). Each page is server-rendered and carries the exercise name, level, exercise
  types, equipment and muscles in its meta description and JSON-LD, so no JavaScript
  rendering was needed and Firecrawl was not spent.
- raw/parse-datasheets.py parsed the pages into raw/calistree-datasheets.tsv (one row per
  exercise: id, slug, name, alternate names, level, types, equipment, primary muscles,
  full anatomy list). raw/classify.py added the mapping columns and the dedupe match
  against our catalog; raw/matched-names.tsv holds the 260 name matches.
- Dedupe baseline: our 195 catalog names (exercises.ts, machines.ts, bodyweight.ts,
  stretches.ts, mobility.ts) are in raw/our-existing-names.txt.
- Sources: https://calistree.app/sitemap.xml, the /exercises index page, and the
  datasheet pages themselves, for example
  https://calistree.app/datasheet/nordic-curl-e0ee. Muscle-hierarchy pages cited below.

## Headline numbers

- 1961 exercises total.
- After dedupe, 260 entries match our 195-name catalog; 1,701 are names our
  catalog does not carry in any form.

## Calistree's organization

### Levels

Six named levels: Novice (697), Beginner (555), Intermediate (391), Advanced (207),
Elite (80) and Herculean (31). We map Novice and Beginner to our beginner, Intermediate
to our intermediate, Advanced to our advanced. Elite and Herculean are skill-sport
territory (planche, one-arm chin-ups, human flag) and out of scope for a running app.

### Exercise types

Each datasheet carries one or more types: strength (935 carry strength alone),
flexibility (589 carry it), balance (376) and cardio (166), often combined. Calistree
folds stretches, yoga asanas and mobility holds into the same list with a flexibility
type, which is convenient: our mobility focus areas can be fed from the flexibility
slice alone.

### Muscle taxonomy

Calistree organizes muscles as a hierarchy of datasheet pages: whole body splits into
upper body, core and lower body; upper body into back, chest, shoulders, arms, biceps,
triceps, forearms, rotator cuff and neck; core into abs, obliques, pelvis and spine;
lower body into glutes, hamstrings, hip adductors, quadriceps and a legs bucket that
holds the shank and foot muscles (tibialis anterior, gastrocnemius, soleus, fibularis,
flexor hallucis and digitorum, popliteus). Sources:
https://calistree.app/datasheet/whole-body-m03y,
https://calistree.app/datasheet/upper-body-m03z,
https://calistree.app/datasheet/core-m040,
https://calistree.app/datasheet/lower-body-m041,
https://calistree.app/datasheet/legs-m02n,
https://calistree.app/datasheet/back-m00z,
https://calistree.app/datasheet/chest-m02l,
https://calistree.app/datasheet/glutes-m02p,
https://calistree.app/sitemap_datasheets_muscles.xml (full muscle list).

The per-exercise grouping below is derived from the muscles each datasheet lists,
resolved to one area through that hierarchy using the first muscle the datasheet
names (its primary emphasis). Exercises whose datasheets list no muscles, mostly
locomotion and conditioning entries, sit under whole body.

### Compound flows

90 entries (ids w001 to w02i) are combined movements: clean and press, thrusters,
squat press, burpee variants, jump-throughs. They are recipes of two base exercises
rather than new patterns.

## Dedupe against our catalog

- 260 of the 1,961 Calistree entries match our 195-name catalog by exact or
  token-overlap names (raw/matched-names.tsv). Examples both ways: their Nordic curl is
  our Nordic hamstring curl; their Horizontal row alt-names include our Inverted row.
- Each partition in 01-candidates.md ends with a skip list of the matches that matter,
  so the four research sessions do not re-research exercises we already carry.
- Where a Calistree exercise is a near-variant of ours but distinct enough to add (a
  ladder rung or a loadable version), it stays a candidate and the Have column names
  the overlap.
