# Wave 8 candidates: the Calistree harvest, selected

Selected from calistree.app's exercise library (1,961 datasheet pages harvested on
2026-11-27; method and raw evidence in raw/). Each candidate lists our proposed slug,
display name (Calistree's name, cited), our muscle group and movement pattern,
equipment, level, the source URL, and a one-line description in our own words.
Calistree's level words map to ours: Novice and Beginner = beginner,
Intermediate = intermediate, Advanced = advanced. Elite and Herculean skills
(111 exercises) were excluded as circus-skill work no runner plan needs.

Items our catalog already carries are not in these tables; each partition ends
with a skip list naming them so the research sessions do not re-research them.
Where a Calistree exercise is a near-variant of ours but distinct enough to add,
the Have column says what it overlaps.
