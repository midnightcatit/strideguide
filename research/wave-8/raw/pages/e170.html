Hip airplane (Runners hang)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

  • Gemellus inferior
  • Gemellus superior
  • Quadratus femoris

Technique

  1. Balance on one leg and hinge forward until your torso is horizontal, the other leg pointing backward.
  2. Rotate your hips toward the floor, then rotate them away toward the ceiling.
  3. Maintain your balance without using any external support.

Form tips

  1. Keep the standing leg slightly bent and the torso aligned with the back leg.
  2. Focus on rotating from the hip socket of the standing leg.
