Hamstring rock (Dynamic hip flexor and hamstring stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Kneel on one knee with the other leg extended straight forward (heel down, toes up).
  2. Shift your hips backward to feel a stretch in the front leg's hamstring ( Kneeling hamstring stretch ).
  3. Rock forward and bend the front's leg knee to stretch the posterior leg's front hip, try to reach the floor with the pelvis ( Kneeling lunge chest up )
  4. Alternate these stretches before switching sides

Form tips

  1. Keep your back straight throughout the movement.

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
  • Ground (standing)
