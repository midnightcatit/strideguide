One leg knee up

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand upright and shift your weight onto one leg.
  2. Lift the opposite knee upward in front of you while keeping the lower leg relaxed.
  3. Bring the knee up to a comfortable height without leaning backward.
  4. Hold the position for time while maintaining balance.

Form tips

  1. Keep your torso tall and your hips facing forward.
