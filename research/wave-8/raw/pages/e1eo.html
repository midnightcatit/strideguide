Cable straight leg raise

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Attach an ankle cuff to a low Cable machine pulley and strap it to your ankle, facing away from the machine.
  2. Stand tall while holding a support pole or rail for balance.
  3. Raise your straight leg forward into Hip flexion as high as comfortable, pause, and lower back down under control.

Form tips

  1. Maintain an upright torso and avoid leaning backward as the leg lifts.
  2. Keep the lifting leg straight with toes pointed.
