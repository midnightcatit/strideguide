Bear crawl (Cat walk, Traveling beast)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Walk on your hands and feet, with the knees hovering just above the ground and the back flat and horizontal.
  2. Practice until you find a regular, smooth general motion.
  3. You can go forward or backward.
