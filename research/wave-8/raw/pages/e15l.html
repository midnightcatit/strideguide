Zercher deadlift

  • strength
  • flexibility

Technique

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out, a Barbell on the floor in front of you.
  2. Squat down and set the bar in the crease of both elbows, hands clasped or palms facing each other.
  3. Drive through your legs and hips with your back straight until you stand tall.
  4. Lower the bar to your thighs, then back to the floor.

Form tips

  1. Start from blocks to make it easier to get under the bar.
