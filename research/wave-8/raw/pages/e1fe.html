Elevated pigeon forward fold (Box pigeon fold, Elevated sleeping pigeon)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand in front of a box and place your front leg across the top in a pigeon shape, knee bent outward and shin across the box. Keep your back leg straight on the floor.
  2. Hinge at the hips and fold your upper body down over the bent leg, chest toward the shin.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips square to the box as you fold.
  2. Lengthen your spine rather than rounding your upper back.
