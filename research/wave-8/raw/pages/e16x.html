Rolling pistol squat

  • strength
  • balance
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Iliacus
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Perform a Pistol squat and roll onto your rounded back.
  2. Roll forward explosively.
  3. Land on the same foot and push upward to complete the concentric phase of the one leg squat.

Form tips

  1. Use the rolling momentum to return to the single leg.
  2. Keep the back curved for a smooth roll.
