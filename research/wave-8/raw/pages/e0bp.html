Straddle handstand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Enter a standard Handstand position.
  2. Slowly open your legs as wide as possible into a Straddle position .
  3. Maintain your balance while keeping your torso and arms vertically aligned.

Form tips

  1. Keep your legs fully straight and your hips open.
