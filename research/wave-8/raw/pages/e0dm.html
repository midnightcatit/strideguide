Wide muscle-up

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Start from hanging from a Pullup bar with a grip wider than shoulder width
  2. Perform a Muscle-up on the bar.
  3. Finish in a support position above the bar.

Form tips

  1. Requires significant pulling power due to the wide arm placement.
