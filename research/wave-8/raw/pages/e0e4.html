Lateral delt raise (Side raise)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Stand holding a Dumbbell in each hand by your sides, palms facing in.
  2. With a slight bend in the elbows, raise both arms to the sides until they are parallel to the floor.
  3. Lower back down with control.

Form tips

  1. Lower slowly: the descent is half the work.
  2. Do not swing or lean back to lift the weights.
