Hollow body hold arms down (Dish)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Only the lower back touches the ground - the upper back, hips and legs are hovering above the ground.
  2. The whole body is tight and forms a nice crescent shape.
  3. Engage your Abs .
  4. Keep your arms alongside your body.
