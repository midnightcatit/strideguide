Wrist flexors stretch internal rotation

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Get on all fours, with the palms flat on the ground, and rotate your hands inward as much as possible.
  2. You should feel a stretch in your forearms.
  3. Do gentle bounces or little circles back to intensity the stretch.
