Toe stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. This can be done sitting on the ground or on a chair.
  2. Curl your toes in as much as possible.
  3. Then extend and open them as much as possible.

Form tips

  1. Move only your toes: keep your feet and legs at rest.
