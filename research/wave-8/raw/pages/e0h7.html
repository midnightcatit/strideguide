Front support

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Stand next to Stall bars , facing the bars.
  2. Step on the first bar and grab a bar with both hands, with the arms straight alongside your body.
  3. Let your feet hang, so that all your weight is now on your hands and hold that challenging position.
