Double cable rear delt fly (Cable reverse fly, Reverse cable crossover)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Stand in the center of a dual Cable machine with pulleys set at eye height, reaching across to hold the left cable in your right hand and right cable in your left hand.
  2. Step back slightly and hold your arms extended in front of your chest.
  3. Pull your arms horizontally outward and backward, squeezing your shoulder blades together, then return slowly to the starting cross.

Form tips

  1. Cross the cables in front of you so the right cable goes to the left hand and vice versa.
  2. Keep a slight, fixed bend in your elbows throughout the horizontal sweep.
