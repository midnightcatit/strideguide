Band-resisted pike compression

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Sit on the floor with legs straight ( Pike position ) and a band anchored above your head.
  2. Hold the ends of the band in your hands.
  3. Reach forward and down with your chest folding against the band resistance

Form tips

  1. Focus on high Abs engagement. Keep your knees locked.
