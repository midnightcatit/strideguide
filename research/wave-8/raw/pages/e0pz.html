Arch body rock

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Start in an Arch body hold position.
  2. Rock back and forth, swinging between your chest and thighs.
  3. Keep full body tension the whole time, with a nice crescent shape.
