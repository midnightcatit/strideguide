Reverse penguin (Belly penguin, Prone penguin)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum

Technique

  1. Start in an Arch body position, with your arms alongside your body.
  2. Bend the side of your body to touch your leg right above the knee with one hand, then do the same on the other side.
