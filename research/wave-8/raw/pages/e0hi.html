One-arm half crimp

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Reach up to a Hangboard ledge with one hand.
  2. Form a half-crimp position with your fingers (second knuckle at 90 degrees).
  3. Hang freely using only that one hand for support.

Form tips

  1. Do not let your body twist excessively while hanging.
