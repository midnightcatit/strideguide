IT band foam roll (Iliotibial Band foam roll)

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie on the ground on one side, placing a foam roller under your bottom thigh.
  2. Roll up and down on the roller along your leg from the hip to the knee, applying pressure where you feel more tension.
  3. Help yourself move and apply the right pressure with your hands and top leg.
