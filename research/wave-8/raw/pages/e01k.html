Advanced tuck planche

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Balance on your hands while holding your torso parallel to the ground.
  2. Straighten your back and form a 90 degrees angle at the hips. Bend the knees.
  3. Keep the arms straight and the shoulders engaged ( Shoulder depression and Shoulder protraction ).
  4. If using the ground, you can turn your hands to the sides to decrease the pressure on the wrists.
