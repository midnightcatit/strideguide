Downward dog to figure-4 table top (Figure-4 flip, Downward dog figure-4 to reverse table top)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Triceps brachii (long head)

Technique

  1. Start in Downward dog , cross one ankle over the opposite knee into a figure-4 and press your hips high.
  2. Pivot on the grounded foot, rotate your upper body and lift your free arm overhead as you drop your hips into a figure-4 Table top .
  3. Re-engage your core, push through the supporting shoulder and rotate back to the figure-4 downward dog.

Form tips

  1. Keep the supporting shoulder active so you don't sink into the joint.
