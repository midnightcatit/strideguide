Straddle to diamond handstand (Handstand straddle-diamond shape shift)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Hold a solid freestanding handstand in a wide straddle.
  2. Bend your knees and bring the soles of your feet together into a diamond, then extend back out into the straddle.
  3. Repeat smoothly.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders locked in place: the movement happens only in your hips and legs.
