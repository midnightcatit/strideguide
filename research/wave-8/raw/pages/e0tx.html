Neck packing

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Longus capitis
  • Rectus capitis anterior
  • Longus colli

Technique

  1. Look forward, with a neutral neck position.
  2. Pull your chin back towards your neck to create a double-chin.
  3. Engage the muscles in the front of your neck.
