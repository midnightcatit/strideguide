Hanging shoulder circles

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. While hanging, slowly push your shoulders forward (protraction), upward (elevation), backward (retraction) and downward (depression) in a controlled circular motion.
  2. Go slowly and take the time to go to the full range of motion in each quadrant.
