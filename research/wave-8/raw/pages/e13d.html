Kettlebell overhead carry

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Stand tall with a Kettlebell in one hand, the arm is extended over your head in the lockout position.
  2. Walk around maintaining a balanced posture and without wobbling.
