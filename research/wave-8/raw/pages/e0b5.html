Standing shoulder retraction and protraction- arms by the sides (Scapular retraction and protraction)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. While standing or sitting, make your shoulders go back and forth, without activating the rest of your body.
  2. Go slowly and push your shoulders as far as they can go, focusing on exploring the full range of motion in Shoulder protraction and Shoulder retraction .
