Standing quad stretch

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand up and flex one knee and grab the foot with both hands behind your back,balancing on the other foot

Form tips

  1. Keep a posterior Pelvic tilt to increase the stretch at the front of the thigh
  2. Keep the stretched leg vertical
