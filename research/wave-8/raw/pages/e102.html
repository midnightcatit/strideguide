Wall straddle stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie on your back, with your butt and legs against a wall.
  2. Open your legs out to the sides into a Straddle position and make it as wide as your flexibility allows.
  3. Relax into this position, taking slow deep breaths.
