Partner squat overhead ball throw

  • strength

  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Stand facing your partner, one of you holding a Medicine ball at chest height.
  2. Squat, then as you rise, explosively throw the ball overhead to your partner.
  3. Your partner catches it and immediately sinks into a squat, bending arms and legs to absorb the ball.
  4. Keep passing back and forth.

Form tips

  1. Drive the throw from your legs and hips, not just your arms.

Risks

  1. Start with a light ball.
