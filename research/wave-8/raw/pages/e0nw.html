Lying maltese press (Hollow dumbbell press, Dumbbell hollow press for planche)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Lie down on the ground in a Hollow body position ( Abs contracted).
  2. Hold weights and press them up, while keeping your arms straights.
