Straight-arm hang (Passive hang, Dead hang)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Hang from Rings or Pullup bar without swinging.
  2. Let the shoulders come up near the ears ( Shoulder elevation ).

Similar Exercises

  • Scapular pull
  • Split towel hang
  • Twin towel hang
  • Tuck hang
  • Bent-arm hang

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
