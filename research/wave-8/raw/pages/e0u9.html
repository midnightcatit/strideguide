Butcher's block stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Kneel in front of a raised surface.
  2. Place your elbows on that surface, grab a staff with a supinated grip and bend at the elbows.
  3. Push your chest down as far as possible, without arching the back.

Form tips

  1. Keep a posterior Pelvic tilt .
