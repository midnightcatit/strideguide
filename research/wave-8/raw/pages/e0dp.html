Windmill

  • flexibility
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand with your feet wide apart and your arms straight out to the sides.
  2. Hinge at the hips and rotate your torso to touch one foot with the opposite hand, while the other arm reaches up to the ceiling.
  3. Come back up to standing and repeat on the other side, alternating.

Form tips

  1. Keep both arms straight and in one line through the shoulders.
  2. Bend the knees slightly if you cannot reach your foot with straight legs.
