Folded leaf

  • balance

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. L-base to start, with the base placing the feet in a V shape on the flyer's upper thighs.
  2. The flyer pikes and leans their weight onto the base's feet.
  3. The base presses the legs into the flyer's thighs while the flyer holds a strong pike ( Pike position ).
  4. To exit, the base lifts the flyer's arms and brings the flyer's feet to the ground.

Form tips

  1. The flyer thinks about pushing the tops of the feet toward the ground to create strong Hip pockets and maintain the pike.
