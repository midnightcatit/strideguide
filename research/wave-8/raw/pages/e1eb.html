Cable biceps curl single arm (Bayesian curl single arm, Bayesian curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Stand facing away from a low cable pulley and grip a single handle with one hand in an underhand grip.
  2. Stand upright with your arm extended back toward the pulley.
  3. Curl the handle toward your shoulder, pause at top contraction, and lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Focus on isolating the working bicep without twisting your torso.
  2. Keep your upper arm stationary during the entire movement.
