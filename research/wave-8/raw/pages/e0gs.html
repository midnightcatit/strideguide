Inverted kettlebell carry (Inverted kettlebell overhead carry, KB bottom up carry walk)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Carry kettlebell straight up and upside down while walking around.
  2. The walking creates small movements that force your shoulder stabilizers to engage.
