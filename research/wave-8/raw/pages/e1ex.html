Preacher bench biceps curl single arm (Scott bench single arm curl, Supported dumbbell curl single arm, Single arm dumbbell preacher curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Sit at a Preacher bench , placing the back of one upper arm flat against the pad while holding a Dumbbell with a supinated grip.
  2. Lower the dumbbell slowly down the pad until your arm is fully straight.
  3. Contract your Biceps to curl the dumbbell upward toward your shoulder, then return with control.

Form tips

  1. Focus on keeping the non-working shoulder square and quiet throughout the rep.
  2. Maintain full contact between the triceps of the working arm and the bench pad.
