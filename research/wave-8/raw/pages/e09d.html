Rings straight-arm crow pose

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Grip the Rings near the ground.
  2. Place your knees on the back of your upper arms with elbows locked straight as in Crane pose .
  3. Balance your full body weight on the rings.

Form tips

  1. Lean forward slowly to find the balance point.
  2. Keeps the core tight and elbows tucked
