Foot-elevated vertical frog (Foot-elevated cossack hold)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit down on the ground, extend one leg to the side and place that foot on a slightly elevated surface.
  2. Bend the other leg, pull your foot close to your butt and push the knee out to open your hips.
