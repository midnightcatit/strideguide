Human flag jumps

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Jump into a Human Flag position and try to hold it for a second before going back down.
  2. Pull with the top arm and engage the scapula ( Shoulder depression ); push with the bottom arm.
  3. We advise to not work on this progression until you are able to do at least a One-leg human flag or Straddle human flag .
