Jump rope double-under

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Begin jumping rope with a higher jump than a standard skip.
  2. Flick your wrists quickly twice while you are in the air.
  3. Make the rope pass under your feet twice before you land.

Form tips

  1. Use your wrists to spin the rope rather than your whole arms.
