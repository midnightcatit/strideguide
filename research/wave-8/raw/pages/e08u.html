Wrist flexors stretch neutral (Wrist flexors stretch fingers forward, Forward facing wrist stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Get on all fours, with the palms flat on the ground, and your fingers facing forward.
  2. Lean forward to intensify the stretch.

Similar Exercises

  • Palms down fingers to the sides side-to-side
  • Palms down fingers forward wrists circles
  • Wrist flexors stretch external rotation
  • Wrist flexors stretch internal rotation
  • Knee palm raise

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
