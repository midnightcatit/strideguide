Dumbbell front hold

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid

Technique

  1. Stand tall and hold a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Raise your straight arms in front of you to shoulder height, palms facing each other.
  3. Hold this horizontal position for the required duration.

Form tips

  1. Do not lean back to assist the hold.
