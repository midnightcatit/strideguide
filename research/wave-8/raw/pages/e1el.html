Cable standing hamstring curl single leg (Cable standing leg curl single leg)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (short)
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Attach an ankle cuff to a low cable pulley, facing the machine while standing tall and holding the frame for support.
  2. Keep your working thigh vertical and in line with your standing leg.
  3. Bend your knee to pull your heel toward your glutes, contracting your Hamstrings , then lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your thighs aligned and avoid swinging the knee forward.
  2. Maintain an upright spine throughout the curl.
