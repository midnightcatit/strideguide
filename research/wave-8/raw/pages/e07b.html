One-arm hang switches

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Hang from a Pullup bar o Rings with one arm.
  2. Switch the arm without touching the floor
  3. Lower the arms by your side between switches

Form tips

  1. Maintain Shoulder depression on each arm as you switch.

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
