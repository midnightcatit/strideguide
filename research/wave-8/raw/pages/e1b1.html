Weighted horizontal row

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Wear a Weight vest or secure a plate to your chest.
  2. Hang from Rings or a bar at hip height with your body parallel to the floor.
  3. Pull your chest toward the bar by driving your elbows back, then lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a straight line from your feet to your shoulders at all times.
  2. Think about pinching a pen between your shoulder blades at the top.
