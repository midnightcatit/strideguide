Ribs-to-bar pullup (Explosive pullup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Pull yourself up explosively so that you touch the bar with the part of your ribs that is under the pectoral muscles.

  • Kip clap pullup

  • Pullup bar
