Hip-tap dip

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Start in a Support position and bend your elbows to go down with control.
  2. Push back up explosively to be able to tap the side of your hips with both hands before placing them back on the bar.

Risks

  1. Be careful not to miss the bar when landing.
