Parallettes bent-arm handstand (Parallettes shoulder stand, Parallettes yaad hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Grip the Parallettes firmly and lower your head toward the floor between the bars.
  2. Bend your arms to 90 degrees and lift your hips and legs vertically overhead.
  3. Hold.

Form tips

  1. Don't rest your shoulders on the bars: your bent arms, upper back and core hold you up.
