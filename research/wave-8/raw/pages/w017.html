Flying knee lunge (Lunge to explosive knee drive, Lunge hop)

  • strength
  • balance
  • cardio

Technique

  1. Take a long step back and squat on your front leg.
  2. Bring your back leg in explosively, driving that knee upward to generate momentum and jump off the ground.
  3. While landing, directly bring your leg back again and repeat, then work the other side as well.
