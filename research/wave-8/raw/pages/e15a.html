Incline biceps curl (Dumbbell seated incline double biceps curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Sit on an incline bench with a dumbbell in each hand, arms hanging straight down.
  2. Curl the weights toward your shoulders while keeping your elbows back.
  3. Slowly lower the weights back to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Do not swing your arms; keep your upper arms vertical throughout.
