High lizard crawl

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Get on your hands and feet in a high Plank position.
  2. Take a step forward with one hand while simultaneously bringing the opposite foot forward, lowering the chest toward the ground as in a staggered stance push up.
  3. After returning to the top of the staggered plank repeat the step with the opposite limbs to continue crawling forward

Form tips

  1. Maintain a high-hip position compared to the low lizard crawl.
  2. Coordinate opposite limbs for a smooth, rhythmic motion.
