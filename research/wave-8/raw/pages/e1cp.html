Weighted Roman chair back extension with twist (Weighted twisting back extension, Weighted rotational hyperextension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Secure your ankles in the Roman chair and hold a weight plate or dumbbell against your chest.
  2. Hinge at the hips to lower your torso toward the floor.
  3. Raise your body until it aligns with your legs, then twist your torso to one side at the peak.
  4. Un-twist back to center, lower down, and alternate sides on the next repetition.

Form tips

  1. Perform the rotation at the top of the extension in a controlled manner without jerking.
  2. Hold the weight securely against your chest.
