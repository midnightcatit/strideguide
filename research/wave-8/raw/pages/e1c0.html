Single-leg leg extension (One-leg leg extension, Unilateral leg extension, Single-leg machine leg extension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Sit in the Leg extension machine with the padded lever positioned over the lower shin of your working leg.
  2. Grasp the side handles for stability and extend your working leg upward until it is straight.
  3. Pause at the top contraction to engage the Quadriceps , then lower the lever back down with control.

Form tips

  1. Adjust the machine backrest so your knee joint aligns directly with the machine's pivot point.
  2. Keep your torso stationary and back flat against the seat; do not swing your upper body.
