Lotus V-sit (Lifted scale pose, Utpluthih, Tolasana)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. From a seated position with legs in front of you, bring your legs into Lotus , right leg first.
  2. Place your hands on each side of the tights.
  3. Lift up and bring the legs to the chest. This requires a lot of core compression.

Form tips

  1. Place the wrist in line with the shoulders
