Partner shoulder stand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Begin in Low bird , with the base bending their knees to lower the flyer so their shoulders are in the base's hands.
  2. The flyer will control their weight by pressing their hands into and down the base's forearms.
  3. To exit, the base will return their feet to the flyer's hips, and bring the flyer back to low bird.

Form tips

  1. The flyer and base should maintain a Hollow body and it is incredibly important for the flyer to maintain their shape through the entire exercise.
  2. The flyer should not hinge at the hips, but maintain the shape and allow their body to lever off the base.
  3. The base should be bending knees and elbows to softly receive the flyer.
