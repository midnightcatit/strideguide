Kipping muscle-up

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Hang from a Pullup bar and initiate a slight forward swing.
  2. Use an explosive kipping motion with your legs to drive your body up.
  3. Transition your weight over the bar and push into a Support position.

Form tips

  1. Time your pull with the peak of your forward swing.
