Half crimp pullup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Grab a Hangboard ledges with a half-crimp finger grip.
  2. Perform a Pullup by pulling your chin toward the board.
  3. Lower yourself back to the starting hang with control.

Form tips

  1. Ensure your crimp grip does not slip into an open hand during the pull.
