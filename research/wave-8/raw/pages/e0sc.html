One-arm bridge

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Start in a Bridge position.
  2. Shift your weight towards one arm and lift the other arm up.
  3. Hold the position for time, then repeat on the other side.
