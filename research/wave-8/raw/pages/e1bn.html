Hip abduction machine (Seated hip abduction, Abductor machine, Machine hip abduction, Outer thigh machine)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Sit in the Hip abduction machine with your knees brought together and outer thighs resting against the padded levers.
  2. Contract your Glutes to push your legs outward as far as comfortable.
  3. Hold the wide position for a moment, then slowly return your knees to the center under control.

Form tips

  1. Lean slightly forward to reach a deeper stretch of the Glutes .
