Wobble board stand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand with both feet on a Wobble balance board , knees slightly bent.
  2. Keep your balance for time, looking straight ahead.

Form tips

  1. To make it harder, slowly move your head around or close your eyes.
