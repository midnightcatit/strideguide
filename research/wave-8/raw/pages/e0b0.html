Standing horizontal shoulder rotations

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand tall with your arms extended to the sides, parallel to the floor, palms down.
  2. Keeping your arms straight, turn your palms up by rotating the whole arm ( Shoulder external rotation ).
  3. Rotate back to palms down and past it ( Shoulder internal rotation ).
