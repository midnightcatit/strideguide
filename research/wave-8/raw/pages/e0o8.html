Swinging rings support

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)

Technique

  1. Start in a rings support position (above the rings, straight-arms).
  2. Push your body forward and backward, trying to lift it up on each side.
  3. Keep your body straight.
