Wall shoulder flexion stretch (Wall lat stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand facing a wall about a step away.
  2. Place your palms flat on the wall slightly above shoulder height.
  3. Push your chest down toward the floor while keeping your arms straight to open your shoulders.

Form tips

  1. Keep your Abs engaged and maintain a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Avoid arching your lower back during the stretch.
