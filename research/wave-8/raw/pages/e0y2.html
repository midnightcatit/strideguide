Arm crossover (Horizontal shoulder adduction abduction, Horizontal arms inside outside)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand upright.
  2. Swing your arms in a more or less horizontal plane, letting them cross over each other in front of you, and then swing behind you towards each other.

Form tips

  1. Alternate between crossing them with the right arm on top and left arm on top.
  2. Aim to touch your hands behind your back if possible.
