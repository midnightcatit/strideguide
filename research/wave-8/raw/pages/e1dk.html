Double cable chest fly downward (Double cable chest fly high to low, Cable crossover high to low)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Stand in a dual Cable machine with the pulleys set to the highest position, holding a handle in each hand.
  2. Step forward with one foot, arms extended out and slightly up to the sides.
  3. Pull the handles downward and inward in an arc until your hands meet in front of your waist, then slowly return to the top position.

Form tips

  1. Keep your core tight and lean slightly forward from your hips.
  2. Bring your hands together near hip or lower abdomen level at the bottom.
