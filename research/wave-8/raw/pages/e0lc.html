Jedi box assisted handstand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Partners will begin in a Jedi box .
  2. The flyer will lift one leg at a time.
  3. The base transitions their hands to the back of the flyer's shoulders to assist their Handstand on the base's ankles.
  4. To exit, flyer will lower one leg at a time back to the base's hands, and then reverse the Jedi box.

Form tips

  1. Both people should maintain a Hollow body .
  2. The flyer should be sure to push through and open the shoulders, while the base counterbalances their weight.
  3. The flyer should be careful when lowering feet to the base's hands.
