Weighted donkey calf raise

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on the edge of a ledge with a Weight belt and weights attached.
  2. Hinge forward at the hips and rest your hands on a sturdy support like a box.
  3. Lift your heels as high as possible into a calf raise, then lower them below the level of the ledge.

Form tips

  1. Use a full range of motion, reaching a deep stretch at the bottom.
