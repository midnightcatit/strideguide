Treadmill sled push (Sled push on curved treadmill, Curved treadmill push, Manual treadmill push, Skillmill sled push)

  • cardio

Technique

  1. Stand on the treadmill belt facing forward with a high resistance setting.
  2. Grasp the front handles firmly and lean your body forward at a 45-degree angle.
  3. Drive forcefully through the balls of your feet to push the belt backward in a continuous stride.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a strong, straight line from your head to your heels with your Core engaged.
  2. Do not round your back as you push against the belt.
