Technique

  1. Stand on one leg.
  2. Squat down to place the hands on the ground
  3. Jump your foot back into a Plank while keeping the other in the air
  4. Jump the foot back toward your hands and explode into a vertical jump, landing on the same leg.

Form tips

  1. Maintain balance on one leg for the entire duration.
  2. Do not let the free foot touch the ground during the plank.
