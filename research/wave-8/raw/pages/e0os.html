Supported QDR pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Squat down and put your hands on the ground in front of you.
  2. Lean forward to one side, turn your chest towards the middle and place the side of your trunk on your elbow.
  3. Touch the ground with your head.
  4. Straighten your legs and lift them both off the ground into a "scissors" position.

  • Ground (hands or body)
