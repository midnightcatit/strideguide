Hand release pushup

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Perform a Pushup .
  2. At the bottom, rest your chest on the floor and lift your hands off the floor.
  3. Place your hands back down and push back up to complete the Pushup .

Form tips

  1. Keep your Abs engaged and a posterior Pelvic tilt , including when lifting the hands off the floor. See Floor W raise for details.

Similar Exercises

  • Palm raise pushup
  • Internally rotated pushup
  • Fingertip pushup
  • Rocking pushup

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
