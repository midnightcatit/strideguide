Blocks jump through forward (Assisted Ashtanga Vinyasa, Blocks jump forward)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Find downward facing dog with hands on blocks.
  2. Bend the knees and crouch.
  3. Jump through the arms with legs crossed.
  4. Extend legs before lowering down.

Form tips

  1. The trajectory of the hips should be close to horizontal. Bend the knees without lowering the hips too much.
  2. Keep weight on the hands even when you crouch.
  3. Keep the gaze forward.
  4. Flex the spine to make room for the legs to go through.
  5. Keep the legs to the chest to clear the floor.
