Jump feet clap

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Start from standing with feet hip-width apart.
  2. Jump high and clap your feet together while in the air.
  3. Land softly bending your knees.
