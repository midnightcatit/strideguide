Wheelbarrow squat

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. The "wheelbarrow" starts on all fours while the "gardener" stands at their feet and lifts their ankles.
  2. The gardener performs a squat while bearing the weight of the wheelbarrow's lower body.
  3. To exit, the gardener slowly lowers the wheelbarrow's feet to the ground.

Form tips

  1. Both partners maintain a Hollow body , especially the wheelbarrow.
  2. The gardener keeps the chest lifted to maintain a neutral spine, and engages the Glutes in the squat.
