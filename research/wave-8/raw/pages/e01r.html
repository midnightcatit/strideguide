Assisted one-arm-chinup (Assisted OAC)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Loop an elastic band around a bar or a ring, place the other end of the loop under your foot and hang from the support with one arm.
  2. Pull yourself up until your chin is above your hand, with your palm facing you.
  3. Lower yourself down with control until your arm is fully extended.

Equipment

  • Long resistance band
  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
