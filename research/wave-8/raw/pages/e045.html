Half layout front lever

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Hang from your arms and pull your upper body to a horizontal position, while keeping the arms fully straight.
  2. Bend your knees but keep your hips straight.

Form tips

  1. The body should be horizontal, with the shoulders, hips and knees on the same line.
  2. Keep your arms fully straight.
  3. Do not let your upper back round.
  4. Engage your scapulas: try to retract your shoulders , so that they end up in a neutral position.
