Supine chin tuck (Supine neck flexion)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Longus capitis
  • Rectus capitis anterior
  • Longus colli

Technique

  1. Lie on your back on the ground or on a bench.
  2. Lift your head up slightly while tucking your chin in.
  3. Lower your head back down with control.

Similar Exercises

  • Assisted front neck bridge front and back
  • Front neck bridge front and back
  • Neck flexion from deep extension
  • Neck packing

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
