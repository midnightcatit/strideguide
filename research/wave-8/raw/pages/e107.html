Wall tuck handstand hold (Wall knee handstand)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Start in a Chest-to-wall handstand position, with your hands placed a small step away from the wall, and only your feet touching the wall.
  2. While keeping the legs together, bend at the knees to bring them down and get your thighs close to your chest.
  3. Hold that position for time.

Form tips

  1. Try to maintain alignment between your trunk and your arms.

Risks

  1. If you fall forward, move one hand forward and carthweel out of the position. If you can't do that, tuck your chin and round your back to do a Forward roll to avoid falling flat on your back.
