Weighted elevated one-leg pike stretch (Box hamstring stretch with weight)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand facing a box with a small weight, such as a Kettlebell or a Dumbbell , in your hands.
  2. Place one heel on the box with that leg straight, standing leg planted.
  3. Hinge forward at your hips and fold over the elevated leg, letting the weight hang down to deepen the fold.
  4. Hold, then switch legs.

Form tips

  1. Keep the elevated knee straight and fold from the hips, chest toward the thigh, rather than rounding your upper back.
  2. Pull your toes back to stretch the calf as well.
