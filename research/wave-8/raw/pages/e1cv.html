Backward treadmill walking (Reverse treadmill walking, Retro walking, Treadmill walking backward, Backward walking)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus

Technique

  1. Stand on the treadmill belt facing away from the front console (toward the back of the treadmill).
  2. Set the machine to a slow walking speed while lightly holding the side handrails for balance.
  3. Reach backward with one foot, landing on your toes, and push through to extend the knee.
  4. Alternate legs continuously in a smooth, rhythmic backward walking pattern.

Form tips

  1. Keep your torso tall and upright throughout the movement.
  2. Roll smoothly from your toes to your heel with each backward step.
  3. Increase the incline for a higher challenge on your Quadriceps .
