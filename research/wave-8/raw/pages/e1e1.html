Assisted reverse nordic (Assisted reverse nordic curl, Cable-assisted reverse nordic)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Kneel on a padded surface with toes pointed back facing a high pulley, holding the cable in front of you for assistance.
  2. Keeping your body straight from knees to head, slowly lean backward by pivoting at the knees.
  3. Lower into a quad stretch, then use your Quadriceps and gentle cable assistance to return to upright.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a straight line from your knees to your shoulders.
  2. Use a band or cable for light assistance to control the leaning depth.
