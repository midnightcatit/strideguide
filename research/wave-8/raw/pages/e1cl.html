Weighted single-leg 45-degree hip extension (Weighted one-leg Roman chair hip extension, Weighted single-leg hyperextension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Set up on a 45-degree hyperextension bench holding a weight against your chest, with your upper thighs on the pad and with one foot locked under the pad, the other leg free.
  2. Hinge at the hip of the working leg to lower your upper body forward.
  3. Drive through the heel of the secured leg, contracting the Glutes and Hamstrings to raise your torso back to the starting line.

Form tips

  1. Keep the working leg aligned with your torso and avoid letting the hips tilt to the side.
  2. Hold the weight close to your chest.
  3. Keep the spine neutral to focus the work on the glutes and hamstrings.
