Arms up straddle pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Perform a standard Straddle pushup , with the difference that from the bottom position, you push yourself up as explosively as possible.
  2. You will need enough speed to get your hands off the ground, lift your arms up quickly and return them to their initial position before your body falls back down.
  3. The wider you open your legs, the more weight is shifted from your hands to your feet, making the exercise easier.
