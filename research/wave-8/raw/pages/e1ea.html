Lat pulldown supine grip (Reverse grip lat pulldown, Underhand lat pulldown)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Sit at a lat pulldown machine with thigh pads adjusted securely over your legs.
  2. Grasp the bar with an underhand (supinated) shoulder-width grip.
  3. Pull the bar down toward your upper chest by driving your elbows down and back, then slowly extend your arms back up.

Form tips

  1. Maintain Shoulder depression and keep your chest lifted as you pull.
  2. An underhand grip increases Biceps recruitment alongside the lats.
