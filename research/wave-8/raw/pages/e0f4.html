Wobble board deep squat

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on a Wobble balance board with feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Slowly lower your hips into a Deep squat while maintaining balance.
  3. Push back up to the starting position without the board edges touching the floor.

Form tips

  1. Maintain equal pressure across your feet to keep the board level.
