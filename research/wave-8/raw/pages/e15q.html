Single-leg box jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus

  • Gastrocnemius

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg in front of a box.
  2. Jump onto the box using only that leg.
  3. Land in a controlled single-leg squat on top of the box.

Form tips

  1. Land quietly on the box.
  2. Stabilize the landing before moving.
