One-leg deficit hip thrust (One-leg double deficit glute bridge)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Biceps femoris (short)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground, with your back to a bench, one foot on an elevated surface in front of you, and the other leg up in the air.
  2. Place your shoulders above the bench and push your hips upward all the way up, activating your Glutes , then slowly go back to the starting position.
  3. Repeat on the other leg.
