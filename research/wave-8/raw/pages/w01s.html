Shoulder-Pressing Pose to Crane Pose

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Lower trapezius
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid

Technique

  1. Take Bhujapidasana ( Shoulder-Pressing Pose sequence )
  2. Uncross the legs.
  3. Flex the spine and take the legs back behind the arms, one at a time or both at the same time.

Form tips

  1. Press through the hand the whole time
