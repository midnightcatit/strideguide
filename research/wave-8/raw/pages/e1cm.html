Weighted Roman chair side bend (Weighted 45-degree side bend, Weighted oblique hyperextension, Weighted side hyperextension, Weighted lateral back extension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Place your hip on the Roman chair pad in a side-lying position with your ankles anchored under the foot pads.
  2. Hold a Weight plate or dumbbell against your chest or in your hand.
  3. Lower your torso sideways toward the floor in a controlled arc.
  4. Engage your Obliques to lift your upper body back up to the starting line.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips square and move strictly in a lateral direction.
