Standing ankle circles

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

  • Extensor hallucis brevis
  • Extensor hallucis longus

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg and keep the other straight in front of you.
  2. Create big circles moving the foot at the ankle, clockwise and counterclockwise.
