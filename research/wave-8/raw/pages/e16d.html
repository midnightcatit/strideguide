L-handstand leg lift (L-shape lift)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Enter a Wall L-handstand with your feet against the wall and your torso vertical.
  2. Slowly lift one leg off the wall and bring it to a vertical position, aligned with your torso.
  3. Hold for a moment, then return the foot to the wall and repeat with the other leg.

Form tips

  1. Focus on keeping your shoulders fully open ( Shoulder flexion backward ).
  2. Aim to keep the supporting leg perpendicular to the wall.
