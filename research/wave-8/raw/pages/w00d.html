Seal rock to tuck (Upward dog to child pose)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start in an Upward dog position, pushing your shoulders down and lifting your chest up.
  2. Bend your knees and push on your hands to lift your hips up and back into Child pose .
  3. Keep rocking between the two positions.
