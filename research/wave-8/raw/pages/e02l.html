Rings l-sit

  • strength

Technique

  1. Get into a Rings support with straight arms.
  2. Engage your Abs and lift your straight legs to horizontal.
  3. Hold the L-sit position while keeping your shoulders depressed.

Form tips

  1. Squeeze the rings into your body to minimize shaking.
