Standing pike stand push (Press toe raises)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Start on your hands and feet in a Pike position , butt elevated.
  2. Lean forward to press on the hands, elevating the heels
  3. Return to the starting position with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips stacked as high as possible over your shoulders.
