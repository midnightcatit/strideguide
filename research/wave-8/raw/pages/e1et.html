Cable shoulder external rotation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor

Technique

  1. Stand perpendicular to a Cable machine with the pulley set at elbow height, holding the handle with the outside hand.
  2. Keep your elbow bent at 90 degrees and tucked close to your torso.
  3. Pull the cable outward away from your body by rotating your forearm outward, then return slowly.

Form tips

  1. Keep the working elbow pinned against your side during the entire motion; you can place a rolled-up towel between your elbow and your torso for feedback.
  2. Focus on isolating the shoulder external rotators without twisting your torso.
