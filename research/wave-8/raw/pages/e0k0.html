Flying butterfly (Farfallina)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit down on the ground (or against a wall).
  2. Put your feet together and pull them close to your butt, letting your knees go out and down.
  3. Flap your wings (legs) like you are a butterfly about to take flight.
