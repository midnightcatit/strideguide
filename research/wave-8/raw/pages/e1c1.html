Leg press (45-degree leg press, Incline leg press, Machine leg press, Plate-loaded leg press)

Technique

  1. Sit on the 45-degree leg press with your back flat against the pad and feet shoulder-width apart on the platform.
  2. Release the safety levers and lower the sled toward your torso until your knees reach approximately a 90-degree bend.
  3. Drive through the soles of your feet to press the weight back up to full extension.

Form tips

  1. Avoid locking your knees forcefully at the top of the extension.
  2. Maintain a stable, braced core with your hips grounded on the seat.
  3. Ensure equal pressure across both feet without letting the knees cave inward.
  4. Keep the whole foot in contact, do not elevate the heels.
