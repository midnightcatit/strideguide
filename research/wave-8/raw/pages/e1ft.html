Pigeon cobra (Cobra with knee forward, Upright pigeon backbend)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start on the floor with one knee bent forward as in a pigeon pose and the back leg straight behind you, hands under your shoulders.
  2. Press into your hands to straighten your arms and lift your chest up and back into a cobra.
  3. Lower and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders away from your ears and sink your hips toward the mat as you lift through the crown of your head.
