Lat pulldown single arm (One-arm lat pulldown)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Sit at a lat pulldown machine with a single handle attached to the overhead cable.
  2. Reach up and grasp the handle with one arm fully extended overhead.
  3. Drive your elbow down toward your ribcage, squeezing your lat at the bottom with a light side bend, then return slowly to full extension.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders square and avoid leaning excessively to the pulling side.
  2. Focus on pulling through the elbow to target the latissimus dorsi.
