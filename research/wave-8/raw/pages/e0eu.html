High-five plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Partners face each other, plant both hands on the ground and walk the feet out until both hold a high Plank .
  2. Using opposite arms, the partners high-five by clapping hands with each other.

Form tips

  1. Both partners stay in a high Plank the whole time and keep a Hollow body .
  2. On the high-five, most of the bodyweight transfers to the supporting side.
