Reverse clamshell

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus minimus
  • Gluteus medius

Technique

  1. Lie on your side on the ground, with your hips and knees slightly bent, and place a Short resistance band around your ankles.
  2. While keeping the knees together, separate your ankles against the resistance of the band, then bring them back with control.
