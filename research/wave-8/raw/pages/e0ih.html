Crunch

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Lie on your back with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor, hands crossed on your chest or beside your head.
  2. Contract your Abs to curl your upper back off the ground.
  3. Lower back down with control.

Form tips

  1. The lower back keeps contact with the ground the whole time.
  2. Do not pull on your head or neck with your hands.

Risks

  1. High-reps flexion of the spine might cause issues over time. Use this exercise only as a first step towards more effective Abs exercises.
