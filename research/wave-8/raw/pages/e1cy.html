Weighted Roman chair back extension (Weighted 45-degree back extension, Weighted hyperextension, Kettlebell back extension on Roman chair)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Position yourself in the Roman chair with your hips resting on the pad and ankles locked under the rollers.
  2. Hold a Weight plate or dumbbell firmly against your chest.
  3. Hinge at the hips to lower your upper body forward toward the floor, flexing your spine.
  4. Contract your Erector Spinae and Glutes to raise your torso back up until your body forms a straight line.

Form tips

  1. Avoid hyperextending your lower back at the top.
  2. Hug the weight securely against your chest throughout the entire movement.
