Cable shoulder internal rotation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Subscapularis
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Stand perpendicular to a Cable machine with the pulley set at elbow height, holding the handle with the nearest hand.
  2. Keep your elbow bent at 90 degrees and close to your torso.
  3. Rotate your forearm inward across your stomach, squeeze your Rotator cuff , and slowly return to the start.

Form tips

  1. Keep your elbow tucked firmly against your side at a 90-degree angle throughout.
  2. Maintain Shoulder depression and avoid shrugging your shoulder up.
  3. Place a rolled-up towel between your elbow and your torso for feedback and correct muscle engagement.
