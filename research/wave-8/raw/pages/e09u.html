Lying shoulder dislocates

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie face down on the ground, tighten your core to lift the belly off the ground and hold a staff or band with a wide grip behind you.
  2. While keeping your arms straight, lift your arms up slowly until the staff or band is all the way overhead, then reverse the motion.
