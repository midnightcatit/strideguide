Banded knee drive (Standing psoas march)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Tibialis anterior
  • Extensor digitorum longus

Technique

  1. Stand with a Short resistance band looped around both feet.
  2. Explosively drive the working knee toward the chest.
  3. Return to the starting position with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep the torso upright and Core engaged to avoid leaning.
