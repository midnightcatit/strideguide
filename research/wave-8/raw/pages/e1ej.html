Cable overhead triceps extension

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Attach a rope to a low cable pulley, turn away from the machine, and hold the rope overhead with elbows bent.
  2. Stagger your feet for balance and lean your torso slightly forward.
  3. Extend your elbows to push the rope forward and overhead until your arms are straight, then slowly bend them to return.

Form tips

  1. Keep your upper arms close to your head and stationary throughout.
  2. Avoid arching your lower back by engaging your Abs .
