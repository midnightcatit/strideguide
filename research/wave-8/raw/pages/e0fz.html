Penguin abs (Heel touch)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Lie on the floor with your knees bent and your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Contract your Abs to lift your head, torso, and arms off the floor.
  3. Continuing to stay off the floor, bend sideways to reach your heels with your hands alternately.

Form tips

  1. Look up to not bend your neck

Similar Exercises

  • Russian twist
  • Lying flutter kicks
  • Lying scissors
  • Lying alternating leg raise

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
