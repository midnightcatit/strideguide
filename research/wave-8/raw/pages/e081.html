Plank squat thrust

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Psoas major
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank with your hands under your shoulders.
  2. Jump both feet forward toward your hands into a low squat, hands staying on the ground.
  3. Immediately jump the feet back to the Plank .
  4. Repeat in a steady rhythm.

Form tips

  1. Land softly on the balls of your feet with bent knees.

Risks

  1. Letting the hips sag in the plank phase stresses the lower back.
  2. Hard landings stress the ankles and knees.
