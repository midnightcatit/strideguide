Block-assisted sitting pike fold on heel (Block-assisted Head-to-Knee Forward Bend, Elevated Janu Sirsasana B)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. From a seated position with straight legs, bend the right knee and bring the right foot to the left thigh, making a L shape with your femur bones.
  2. Draw the right heel to the right groin.
  3. Lift the hips up and place a block under your sitbones. Lengthen the spine and fold forward over the inside of the left (straight) leg. You should feel a stretch on the side of the bent leg. Grab the sides of the foot or the left wrist with the right hand.

Form tips

  1. Don't close the angle between your legs as you sit on the heel. Aim for 85°.
  2. The foot is in the same position as One-leg forward bend , with toes pointing forward.
