Front snap kick

  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand up and bring your guard up (elbows bent and close to your body, hands in front of your face).
  2. Stagger your legs to have your working leg at the back.
  3. Bring your leg in while bending that knee and snap that leg upward.
  4. Come back to the starting position and repeat for as many repetitions as desired, then switch to the other side.
