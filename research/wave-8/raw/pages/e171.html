Bent-legs plank (Knees up, Bear crawl hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Start on all fours with hands below the shoulders and knees below the hips, toes tucked
  2. Push down into the ground with your feet to slightly elevate the knees from the floor
  3. Hold the position for time

Form tips

  1. Maintain a flat back and engage your Abs .
