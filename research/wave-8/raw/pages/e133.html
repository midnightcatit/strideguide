Weighted bulgarian split squat

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Hold weights in your hands and place one foot behind you on an elevated surface.
  2. Squat down on the front leg until the back knee is near the ground.
  3. Drive through the front heel to push back up to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep your torso upright and your front foot flat on the floor.
