Weighted single leg ledge calf raise

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on the ball of one foot on a ledge while holding a weight.
  2. Lower your heel below the ledge level, then drive it up as high as possible.
  3. Lower with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Use a full range of motion, stretching the calf at the bottom.
  2. Do not bounce on your heel.
