Kneeling german hang

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Grab the rings, bring your arms behind you and kneel down to hang from them.
  2. Adjust by leaning deeper into the hang or holding it for longer.

Form tips

  1. Keep tension in your arms to protect your elbows: you should engaged them as if trying to bend your elbows, while keeping them almost straight.
