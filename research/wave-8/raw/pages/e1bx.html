Seated leg curl (Machine leg curl, Seated hamstring curl, Leg curl machine, Leg curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (short)
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Sit in the Seated leg curl machine with the lower padded roller resting on the back of your calves and the thigh pad secured above your knees.
  2. Hold the side handles and flex both knees to pull the pad down toward your glutes.
  3. Pause at peak contraction, then slowly extend your legs back to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep your lower back pressed firmly against the seat backrest.
  2. Avoid using momentum to swing the weight stack.
