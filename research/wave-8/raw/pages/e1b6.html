Horizontal supine plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Sit on the floor with legs on an elevated surface in front and hands behind you.
  2. Lift your hips until the body forms a straight line from heels to shoulders.
  3. Hold this face-up plank position while looking upward.

Form tips

  1. Activate your glutes and core to keep the body level.
  2. Keep the chest wide and open.
  3. Hand should be under the shoulders in the final position
