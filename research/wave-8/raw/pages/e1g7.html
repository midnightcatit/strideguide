Backstroke (Backstroke swimming, Back crawl)

Technique

  1. Lie on your back at the surface, ears in the water and eyes looking up.
  2. Reach one straight arm over your head, enter the water little finger first and pull it down to your thigh with a bent elbow.
  3. Lift the other arm out of the water at the same time, alternating arms.
  4. Flutter kick from the hips with pointed toes, knees under the surface.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips up near the surface instead of sitting in the water.
  2. Keep your head still while your shoulders roll with each stroke.
