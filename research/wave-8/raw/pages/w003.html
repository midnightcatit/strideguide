Thruster (Front squat press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus

  • Rectus femoris

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Hold weights at shoulder height with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Perform a Deep squat by sitting back and down.
  3. As you stand up explosively, use the momentum to press the weights straight overhead until your arms are fully extended.

Form tips

  1. Move in one continuous motion from the bottom of the squat to the overhead press.
