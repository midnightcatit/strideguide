Inverted straddle (Hanging straddle inversion)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. From an Inverted hang , open your straight legs wide to the sides into a Straddle position .
  2. Hold the position, focusing on active compression at the hips.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms straight and shoulders active.
