Side body crunch arms down

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Lie on your side with your legs straight, your top arm along your body and your lower forearm on the ground in front of you.
  2. Lift your legs up and crunch the side of your body to lift your upper torso off the ground.
  3. At the top position, only your hip and the side of your lower trunk should be touching the ground, as well as your lower forearm for balance.
  4. Slowly come back down and repeat for the number of repetitions.
