Mountain climber

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Serratus anterior
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank on your hands, wrists under your shoulders and body in a straight line from shoulders to heels.
  2. Drive one knee toward your chest, then switch legs quickly, as if running in place.
  3. Keep alternating at a fast pace while your hands stay in place.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips low: no butt up in the air.
  2. Keep your shoulders over your hands instead of letting them rock back as the legs move.
