Kettlebell radial deviation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Extensor carpi radialis brevis
  • Extensor carpi radialis longus
  • Flexor carpi radialis
  • Abductor pollicis longus
  • Extensor pollicis brevis
  • Extensor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Stand tall while holding a kettlebell in your hand by the front side of the handle, arm by your side, palm facing the thigh.
  2. Lift the kettlebell moving at the wrist as high as possible toward the front without bending the elbow.
  3. The bottom of the kettlebell should face forward.
