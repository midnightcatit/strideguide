Technique

  1. Stand facing the machine and reach up to grasp the handles overhead.
  2. Pull the handles down in a powerful arc toward your thighs while hinging at the hips.
  3. Return to the starting position by extending the body and repeat rhythmically.

Form tips

  1. Use your Core and hips to drive the movement, not just your arms.
  2. Maintain a fluid and continuous path.
