Box L-headstand (Box headstand, Feet-elevated Sirsasana)

  • strength
  • balance

Technique

  1. Set up a Headstand base: forearms and the crown of your head on the floor, hands clasped.
  2. Place both feet on a box behind you.
  3. Move your forearms and head closer to or further from the box until your hips stack over your shoulders, forming a 90 degree L.
  4. Hold.

Form tips

  1. Press through your forearms to keep weight off your head and neck.
  2. Keep your feet as light as possible on the box.
