Superman pushup (Flying pushup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. From a Plank position, lower yourself down until your chest lightly touches the floor.
  2. From the bottom position, push yourself up as explosively as possible.
  3. You will need enough explosiveness to get your hands and feet off the ground and quickly reach up overhead with your arms and return them to their initial position before your body falls back down.

Form tips

  1. Aim to have your whole body parallel to the floor at the top position when your are airborne.

Similar Exercises

  • Air pushup
  • Chest clap pushup
  • Feet up pushup
  • Air straddle pushup

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
