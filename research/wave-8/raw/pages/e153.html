Seated bent-over dumbbell rear delt fly

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Sit on the edge of a bench and lean your torso forward.
  2. Hold dumbbells straight down with a slight bend in your elbows.
  3. Lift the weights out to the sides in a wide arc until they are at shoulder height.

Form tips

  1. Focus on the movement of your shoulder blades.
  2. Do not swing or bend the elbows
