Assisted QL stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit down on a slightly elevated surface (such as a Yoga block or a thick mat).
  2. Open your legs straight out to the sides.
  3. Place one hand under the opposite knee, lift the other arm up and lean to that side to stretch the side of your torso.
