Glute kickback machine (Machine glute kickback, Standing glute kickback machine, Machine hip extension, Glute press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Stand in the Glute kickback machine with your chest supported on the padding.
  2. Place the back of the thigh of the working leg against the roller pad.
  3. Push the pad backward and upward by extending your hip.
  4. Return the leg slowly to the starting position under full control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your Abs engaged and maintain a neutral spine to avoid arching your lower back.
  2. Focus on driving the movement from the glute of the working leg.
