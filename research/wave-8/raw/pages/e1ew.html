Preacher bench biceps curl (Scott bench biceps curl, Supported dumbbells curl, Dumbbell preacher curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Position yourself at the Preacher bench with your upper arms resting firmly against the inclined pad, holding dumbbells.
  2. Allow your arms to extend fully down the pad to start from a dead stop.
  3. Curl the weight upward toward your shoulders by flexing your elbows, then lower back down slowly.

Form tips

  1. Keep your wrists straight and aligned with your forearms during the lift.
  2. Avoid swinging or letting your body slide off the seat as the weight increases.
