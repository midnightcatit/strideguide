Cable lateral raise (Cable side raise)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Stand sideways to a low cable pulley, holding the handle with the outside hand in front of your thighs.
  2. Keep your core engaged and knees slightly soft.
  3. Raise the arm out to the side until your hand reaches shoulder height into Shoulder abduction , then lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Lead with your elbow and keep a slight, fixed bend in the arm.
  2. Maintain Shoulder depression and avoid shrugging.
