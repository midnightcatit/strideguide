45 deg dip

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Start in a Support position.
  2. Lower down by bending your arms.
  3. While going down, incline your body forward until you reach a 45 degrees angle.
  4. Then push yourself back up to the starting position.
