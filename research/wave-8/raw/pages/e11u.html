Wide rings pullup (Bulgarian pullup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang from Rings placed wider than shoulder width apart
  2. Perform a pullup while moving your hands out to the sides.

Form tips

  1. Pull the rings outward away from each other as you ascend.
