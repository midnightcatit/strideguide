Finger pulses

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor carpi radialis
  • Flexor carpi ulnaris
  • Palmaris longus
  • Abductor pollicis longus
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Place your palms flat on a table or the floor.
  2. Lift the palms off the surface by driving the fingers down
  3. Lean forward to place more weight

Form tips

  1. Keep your fingers straight
