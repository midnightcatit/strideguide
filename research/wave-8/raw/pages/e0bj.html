Straddle l-sit

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Place your hands between your open legs in a Straddle position
  2. Push down to raise your hips and legs off the ground
  3. Hold the legs parallel to the floor.

Form tips

  1. Maintain Shoulder depression and keep your legs straight.
