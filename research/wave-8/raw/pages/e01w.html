Back lever pullout

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Start in a Back lever position.
  2. Keep your body completely straight and pull yourself up into Inverted hang
  3. Then return to starting position and repeat.
