L-sit ice-cream maker

Technique

  1. Maintain an L-sit leg position while hanging from a Pullup bar .
  2. Perform a pullup.
  3. Lean your torso back to horizontal while straightening your arms, then pull back.

Form tips

  1. Focus on the transition between the pull and the lean.
  2. Do not lower to the ground between repetitions
