Dolphin rock

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Start in a Dolphin Pose position.
  2. Lean forward so that your head dips towards a spot between your hands.
  3. Push yourself back into the starting position.
