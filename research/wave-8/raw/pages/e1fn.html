Banded sitting pike fold (Seated compression with band)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Sit with your legs straight in front of you. Loop a Long resistance band around your lower back, just above your glutes, and hook the ends over your feet.
  2. Fold forward at the hips, reaching your arms toward your feet against the pull of the band.
  3. Hold.

Form tips

  1. Compress actively: pull your belly toward your thighs and fold through your hips rather than rounding your neck.
  2. Reach your shoulders forward and up toward your ears ( Shoulder elevation ) to fold deeper.

  • Ground (hands or body)
  • Long resistance band
