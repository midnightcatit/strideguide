Back bird on hand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Base will start in a supine position with flyer standing over their hips, and base will reach up to place hands on flyer's low back and high hip area.
  2. The flyer will lean back while the base lies back down and lifts the flyer into their stack ( Joint stacking ).
  3. To exit, the flyer will lower their feet to the ground and the base can sit up.

Form tips

  1. The base will place their pointer and middle fingers and thumb going up the flyer's back and their pinky and ring fingers on the outside of the flyer's hips.
  2. When loading, the flyer should maintain a Hollow body , lean back and keep their gaze facing their feet.
