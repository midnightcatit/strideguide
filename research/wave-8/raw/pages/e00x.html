Hanging active splits

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Hang from rings or a bar with straight arms and your shoulders engaged.
  2. Open your legs as wide as possible to the sides into a middle split, using only your leg muscles, and hold briefly.
  3. Bring the legs back together, then open them front and back into a front split, one leg forward and one back.
  4. Alternate between the positions, or hold each one, with straight legs and pointed toes.

Form tips

  1. Lift the legs into the split actively instead of letting them fall: the goal is to strengthen the end range of your Splits .
