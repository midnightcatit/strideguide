Technique

  1. Make tight fists and place them on the floor shoulder-width apart, wrists straight.
  2. Do a Pushup , going down until your chest touches the floor: the fists add range of motion.

Form tips

  1. Start on a soft surface like a mat and move to harder ones as your knuckles toughen.
