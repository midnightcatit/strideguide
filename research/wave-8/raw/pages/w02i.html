Parallettes bent-arm handstand to L-sit (Shoulder stand to L-sit, Yaad hold to L-sit)

  • strength
  • balance

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in a Parallettes bent-arm handstand , arms bent to 90 degrees and shoulders hovering above the bars.
  2. Press strongly through your hands to straighten your arms while slowly lowering your hips and straight legs forward between the bars.
  3. Finish in an L-sit with locked arms.

Form tips

  1. Stay in control the whole way: no momentum, and never rest your shoulders on the bars.
