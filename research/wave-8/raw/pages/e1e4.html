Kneeling lat pulldown single arm

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Kneel on the floor in front of a high cable pulley and hold the handle with one hand overhead.
  2. Keep your torso upright and core braced.
  3. Pull the cable down by driving your elbow toward your hip, squeeze your lat, then return to full overhead extension.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a tall kneeling posture with your Abs engaged.
  2. Add a light side bend at the end for maximum lat contraction.
