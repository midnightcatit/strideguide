Decline downward dog one-leg squat

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Start in a declined Downward dog with your feet on a raised surface.
  2. Lift one leg into the air.
  3. Bend the knee of your supporting leg to lower your hips toward the floor, then push back to the start.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders pushed away from your ears and your core engaged.
  2. Control the descent to avoid jerky movements.
