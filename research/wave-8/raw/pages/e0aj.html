Side-lying shoulder external rotation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor

Technique

  1. Lie on your side holding a light Dumbbell in the top hand, elbow bent at 90 degrees and tucked against your side, forearm across your belly.
  2. Rotate your forearm up and away from your body ( Shoulder external rotation ), keeping the elbow against your side.
  3. Lower back with control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulder blade down; do not shrug.
  2. Move only at the shoulder: the elbow angle and wrist stay fixed.
