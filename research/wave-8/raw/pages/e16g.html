Foot-assisted elbow lever

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. From a kneeling position, place your hands close together on the floor with the fingers pointing to the sides, or towards your feet.
  2. Keeping the upper back domed, lean forward and down to place your elbows into your belly, below the ribcage.
  3. Continue leaning forward while reaching the feet back
  4. Lift one leg off the ground, keeping some of your weight on the other leg's toes. Aim to place most of your weight on your hands.
  5. The primary muscles work equally regardless of which leg you lift up, so it is not necessary to count the exercise as unilateral: it is enough to alternate the legs with each set.

Form tips

  1. Experiment with where your elbows need to be to create a strong fulcrum on which to balance. They may need to be closer together, or lower on the belly, to be able to balance.
  2. Gaze forward at a spot on the floor in front of you.
