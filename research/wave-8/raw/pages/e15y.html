Banded behind-the-neck pulldown (Band overhead row)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Grasp the band with a wide grip overhead, arms straight and minimal tension in the band.
  2. Widen the grip and pull down the band behind your head.
  3. Continue until the band reaches the level of your neck, then return to the start.

Form tips

  1. Do not let your head tip forward; keep your neck neutral.
