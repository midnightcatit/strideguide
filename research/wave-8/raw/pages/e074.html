Non-kip clap pullup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Perform a very explosive pullup.
  2. At the peak, release your grip and clap your hands together.
  3. Quickly re-grab the bar and lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Pull as fast and explosively as possible from a dead hang.
  2. Do not kip with your legs
