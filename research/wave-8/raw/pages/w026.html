Levitating side kick-through to reach

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance
  • cardio

Technique

  1. Perform a Levitating side kick through and put the heel of the extended leg down.
  2. Push your hips up whilst reaching backwards with the free arm ( Table top reach back ).
  3. Then go back to the start and repeat on the other side.
