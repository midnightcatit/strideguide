Chest-to-bar pullup (C2B pullup, Explosive pullup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang from a Pullup bar with an overhand grip.
  2. Pull yourself up explosively toward the bar.
  3. Continue the pull until your upper chest makes physical contact with the bar.

Form tips

  1. Pull explosively to reach the required height.
