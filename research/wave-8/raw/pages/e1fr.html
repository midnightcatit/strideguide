Tuck jump to handstand (Tuck hop to handstand, Tuck entry to handstand)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Place your hands shoulder-width apart on the floor or Parallettes and look between them.
  2. Hop your hips over your shoulders in a tuck and extend your legs straight up into a Handstand in one continuous motion.
  3. Come down with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders locked over your wrists: drive the hips up to find the stack instead of shifting the shoulders forward and back.

Equipment

  • Parallettes
  • Ground (hands or body)
