Sitting straddle compression (Straddle leg lift, Straddle leg raises)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground with your legs open wide in a Straddle position .
  2. Place your hands on the ground between your legs.
  3. Push on your hands and lift your straight legs off the ground repeatedly without letting them touch the floor between reps.

Form tips

  1. Do not lean back; you must lean forward and push on your hands to work compression strength.
