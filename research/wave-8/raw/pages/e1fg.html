Forearm stand scorpion (Pincha scorpion, Scorpion pose, Vrschikasana)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Hold a stable Forearm stand with your elbows shoulder-width apart.
  2. Lift your head slightly, open your chest and arch through your spine as you bend your knees and bring your feet toward your head.

Form tips

  1. Keep pressing through your forearms to keep your shoulders high.
  2. Engage your core and upper back to protect your lower back.
