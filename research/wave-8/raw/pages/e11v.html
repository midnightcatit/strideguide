Inverted hang pike lifts (Inverted deadlift)

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Internal oblique
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Start in an Inverted hang position.
  2. Keep your legs straight and bend at the hips to lower them into a Pike position .
  3. Lift your legs back up to the vertical inverted position and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms straight and shoulders active.
