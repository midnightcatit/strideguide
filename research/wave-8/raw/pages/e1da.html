Technique

  1. Move to the beat of the music, shifting your weight from one foot to the other.
  2. Let the movement travel through your whole body: knees, hips, torso and arms.
  3. Start with a simple step and add variations as you get comfortable.

Form tips

  1. Keep your knees soft and your shoulders relaxed.
  2. Stay in time with the music rather than rushing.
