Parallettes straddle L-sit

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Place your hands on Parallettes between you legs.
  2. Lift your straight legs into a wide Straddle position .
  3. Hold the legs horizontal and parallel to the ground for time.

Form tips

  1. Keep your legs locked straight and open them as wide as possible.
