90-90 (Modified pigeon)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground with your knees bent and lower them to one side, so that one knee is beside the other leg's foot.
  2. Get comfortable in this position and explore slightly different torso positions.

Form tips

  1. Try to keep your torso upright.
