Two-feet support l-sit (L-sit support hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground and place your hands on the sides, in front of the hips.
  2. Push on your hands to lift your butt off the ground, so that only your hands and heels touch the ground.

Form tips

  1. Push your shoulders down ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Keep both legs straights.
  3. If you think your arms are too short (which is very unlikely), try the Chair l-sit ; if you manage to perform that exercise, it is a muscle activation issue, not an arm length issue.
