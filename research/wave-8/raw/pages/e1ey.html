Preacher bench EZ bar curl (Scott bench EZ bar curl, EZ bar preacher curl, Preacher curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Sit at the Preacher bench and hold the inner angled grips of an EZ bar with an underhand grip.
  2. Rest your triceps against the pad with your arms fully extended down the slope.
  3. Flex your elbows to curl the bar upward toward your shoulders, then lower it slowly to full extension.

Form tips

  1. Use the angled grips of the EZ bar to reduce strain on your wrists and elbows.
  2. Keep your chest resting comfortably against the bench padding without leaning backward at the top.
