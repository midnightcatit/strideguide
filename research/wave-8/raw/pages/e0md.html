Double squat

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Stand toe to toe, facing your partner.
  2. Cross your arms over one another and grab your partner's hands (right hand with right hand, left hand with left hand).
  3. Lean back so the arms extend, creating a counterbalance.
  4. Squat all the way down together, then stand back up.

Form tips

  1. Keep the arms straight the whole time, pulling on them lightly.
  2. Squat in sync with your partner.
  3. Engage the Glutes !
