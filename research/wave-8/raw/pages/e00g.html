Barbell overhead press (Shoulder press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, holding the Barbell at shoulder level.
  2. Press the weight overhead until your arms are fully extended.
  3. Lower it back to shoulder level with control.
  4. Inhale while lowering, exhale while pressing.

Form tips

  1. Point your elbows more forward than to the sides.
  2. Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows.
  3. Do not lean back or arch your lower back to finish the press.
