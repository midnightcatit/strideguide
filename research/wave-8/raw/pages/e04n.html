Ice-cream maker

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Perform a standard pullup.
  2. At the top, lean your body back to horizontal while extending your arms.
  3. Pull yourself back to the vertical chin-over-bar position.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a straight body line as you lean back.
  2. Do not lower yourself between repetitions
