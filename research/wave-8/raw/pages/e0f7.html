Wobble board calf stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Place the ball of one foot on a Wobble balance board with the heel pressed down to create a stretch.
  2. Adjust your weight and the board angle until you feel a deep stretch in the calf of the working leg.

Form tips

  1. Keep the leg straight
