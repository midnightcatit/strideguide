Hip adduction machine (Seated hip adduction, Adductor machine, Machine hip adduction, Inner thigh machine)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Adductor longus
  • Adductor brevis
  • Adductor magnus
  • Gracilis

Technique

  1. Sit on the Hip adduction machine with your legs opened wide and inner thighs resting against the padded levers.
  2. Engage your Hip adductors to squeeze your legs together until the pads meet in the center.
  3. Pause briefly, then slowly allow the pads to return outward to the starting wide stretch.

Form tips

  1. Keep your back flat against the seat pad.
  2. Move smoothly without letting the weight plates slam at either end of the movement.
