Smith machine bench press

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Lie on a flat bench inside the Smith machine with eyes directly under the bar and grasp it slightly wider than shoulder width.
  2. Unhook the safety latches and lower the bar slowly to your chest.
  3. Press the bar straight up until your arms reach full extension.

Form tips

  1. Align the bench so the bar lowers to mid-chest level.
  2. Keep your feet flat on the floor and shoulder blades retracted.
