Front-Staggered Squat (Cat stance squat)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Start in the Cat Stance or slightly wider and rest your whole front foot on the ground in front of you.
  2. Slowly squat down until you're not able to descend any further.
  3. Pause and smoothly bring yourself back up.
