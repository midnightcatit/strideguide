One-arm hang (One-arm dead hang, One-arm straight-arm hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Hang from a Pullup bar or Rings using only one hand.
  2. Maintain a tight, controlled grip.
  3. Keep your shoulder engaged and prevent your body from spinning excessively.

Form tips

  1. Keep your active shoulder pulled away from your ear ( Shoulder depression ).

Similar Exercises

  • One-arm hang switches
  • One-arm scapular pull
  • Climbing towel hang
  • One arm towel hang
  • L-sit one-arm hang

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
