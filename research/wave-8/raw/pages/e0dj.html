Hanging external rotation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor

Technique

  1. Hang from the rings with the arms straight and an overhand grip (palms facing forward).
  2. Rotate your arms ( Shoulder external rotation ) so that your palms face each other or, even better, face backward.
  3. Hold the rotated position for the set time.

Form tips

  1. Keep the arms straight: the rotation comes from the shoulders, not from bending the elbows or twisting the wrists.
