Bent-knee single-leg calf raise (One-leg squat calf raise)

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus

  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg and bend at the knee as far as your flexibility allows, while keeping the chest upright.
  2. Lift your heel off the ground to get on the ball of your foot, while keeping the angle at the knee and hip.
  3. Slowly come back down to the starting position.
