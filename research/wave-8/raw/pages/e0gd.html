Jump rope basic skip

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Swing the rope forward from behind your back over your head and jump over it as it reaches your feet.
  2. Maintain a steady, repetitive pace with both feet jumping together.

Form tips

  1. Jump on the balls of your feet with a slight bend in the knees.
