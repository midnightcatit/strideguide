Assisted incline lunge (Assisted Leg Press Lunges, Assisted incline split squat)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Stand a small distance away from a raised surface, place your foot on the raised surface and hold on to the object for balance.
  2. Slowly bend your front knee to bend it fully.
  3. Pause and then press through your foot to smoothly return to the starting position.
  4. Keep your torso upright and neutral throughout the whole exercise.
