Stepper touchdown squat

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg on top of a low step.
  2. Bend the working knee to lower the opposite heel toward the ground.
  3. Touch the heel to the floor and immediately return to an upright position.

Form tips

  1. Control the descent to touch the floor delicately.
  2. Do not rest weight on the leg that descends.
  3. Keep the non working leg vertical
