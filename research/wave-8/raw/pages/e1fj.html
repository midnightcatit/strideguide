Seated side stretch with leg hold (Seated one-leg side bend)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit with one leg bent in on the floor.
  2. Lift the other leg and hold the outer edge of its foot with the opposite hand.
  3. Lean your torso sideways toward the lifted leg, opening your chest to the side.
  4. Hold, then switch sides.

Form tips

  1. Keep your spine long and lengthen through your side body rather than pulling the leg close.
