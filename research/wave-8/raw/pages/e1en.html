Cable straight leg adduction (Cable hip adduction)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Adductor longus
  • Adductor brevis
  • Adductor magnus
  • Gracilis

Technique

  1. Attach an ankle cuff to a low cable pulley and stand sideways with the working leg closest to the anchor point.
  2. Step away from the anchor to put tension on the cable.
  3. Stand tall while holding onto a support for balance.
  4. Pull the working leg across the front of your body into Hip adduction , then slowly return to the starting stretch.

Form tips

  1. Keep the standing leg slightly bent and your torso square to the front.
  2. Keep the working leg straight as it crosses in front of your standing leg.
