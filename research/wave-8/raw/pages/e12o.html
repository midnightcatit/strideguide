Pelican curl eccentric (Negative pelican curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Start with an Incline rings pushup , go past the rings and continue lowering yourself down with control, slowly extending your arms until they get fully straight.

Risks

  1. Eccentric training can quickly push your body to its limits; start with a very small volume (1-2 reps at a time) and progress cautiously.
