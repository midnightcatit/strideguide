Wall planche lean

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Place your hands on the floor and your feet against a wall.
  2. Lean forward so your shoulders move past your wrists.
  3. Maintain a straight body line from shoulders to heels.

Form tips

  1. Maintain straight arms and Shoulder protraction .
  2. Press your feet firmly into the wall.
