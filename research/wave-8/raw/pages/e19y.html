Weighted slanted quarter pistol squat (Weighted Poliquin step)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg on a Slant board holding a weight between your hands.
  2. Perform a quarter-depth squat.
  3. Return to a full standing position by pushing off the standing foot.

Form tips

  1. Keep the weight close to your chest.
  2. Maintain a steady gaze forward for balance.
