Tuck hanging clutch flag (Clutch hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Stand by a sturdy vertical beam, place the closest hand against the beam at the level of your hips and wrap the other arm around the beam such that your forearm is pressing against it.
  2. Support the side of your body with your lower elbow and pull with your upper arm to lift yourself off the ground.
  3. In this intermediate step towards the Tuck Clutch Flag , your body is hanging at an angle and your legs are tucked to your chest.
