Back extension (Hyperextension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Lie face down on an elevated surface (such as a Plyo Box ), place your legs on the box and the crest of your hips on the edge of the box.
  2. Let your chest hang down from the box. Secure your feet under a sturdy support or with the help of a partner. This is the starting position.
  3. Lift your chest up until it is at least as high as your hips.
  4. Come back to the starting position with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. You can keep your arms close to your body (easier) or extend them overhead (more difficult) or even hold small weights in your hands.
  2. If this is too intense for your lower back, engage your Core to limit the arch in your lower back, or don't raise your chest as high.
