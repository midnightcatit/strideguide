Lateral leg raise (Side leg raise, Standing hip abduction)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand up, shift your weight to one leg and lift the other leg straight up to the side.
  2. Get your foot as high as your current flexibility allows.
  3. Lower it back down with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your whole body in one plane of motion.
