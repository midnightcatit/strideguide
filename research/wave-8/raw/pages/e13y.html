One-leg hinge (Warrior 3 dynamic, One-leg romanian dealift)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg with a slight bend in the knee.
  2. Hinge forward at the hips while simultaneously lifting the free leg straight behind you.
  3. Lower your torso until it is roughly parallel with the floor, then return to the starting position by contracting the glutes and hamstrings.

Form tips

  1. Keep your back straight and avoid rounding or arching during the hinge.
  2. Keep your hips squared and facing forward.
