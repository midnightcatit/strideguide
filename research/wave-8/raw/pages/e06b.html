L-sit active hang (Hanging l-sit, L-hang, L-sit hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Start in an Active hang with straight arms and shoulders pulled down ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Lift your legs together until they are parallel to the ground.
  3. Hold, with knees straight and toes pointed if possible.

Similar Exercises

  • Tuck hang
  • Hanging leg raises
  • L-sit one-arm hang
  • Parallettes L-sit
  • Hanging knees to chest

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
