Sliding pike pull-through

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position with your feet on sliders.
  2. Hinge at the hips and pull your feet forward toward your hands.
  3. Lift your hips toward the ceiling until you are in a Pike position .

Form tips

  1. Focus on Shoulder protraction to help lift your hips.
