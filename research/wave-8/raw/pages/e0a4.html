RTO straddle-sit (Rings Straddle-sit)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Support yourself on Rings with straight arms.
  2. Lift your straight legs into a wide straddle.
  3. Simultaneously turn the rings outward to have the palms facing forward and hold.

Form tips

  1. Keep the legs straight and Shoulder depression
