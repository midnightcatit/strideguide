Behind-the-neck lat pulldown

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Sit at the lat pulldown machine and grasp the wide bar with an overhand grip.
  2. Pull the bar down smoothly behind your head toward the base of your neck.
  3. Pause briefly, then slowly extend your arms back overhead.

Form tips

  1. Keep your neck neutral and avoid craning your head forward.
  2. Pull only as low as your shoulder flexibility comfortably allows.

Risks

  1. This exercise places the shoulder joints in extreme external rotation and abduction; perform with caution or lighter weights to avoid impingement.
