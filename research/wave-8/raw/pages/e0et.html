Wheelbarrow pushup with squat

Technique

  1. Partners set up in the Wheelbarrow squat position.
  2. The "wheelbarrow" performs a Pushup while the "gardener" performs a squat at the same time.

Form tips

  1. Both partners maintain a Hollow body .
  2. The gardener keeps the arms straight throughout.
