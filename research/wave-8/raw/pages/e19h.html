Block-assisted Lotus L-sit

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. From a seated position with legs in front of you, place blocks on each side of the hips.
  2. Fold your legs into Lotus , right leg first.
  3. Place the hands on the blocks and lift up.

Form tips

  1. Aim to keep the legs parallel to the floor.
