Cable trunk rotation (Cable torso rotation)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Stand perpendicular to a Cable machine with the pulley set at chest height, holding the handle with both hands.
  2. Step away to create tension and extend your arms straight out in front of your chest.
  3. Rotate your torso away from the machine in a controlled arc, pause at full rotation, and return under control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms straight or slightly bent, using your Obliques to drive the rotation rather than pulling with your arms.
  2. Keep your hips stable and square to the front.
