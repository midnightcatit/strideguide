Weighted wrist extension

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Extensor carpi radialis brevis
  • Extensor carpi radialis longus
  • Extensor carpi ulnaris
  • Extensor digitorum
  • Extensor pollicis longus
  • Extensor indicis

Technique

  1. Rest your forearm on your thigh or a bench, palm facing down, holding a weight with your hand past the edge.
  2. Let the weight bend your wrist downward ( Wrist flexion ), then raise it as high as you can by extending your wrist ( Wrist extension ).
  3. Lower with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep the forearm still on its support: only the wrist moves.
