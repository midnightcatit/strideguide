Assisted hip airplane (Assisted runners hang)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis
  • Gluteus minimus
  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Piriformis
  • Obturator externus
  • Obturator internus
  • Gemellus inferior
  • Gemellus superior
  • Quadratus femoris

Technique

  1. Hold a support and balance on one leg, hinging forward until your torso is horizontal, the other leg straight backward (as in Back scale position).
  2. Rotate your hips toward the floor (internal rotation).
  3. Rotate your hips away from the floor toward the ceiling (external rotation).

Form tips

  1. Keep the standing leg slightly bent and the torso aligned with the back leg.
  2. Use a stable support to maintain balance.
