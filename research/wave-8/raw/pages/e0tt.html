Neck lateral flexion (Maybe-maybe)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Splenius
  • Upper trapezius
  • Rectus capitis lateralis

Technique

  1. Bend your head to one side, as if your were trying to touch your ear with your shoulder (but without elevating the shoulder).
  2. Repeat on the other side.
  3. Do this motion in a slow and controlled manner.
