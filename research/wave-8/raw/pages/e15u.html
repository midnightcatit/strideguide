Decline downward dog squat

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Place your feet on a raised surface and hands on the floor in a declined Downward dog .
  2. Bend your knees, bringing them toward the floor and glutes toward the heels.
  3. Push through your hands and feet to return to the start with straight legs.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips high throughout the exercise.
  2. Keep your shoulders pushed away from your ears.
