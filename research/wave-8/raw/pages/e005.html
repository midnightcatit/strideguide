Jumping wide squat (Jumping sumo squat)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius

Technique

  1. Take a wide stance.
  2. Jump and land back in the wide stance.
