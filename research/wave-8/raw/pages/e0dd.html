Standing rear delt stretch (Shoulder crossbody stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand tall and bring one arm across your body at shoulder height.
  2. Use your opposite hand to gently grab the back of the elbow and pull it towards the opposite shoulder.
  3. You should feel the stretch in the back of the shoulder.
