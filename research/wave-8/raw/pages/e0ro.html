Arms up pushup (Explosive pushup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Perform a standard Pushup , with the difference that from the bottom position, you push yourself up as explosively as possible.
  2. You will need enough speed to get your hands off the ground, lift the arms up quickly and return them to their initial position before your body falls back down.

Similar Exercises

  • Chest clap pushup
  • Air straddle pushup
  • Feet up pushup
  • Clap pushup

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
