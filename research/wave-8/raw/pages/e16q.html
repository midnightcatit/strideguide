Standing cat cow

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and hands resting on your slightly bent knees.
  2. Inhale as you arch your back and look upward.
  3. Exhale as you round your spine and tuck your chin toward your chest.

Form tips

  1. Focus on moving one segment of your spine at a time.
