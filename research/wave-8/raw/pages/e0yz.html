Knee to wall

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Place one foot at a short distance from the wall (try to keep track of that distance to check improvement over time).
  2. While keeping that foot flat on the ground, push your knee forward, past your toes until it touches the wall.

Form tips

  1. Keep your toes and knees aligned and facing forward.
