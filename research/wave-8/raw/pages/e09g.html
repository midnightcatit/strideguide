Staff radial deviation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Extensor carpi radialis brevis
  • Extensor carpi radialis longus
  • Flexor carpi radialis
  • Abductor pollicis longus
  • Extensor pollicis brevis
  • Extensor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Stand straight, and hold a staff horizontally with one hand, with most of its weight in front of you.
  2. Keeping your arm straight and hanging by your side, bend your wrist to lift the staff as high as possible.
