Bent-arm hang (Top of pullup hold, Chin-up hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hold the top position of a Pullup .
  2. Aim to keep yourself as high as you can to work on the top portion of the exercise.

Similar Exercises

  • Tuck hang
  • Pullup eccentric
  • Band-assisted pullup
  • Band-assisted rings pullup
  • 90 degrees hang hold

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
