90-90 to hurdle

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start in a 90-90 position on the floor.
  2. Lift your front leg and straighten it into a Hurdler stretch position.
  3. Lower your chest toward your knee to deepen the stretch
  4. Return to the 90/90 position and switch to the other side.

Form tips

  1. Maintain control in your hips during the rotation.
  2. Keep your spine long and tall.
