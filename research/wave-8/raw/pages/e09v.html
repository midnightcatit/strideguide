Elbow dislocates

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Hold a short straight object in front of you, with the palms facing up.
  2. Bend your arms and rotate them inward, one at a time, and then extend them in this internally-rotated position.
  3. Reverse the motion and make sure to alternate each side going in first.
