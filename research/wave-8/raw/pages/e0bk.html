Straddle one-arm pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Start in a One-arm plank position, with your body facing the ground and open your legs wide into a Straddle position . The wider you open your legs, the easier the exercise will be.
  2. Bend your arm to lower yourself down with control until your chest almost touches the ground.
  3. Then push yourself back to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Avoid twisting your body to the side: aim to keep it facing the ground the whole time.
