Side plank leg raise

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Get into a Side plank on a straight arm, hand under your shoulder, feet stacked, and reach your top arm to the ceiling.
  2. Lift your top leg straight up as high as you can.
  3. Lower it back down onto the bottom leg with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Do not let your shoulder shrug: push the floor away from you.
  2. Keep your hips up and in line with your body; do not let them sag or pike as the leg rises.
