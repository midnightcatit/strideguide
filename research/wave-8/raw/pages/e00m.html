One-arm bent-arm hang (Top of OAC hold, One-arm lock-off)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hold the top position of a One-arm-chinup : hanging from a support with one arm, the elbow fully flexed, your chin above the bar.

  • One-arm chinup with 2 fingers support
  • One-arm chinup eccentric
  • Assisted one-arm-chinup
  • Counterweight partial one-arm chinup

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
