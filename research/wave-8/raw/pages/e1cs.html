Neutral-grip machine shoulder press (Close-grip machine shoulder press, Neutral grip shoulder press machine)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Adjust the seat height so the neutral handles (palms facing each other) are at shoulder level.
  2. Sit tall, grip the handles firmly, and press upward until your arms are extended overhead.
  3. Lower the handles back down under control until your hands return to shoulder height.

Form tips

  1. Keep your lower back flat against the backrest; do not arch your spine to press.
  2. Drive the movement with your shoulders without shrugging.
