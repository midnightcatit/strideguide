Cable hip extension (Cable glute kick, Cable glute kickback)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Attach an ankle cuff to a low cable pulley and stand facing the machine, holding the frame for balance.
  2. Keep your standing leg slightly bent and your working leg straight.
  3. Drive the working leg backward into Hip extension and a slight external rotation against the cable resistance, pause, and lower forward under control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips square and avoid arching your lower back at full extension.
  2. Squeeze your glute at the peak of the movement.
