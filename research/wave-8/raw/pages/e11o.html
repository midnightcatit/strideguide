Goblet cyclist squat

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Stand on a Slant board with your heels up and toes down, feet hip-width apart.
  2. Hold a weight close to your chest.
  3. Squat down with your back straight until your thighs touch your calves.
  4. Push through your legs to stand back up.

Form tips

  1. Let your knees travel far forward past your toes: that is the point of the slant board.
