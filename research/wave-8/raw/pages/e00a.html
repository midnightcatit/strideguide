Jumping tap foot crossing front

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Jump alternately on one leg and the other.
  2. Touch the opposite hand with your foot when your leg bends in front of you.
