Standing front split

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg and lift the other straight leg backward as high as possible
  2. Lean your torso forward and place your hands on the ground for support and balance
  3. Hold the position for time

Form tips

  1. Keep the standing leg as straight as possible.
  2. Keep the hips square, do not rotate toward one side

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
