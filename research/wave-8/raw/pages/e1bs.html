Pec deck (Chest fly machine, Machine chest fly, Pec deck fly, Pec fly machine, Butterfly)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Sit on the Pec deck machine with your back against the pad and your forearms on the vertical pads.
  2. Contract your Chest muscles to bring the pads together in front of your chest.
  3. Pause at the center squeeze, then slowly open your arms out to the sides to feel a full chest stretch.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a slight, fixed bend in your elbows throughout the arc. Use the handles if present.
  2. Keep your chest lifted and shoulders pulled down and back ( Shoulder retraction ).
