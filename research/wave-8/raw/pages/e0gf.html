Jump rope front-to-back

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Begin jumping rope with a basic skip.
  2. With each revolution of the rope, hop both feet together slightly forward and then slightly backward.
  3. Keep your torso upright and your jumps consistent.

Form tips

  1. Maintain consistent timing between your hops and the rope swing.
