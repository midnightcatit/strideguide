Incline shoulder row

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang under a bar or rings in an incline position, feet on the ground, body straight and arms straight.
  2. Keeping the arms straight, pull your shoulder blades together ( Shoulder retraction ) to lift your chest slightly.
  3. Slowly lower back down, keeping tension in your upper back.
  4. Make it harder with a more horizontal body, easier with a more vertical one.

Form tips

  1. Initiate with the shoulder blades only, not the feet, hips or arms.
  2. Keep the elbows locked.
  3. The range of motion is small: go slowly.
