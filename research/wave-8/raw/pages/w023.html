Arch body to Lalanne pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Lay down on the floor face down and arch your body up while keeping the belly on the floor ( Arch body hold ).
  2. Place your toes and fingertips on the floor and push up in the Fingertip lalanne pushup .

Form tips

  1. There should not be any compression on the lower back when going up into the Fingertip lalanne pushup , all strength for going upwards should come from the front of the body.
