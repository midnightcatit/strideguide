Support legs raises to l-sit (PB leg raises)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Start in a Support position on parallel bars with arms locked.
  2. Slowly lift your straight legs in front of you toward a horizontal position.
  3. Hold the L-sit briefly, then lower them back down with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Avoid using momentum or swinging your legs.
  2. Keep your chest open and Shoulder depression .
