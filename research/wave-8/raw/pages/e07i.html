One-arm-chinup (OAC)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang from a bar with one arm, engage your scapula back ( Shoulder retraction ) and down ( Shoulder depression ) and pull your elbow back.
  2. Lift yourself up as high as possible, until your chin is above the bar and your elbow is in full flexion.
  3. At the top, your palm is facing you (= supinated grip).
  4. Come back down with control until your arm is fully extended.
