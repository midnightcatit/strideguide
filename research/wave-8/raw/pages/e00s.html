Stability ball pike compression

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position, with your legs on the ball.
  2. Bend at the hips to bring them higher and your feet closer to your hands.
  3. Try to get to a position where the hips and placed vertically over your shoulders and the shoulders are fully opened ( Shoulder flexion backward ).
