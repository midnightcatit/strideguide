Cable woodchop low to high (Bottom-to-top woodchop, Upward woodchop)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid

Technique

  1. Stand perpendicular to a Cable machine with the pulley set to the lowest position, holding the handle with both hands by your closest hip.
  2. Bend your knees slightly and hinge at the hips toward the pulley.
  3. Explosively pull the cable diagonally upward across your body to overhead on the opposite side, then lower slowly along the same path.

Form tips

  1. Pivot on your back foot as you rotate your torso upward.
  2. Drive the diagonal movement with your Core and hips.
