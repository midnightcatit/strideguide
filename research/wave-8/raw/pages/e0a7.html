Scissor jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Jump off the ground and, while in the air, quickly split your legs forward and backward resembling a scissor motion.
  2. Land softly on your feet together and repeat the motion.

Form tips

  1. Aim for maximum height and explosive power in each jump.
