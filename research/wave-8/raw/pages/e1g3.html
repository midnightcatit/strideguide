Soccer field player (Football field player, Outfield player, Outfielder)

  • cardio

Technique

  1. Receive the ball with a soft first touch that sets up your next pass, dribble or shot.
  2. Pass with the inside of the foot, planting your standing foot beside the ball, and shoot with the laces.
  3. Dribble with small touches and change direction with the inside and outside of the foot.

Form tips

  1. Look around before the ball reaches you.
  2. Stay on the balls of your feet with your knees slightly bent.
