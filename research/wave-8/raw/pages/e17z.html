Cook bridge

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Biceps femoris (short)

Technique

  1. Lie on your back and pull one knee firmly against your chest.
  2. Place the other foot flat on the ground near your buttocks.
  3. Push through the heel to lift your hips as high as possible without arching the back.

Form tips

  1. Squeeze your knee tightly to your chest to lock the lumbar spine.
  2. Do not arch your back as you lift the pelvis.
