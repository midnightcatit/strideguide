Straps rings chest fly

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Attach your arms (around the elbows) to the rings straps.
  2. Get into a Rings plank position.
  3. Drive the rings all the way out.
  4. Try to keep your arms straight - bending is fine as a progression.
