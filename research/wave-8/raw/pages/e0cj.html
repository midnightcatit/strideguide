Tuck front lever

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Hang from your arms and tuck your legs close to your chest.
  2. Pull your upper body to a horizontal position, while keeping the arms fully straight.

Form tips

  1. The upper body should be horizontal.
  2. Keep your arms fully straight.
  3. Engage your scapulas: try to retract your shoulders, so that they end up in a neutral position.
