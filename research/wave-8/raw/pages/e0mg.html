Pyramid pushup

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Stand more than arm's length apart with the feet about hip-width, and lean in to meet hands, bearing each other's weight through the hands.
  2. Take turns bending the elbows to create a Pushup motion.
  3. Whoever is not moving stays strong and acts as a frame for the partner who is pressing.

Form tips

  1. A Clasped hand grip or an Interlocking-fingers grip is usually preferred, but do what feels best for both partners.
