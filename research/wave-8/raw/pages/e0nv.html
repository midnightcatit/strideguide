Standing rear delt fly (Wide Y back, Reverse dumbbell fly)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Posterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Stand up and bend forward, while keeping your trunk straight (no back rounding). The legs are slightly bent.
  2. Hold weights and pull them to the sides and up behind you, while keeping your arms straight.
