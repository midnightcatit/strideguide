Dancer pose (Natarajasana, Lord of the dance pose)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Stand tall on one leg, bend the other knee and catch the inside of that foot or ankle with the hand on the same side.
  2. Reach your free arm straight forward for balance.
  3. Kick the back foot firmly into your hand to lift the leg up and back, opening your chest.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips as square to the front as you can and your standing leg strong.
  2. The kick of the foot into the hand creates the bow shape: don't just pull the leg with the arm.
