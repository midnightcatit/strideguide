Chair handstand

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Find a sturdy chair with a grabbable back rest.
  2. Place one hand on chair and the other hand on top of the back rest.
  3. Press or jump into a Handstand position, while keeping the upper arm bent.
