Patrick step to reverse lunge

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Iliacus
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Start from standing on a low step with the lifted leg straight in front
  2. Perform a Patrick step by reaching one heel forward to touch the floor.
  3. Without resting the elevated foot step that same leg back into a Reverse lunge .
  4. Return to the initial upright standing position.

Form tips

  1. Try to keep the foot off the floor for every repetition
  2. Keep the torso upright.
