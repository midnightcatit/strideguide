Wrists together circles

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand tall with hands together in front of you, elbows bent, fingers interlaced.
  2. Rotate the wrists in big circles clockwise maintaining the palms and the forearms in contact, then switch direction.
