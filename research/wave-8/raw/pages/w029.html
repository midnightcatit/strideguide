Technique

  1. Start from standing with a pair of dumbbells in hand
  2. Place your hands on the floor and jump back into a Plank , performing a Deep pushup using the dumbells as parallettes.
  3. Jump your feet back in and stand up
  4. Perform a overhead press to finish the rep.

Form tips

  1. Move in one continuous motion from the bottom of the squat to the overhead press.
  2. Ensure your back stays flat during the deep pushup phase.
