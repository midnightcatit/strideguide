Wide arm plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Get into a Plank with your hands much wider than shoulder-width.
  2. Hold with your arms locked.

Form tips

  1. Keep a Hollow body position.
