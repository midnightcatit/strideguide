45-degree hip extension (Roman chair hip extension, 45-degree hyperextension, Hip-hinge hyperextension, Flat-back extension on Roman chair)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Lock your feet on the 45-degree hyperextension bench with your thighs supported on the pad.
  2. Bend forward at the waist to lower your torso toward the floor.
  3. Drive through your feet and engage your posterior chain to raise your torso until aligned with your legs.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a flat back and a braced Core .
  2. Avoid swinging or using momentum to lift your upper body.
