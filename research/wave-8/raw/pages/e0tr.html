Incline fingertip pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Get into a Plank position, with your fingertips on an elevated surface.
  2. Go all the way down until the chest touches the support.
  3. Go all the way up with arms fully extended and pushing the shoulders forward ( Shoulder protraction ).
  4. A higher support surface will make the pushup easier, and vice versa.

Form tips

  1. Keep your Abs engaged and a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Arms are close to the body - max 45° out.
  3. Keep your head neutral (do not crane forward).
