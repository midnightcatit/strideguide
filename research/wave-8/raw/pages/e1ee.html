Cable goblet squat

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Attach a rope or handle to a low cable pulley and hold it at chest height while standing facing the machine.
  2. Step back to create cable tension with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  3. Lower into a squat by bending your knees and hips, then drive through your heels to return to standing.

Form tips

  1. Keep your chest lifted and torso upright as you descend.
  2. Keep your heels flat on the floor and knees tracking over your toes.
