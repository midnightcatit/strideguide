Arch body twist (Alternating bring back)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in an Arch body hold position.
  2. Turn your upper body to one side, aim to look back at your feet from above your shoulder.
  3. Alternate between both sides.
