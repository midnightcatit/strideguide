Power clean

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Begin with a Barbell on the floor, feet shoulder-width apart, toes under the bar.
  2. Bend at the hips and knees, grip the Barbell slightly wider than shoulder-width, palms facing you.
  3. Explosively extend your hips and knees, shrug your shoulders, and pull the bar upward.
  4. Use the momentum generated by the lower body and the push of the hips to propel the bar upward.
  5. Drop into a partial squat and catch the bar at shoulder height, elbows forward.

Form tips

  1. Keep the bar close to your body during the pull to generate power and control.
  2. Use the momentum generated by the lower body to propel the bar upward.
  3. Control the movement and avoid excessive arching or rounding of the back.
