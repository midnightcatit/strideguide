Box T-handstand (Box one-leg L-handstand)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Place one foot on a box and walk your hands toward it until your hips stack over your shoulders and wrists.
  2. Extend the other leg vertically toward the ceiling, in a straight line from your hands through your shoulders, hips and top foot.
  3. Hold, then switch legs.

Form tips

  1. Open your shoulders fully and push actively into the floor.
  2. Keep the foot on the box light.
