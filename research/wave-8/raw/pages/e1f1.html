Wall diamond handstand flag (Wall baby flag, Chest-to-wall diamond handstand side shift)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Walk up into a Chest-to-wall handstand .
  2. Bend your knees and bring the soles of your feet together into a diamond shape, toes lightly on the wall.
  3. Shift your hips and bent legs smoothly to one side, then to the other, in a controlled flag tilt.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders elevated and stacked over your wrists the whole time.
  2. Let your obliques and hips guide the shift: the shoulders stay put.
