Technique

  1. To throw, grip the ball across the laces, bring it up by your ear and step toward your target with the opposite foot.
  2. Turn your hips and shoulders, release with a flick of the wrist so the ball spirals, and follow through.
  3. To catch, make a triangle with your thumbs and index fingers and watch the ball into your hands.

Form tips

  1. Keep your knees bent and your weight on the balls of your feet, ready to move.
  2. Catch with your hands, away from your body, then tuck the ball in.
