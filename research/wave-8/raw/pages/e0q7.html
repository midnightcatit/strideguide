Corkscrew squat

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus minimus
  • Gluteus medius

Technique

  1. Sit cross-legged and lean forward to put all your weight on the feet.
  2. Extend your legs while letting the feet rotate as needed on their spot; this will make your body rotate as you untwist your legs.
  3. Then twist the other way when going back down, to work both sides equally.
