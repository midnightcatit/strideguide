Banded deadlift

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Stand with feet hip-width apart and slightly turned out, stepping over an elastic band.
  2. Bend at the hips and knees and grab the band at a short distance from your feet.
  3. Straighten your back, lift your chest, extend your hips and knees, putting the band under tension.
  4. Stand tall with your arms close to your body, then lower back down, bend at the hips and at the knees.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a neutral spine throughout the movement, avoiding rounding or arching of the back.
