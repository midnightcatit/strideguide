One-leg deadlift knee crunch

  • balance
  • cardio

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg and bend at the hips to lean forward until your torso becomes parallel to the ground, with your other leg extended to the back.
  2. Come back to a vertical position and bring the back leg forward with the knee up, touching your knee lightly with the opposite elbow.
