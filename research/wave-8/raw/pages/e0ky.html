Kneeling lunge forearm down reach up

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Get into a kneeling lunge: one leg forward with the toes pointing forward and the foot flat on the ground, and the back leg's knee on the ground and the toes pointing back.
  2. Bring down to the ground the forearm which is on the side opposite to the front leg.
  3. Then drive the other hand up, trying to form a vertical line with your two arms (from the elbow on the ground to the hand up in the air).
  4. Open your chest up.
