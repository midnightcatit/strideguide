Hanging side plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Grab a ring or a bar with one hand and walk your feet to the side until your arm is perpendicular to the floor.
  2. Engage your Abs to create a straight line from head to feet.
  3. Keep the other arm close to your body.
