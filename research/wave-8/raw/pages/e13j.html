Double kettlebell front squat (Dual kettlebell front squat)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Hold two kettlebells in the front rack position at the shoulders.
  2. Perform a Deep squat while keeping the back straight and chest up.
  3. Push through the heels to return to a standing position.

Form tips

  1. Keep the elbows high to support the Kettlebell weight on the chest.
