Moon salutation A (Chandra Namaskara A)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start sitting on the shins.
  2. Inhale lift the arms up.
  3. Exhale fold into a Child pose .
  4. Inhale come to all fours.
  5. Exhale Downward dog .
  6. Inhale to all fours.
  7. Exhale cow pose.
  8. Inhale come forward to the shins, hips up.
  9. Exhale bend backwards a bit.
  10. Inhale come up.
  11. Exhale lower the hips down.
