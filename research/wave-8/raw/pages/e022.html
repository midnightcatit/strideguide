Bar archer pullup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Hang from a bar with a wide grip.
  2. Pull up on one arm while keeping the other arm straight the whole time. Aim to get your chin over the bar at the top.
  3. Slowly reverse the movement to return to the starting position.
  4. Repeat on the other side.
