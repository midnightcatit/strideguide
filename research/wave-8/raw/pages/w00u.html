One-leg hold

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Stand upright on one leg.
  2. Lift the other leg and grab the big toe with the same side hand keeping it in front of you ( Standing front leg toe hold )
  3. Pull your chest toward the elevated knee ( Standing front leg pull )
  4. Open the leg to the side ( Standing side leg toe hold )
  5. Return with the leg to the front and release the hand, keeping the leg straight in the air ( Front scale )
  6. Hold these positions before switching to the other side

Form tips

  1. Maintain a tall spine and square hips.
