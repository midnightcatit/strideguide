Ladder climber (Jacobs ladder)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Face the climber and grasp the handles or rungs.
  2. Step up and reach up simultaneously with opposite limbs.
  3. Continue the climbing motion in a smooth, continuous flow.

Form tips

  1. Keep a steady rhythm and coordinate opposite arms and legs.
