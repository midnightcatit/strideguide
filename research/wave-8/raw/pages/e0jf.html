Hanging L-scissors

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Start in an active hang with shoulders engaged ( Shoulder depression ) and arms straight.
  2. Raise your legs to about hip height to form an L-sit shape.
  3. Perform small alternating kicks with control.

Form tips

  1. Straight arms
  2. Active shoulders
  3. Legs lifted to horizontal
  4. No body swing

Similar Exercises

  • Hanging leg raises toes-to-hands
  • Hanging knees to elbows
  • Hanging knees to chest
  • L-sit dead hang
  • Hanging leg raises

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
