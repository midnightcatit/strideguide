Plate pancake (Pancake good morning, Weighted sitting straddle good morning)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit in a Pancake stretch position with legs wide.
  2. Hold a Weight plate against your chest or back.
  3. Hinge forward to lower your torso toward the ground, then return.

Form tips

  1. Focus on rotating your pelvis forward rather than rounding your back.
  2. Keep your knees locked and toes pointing up.
