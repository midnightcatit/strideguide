Supported dip shrugs (Supported support elevation depression, L-sit shoulder shrug)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Support yourself on Parallettes with your feet resting on the ground.
  2. While keeping your arms straight, let your body dip between your shoulders (go into Shoulder elevation ).
  3. Push your body back up, by pushing your shoulders down away from your ears ( Shoulder depression ).
