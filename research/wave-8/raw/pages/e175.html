Half kneeling hip opener

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Kneel on one knee with the other foot flat on the floor out to the side, knee bent ad 90 degrees.
  2. Shift your weight sideways on the bent leg, pushing your hips toward the floor.
  3. Hold the stretch, feeling it in the inner thigh of the outward leg.

Form tips

  1. Keep your torso upright and square to the front.
