Technique

  1. From standing, squat down and place your hands on the floor.
  2. Jump your feet back into a high Plank position.
  3. Jump your feet back toward your hands and stand up completely.

Form tips

  1. Keep your Core tight and hips stable during the plank.
  2. Avoid letting your back arch when feet are jumped back.
