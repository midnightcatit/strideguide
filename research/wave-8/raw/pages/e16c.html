Handstand shoulder shrugs (Handstand shoulder pushup, Overhead scapular elevation)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Enter a Handstand against a wall.
  2. Allow your shoulders to collapse toward your ears ( Shoulder elevation ).
  3. Push hard against the floor to drive your shoulders back into a strong, active position ( Shoulder depression ).

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms locked straight the entire time.
  2. Focus on the vertical travel of your entire body.
