Low double pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. The base lies face up and the flyer places their hands on the base's ankles.
  2. The base grips the flyer's ankles, so both partners hold a mirrored plank facing each other.
  3. Both partners bend the elbows at the same time to create a double Pushup .
  4. To exit, reverse the entry with control.

Form tips

  1. Both partners maintain a Hollow body .
