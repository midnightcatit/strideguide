Cable knee drive (Standing cable knee raise)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Tibialis anterior
  • Extensor digitorum longus

Technique

  1. Attach an ankle strap to a low cable pulley and face away from the machine.
  2. Stand tall with tension on the cable and your working foot extended behind you.
  3. Explosively drive your working knee forward and upward toward your chest, then slowly extend it back to the start.

Form tips

  1. Maintain an upright stance and braced Core without leaning backward.
  2. Focus on explosive hip flexor drive.
  3. Do not use swing or momentum to repeat the motion.
