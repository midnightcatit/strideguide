Cable face pull (Cable facepull, Face pull)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Attach a rope handle to a Cable machine at upper chest or eye height and grasp the ends with an overhand or neutral grip.
  2. Step back to create tension with your arms extended in front of you.
  3. Pull the rope toward your face while flaring your elbows out and back, squeezing your upper back muscles, then lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Pull the center of the rope attachment toward your forehead or nose level.
  2. Focus on Shoulder retraction and external rotation at the finish of each rep.
