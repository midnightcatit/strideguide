Hanging prone plank (Inverted plank)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Lie face down under a Dip station and grab the bars above you.
  2. Lift your body into a straight horizontal line facing the ground, toes on the floor, hanging from your straight arms reaching up behind you ( Shoulder extension backward ).
  3. Hold for the desired time.

Form tips

  1. Keep a Hollow body position and your arms completely straight.

Risks

  1. This puts significant load on the shoulders: progress gradually.

  • Rings

  • Row bar
