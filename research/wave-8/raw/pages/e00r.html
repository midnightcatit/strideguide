Pike inverted hang (Pike inversion hang, Hanging pike inversion)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang upside down with the arms straight.
  2. Keeping the legs straight, bend at the hips to bring your legs closer to your torso.
