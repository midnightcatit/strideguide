RTO archer pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Get into a RTO plank position.
  2. Bend one arm to let your body go down, while pushing the other arm straight out to the side and keeping both rings turned out the entire time.
  3. Push back up to the starting position, then repeat on the other side.
