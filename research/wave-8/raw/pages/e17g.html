One-leg cat-cow (Tiger Pose, Vyaghrasana)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Get on all fours in a Cat Cow position.
  2. As you inhale and arch your back, extend one leg straight back and up.
  3. As you exhale and round your back, pull that same knee in toward your chest.

Form tips

  1. Coordinate the leg movement with the curve of your spine.
