Smith machine incline bench press (Smith machine incline press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Set an incline bench inside the Smith machine , lie down, and grasp the bar with a medium overhand grip.
  2. Unhook the bar and lower it with control toward your upper chest.
  3. Press the bar upward until your arms are fully extended without locking out aggressively.

Form tips

  1. Position the incline bench so the bar lowers to upper chest height.
  2. Retract your shoulder blades against the backrest ( Shoulder retraction ).
