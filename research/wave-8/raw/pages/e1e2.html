Cable bar triceps pushdown (Straight bar triceps pushdown)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Stand facing a high cable pulley attached to a straight or angled bar, grasping it with an overhand grip at chest height.
  2. Tuck your elbows into your sides and lean slightly forward.
  3. Press the bar downward by extending your arms fully toward your thighs, then slowly allow it to rise back to 90 degrees.

Form tips

  1. Keep your upper arms stationary against your sides throughout the set.
  2. Lock out your elbows at the bottom to maximize Triceps contraction.
