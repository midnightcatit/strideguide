Assisted front neck bridge side-to-side

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Splenius
  • Upper trapezius
  • Rectus capitis lateralis

Technique

  1. Get on your hands and feet and lower your head to a place on the ground between your hands.
  2. Slowly pivot to one side and the other side.

Form tips

  1. To alleviate discomfort to your head, perform the exercise on a soft surface or use a soft support like a towel.

Risks

  1. This exercise places significant strain on the neck. Make sure to warm up your neck properly and progress carefully.

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
