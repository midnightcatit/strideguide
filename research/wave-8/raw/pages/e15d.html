Weighted side crunch (Dumbbell standing oblique crunch)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Stand with feet hip-width apart, a Dumbbell or Kettlebell hanging in one hand.
  2. Place the other hand on your hip or behind your head.
  3. Bend sideways toward the weight, lowering it toward your knee.
  4. Contract the opposite side to lift the weight back up, past upright.
  5. Do all reps, then switch sides.

Form tips

  1. Bend only sideways: don't lean forward or back or turn your hips.
  2. Don't shrug the weighted shoulder.
