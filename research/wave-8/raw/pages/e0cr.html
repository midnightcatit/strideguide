Tuck planche pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Start from a Tuck planche position.
  2. Bend your arms to perform a pushup, while keeping the back horizontal.
  3. If using the ground, your range of motion will be limited by the knees hitting the ground; you can use parallettes to be able to go lower and make the movement harder.

Equipment

  • Dip station
  • Parallel bars
  • Parallettes
  • Ground (hands or body)
