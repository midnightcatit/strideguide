V-sit speed push

  • strength

Technique

  1. Start from a L-sit position with your hands by your hips on the Parallettes , legs straight.
  2. Kick your legs up into a V-sit
  3. Control the descent and do not rest your feet between repetitions

Form tips

  1. Focus on explosive compression from the Abs and hip flexors.
