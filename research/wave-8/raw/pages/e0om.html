Side plank pulls

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Stand sideways to stall bars or a flag pole, grip it with your top hand high and your bottom hand low, arms straight.
  2. Lean your body out sideways in a straight line, with your feet together on the ground.
  3. Pull with the top arm and push with the bottom arm.
  4. Alternately exaggerate the scapula depression ( Shoulder depression ) of the pulling arm, then let it relax and elevate.

Form tips

  1. This teaches your body to depress the scapula properly during Human Flag training.
  2. A slight bend in the pulling arm is fine if it helps you depress the scapula; over time, aim to keep that arm straight.
  3. Abs should be tight; avoid a lower back arch.
