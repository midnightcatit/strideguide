Hands-release fingertip pushup (Explosive fingertip pushup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Perform a Fingertip pushup , and push explosively on your way back up, to allow your fingertips to get off the ground at the top.

Form tips

  1. Keep your Abs engaged and a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Arms are close to the body - max 45° out.
  3. Go all the way down until you almost touch the ground.
  4. Keep your head neutral (do not crane forward).
  5. Distribute the weight evenly on your hands.

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
