Standing pike on the ground (Standing pike stand, Standing pike stretch hands flat, Standing Forward Bend, Uttanasana, Standing forward fold)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Separate the feet about hip width, hinge at the hips to reach the hands to the floor near the pinky toes.
  2. Lower the head toward the legs.
