Kneeling woodchopper (Kettlebell kneeling chop and lift)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid

Technique

  1. Start in a half-kneeling position holding a weight at your bottom hip.
  2. Pull the weight diagonally across your chest and up toward the opposite shoulder.
  3. Return the weight slowly along the same diagonal path.

Form tips

  1. Focus on rotating from your trunk.
