Machine chest press (Seated chest press, Chest press machine, Seated machine chest press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Adjust the seat height of the Chest press machine so the handles align with the middle of your Chest .
  2. Sit tall, grasp the handles with an overhand grip, and press forward until your arms are fully extended.
  3. Slowly lower the handles back toward your chest until your hands return to chest level.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulder blades retracted and pressed against the backrest ( Shoulder retraction ).
  2. Plant your feet flat on the floor for a stable pressing base.
