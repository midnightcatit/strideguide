High double pushup

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Person one starts in a high Plank .
  2. Person two grips person one's ankles and hooks their feet over the tops of person one's shoulders, lying on top and facing person one's feet.
  3. Both partners perform a Pushup together, bending the arms to 90 degrees and extending back to a high plank.

Form tips

  1. Both partners keep the Core engaged.

Risks

  1. High stability requirement; if one person fails, both may fall.
