Seated cable row with forward lean (Seated cable row with hip hinge, Seated row with torso lean, Stretch seated row, Dynamic seated cable row)

Technique

  1. Sit at a cable row machine with feet on the footplates, knees slightly bent, grasping the handle.
  2. Lean slightly forward at the hips to let the cable pull your arms into a full stretch.
  3. Hinge back to an upright torso while pulling the handle to your abdomen, driving your elbows back and retracting your shoulder blades ( Shoulder retraction ).

Form tips

  1. Use the forward lean to stretch the lats at the start, then pull to a tall, vertical chest.
  2. Avoid excessive swinging or jerking with the lower back.
