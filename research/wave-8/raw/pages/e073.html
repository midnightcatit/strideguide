No false grip rings muscle-up

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Start by hanging from the rings with a standard grip.
  2. Pull yourself up and at the end of the Pullup , switch to a False grip placement.
  3. Transition to go over the rings to complete the Rings muscle-up .
  4. Go back to the starting position with control.
