Quarter pistol squat touchdown (Patrick step up)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Iliacus
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg on a step or yoga blocks.
  2. Bend the working knee to lower the free leg's heel toward the floor.
  3. Lightly touch the ground and immediately push back to a standing position.

Form tips

  1. Control the descent to barely graze the floor with your heel.
  2. Do not shift weight onto the descending leg.
