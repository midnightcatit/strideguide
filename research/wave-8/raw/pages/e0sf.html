Uneven pushup

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Place your hands on the floor shoulderwidth apart, directly below your shoulders, with one hand on a raised platform.
  2. Slowly lower yourself down past the raised platform and go as low as your flexibility allows.
  3. Pause and then smoothly raise yourself back to the starting position.
  4. Using an unstable platform, like a ball, makes the exercise harder.

Form tips

  1. Make sure to distribute the load evenly between your hands.
