Plank fingers back

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Assume a Plank position with straight arms.
  2. Rotate your hands 180 degrees so that your fingers point back toward your feet.
  3. Hold this isometric position with your body in a straight line.

Form tips

  1. Do not let your back arch; maintain a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Push your shoulders away from your ears.

Similar Exercises

  • Bottom pushup hold
  • Plank
  • Bent-legs plank
  • Tuck plank
  • Pushup

Equipment

  • Parallettes
  • Ground (hands or body)
