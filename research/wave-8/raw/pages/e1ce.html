Single-leg seated leg press calf raise (One-leg seated calf press, Single-leg horizontal calf press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Sit in the Seated leg press machine and place the ball of one foot on the bottom edge of the sled, leaving the other foot off.
  2. Extend the working leg and release the safety handles.
  3. Press through the ball of the foot to extend your ankle ( Ankle plantarflexion ), pushing the sled away.
  4. Flex the ankle slowly to lower the sled back down into a full calf stretch.

Form tips

  1. Keep the working knee nearly straight with a slight safety bend; do not lock it out hyper-extended.
  2. Move deliberately to prevent your foot from slipping off the footplate edge.
