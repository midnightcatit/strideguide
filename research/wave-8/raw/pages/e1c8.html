Single-leg bent-knee leg press calf raise (One-leg bent-knee calf press, Single-leg soleus calf press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Position the ball of one foot on the bottom edge of the leg press footplate and bend the knee around 90 degrees.
  2. Maintain that fixed knee angle while flexing the ankle to allow the sled to move toward you.
  3. Push through the ball of your foot to extend the ankle, driving the sled away.

Form tips

  1. Keep a fixed bend in the working knee throughout the entire set to target the Soleus .
