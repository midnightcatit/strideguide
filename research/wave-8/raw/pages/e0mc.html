Wheelbarrow knee to elbow

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. The "wheelbarrow" starts on all fours while the "gardener" stands behind them.
  2. The wheelbarrow lifts one foot at a time into the gardener's hands.
  3. The wheelbarrow brings one knee to the elbow on the same side or on the opposite side (your choice!).
  4. To exit, the gardener lowers the wheelbarrow's feet to the ground, one side at a time.

Form tips

  1. When lifting and lowering the wheelbarrow's ankles, the gardener squats rather than bending the back.
  2. Both partners maintain a Hollow body .
  3. The wheelbarrow is essentially in a Plank position, and may find themselves leaning slightly over the wrists.
