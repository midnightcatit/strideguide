Weighted quarter pistol squat touchdown (Weighted Patrick step up)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Iliacus
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg on a low step while holding a weight.
  2. Bend the knee to touch the floor with your other heel.
  3. Return to a full standing position.

Form tips

  1. Keep your torso as vertical as possible.
  2. Control the movement so you don't "drop" onto your heel.
