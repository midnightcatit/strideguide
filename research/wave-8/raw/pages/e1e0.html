Smith machine squat

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Step under the Smith machine bar, resting it across your upper back, and unhook the safety latches.
  2. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart and lower into a squat by bending your knees and hips.
  3. Descend until your thighs reach at least parallel to the floor, then press back up to full extension.

Form tips

  1. Position your feet slightly in front of the bar line to maintain a natural squat path.
  2. Drive through your heels and keep your chest lifted.

Risks

  1. The fixed vertical line of motion requires careful foot placement to avoid knee or lower back stress.
