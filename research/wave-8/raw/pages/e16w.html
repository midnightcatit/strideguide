Kneeling lunge side bend (Low lunge with side bend)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start in a Kneeling lunge arms up position
  2. Bend your torso sideways toward the front leg side to stretch the flank.

Form tips

  1. Avoid rotating your torso while bending sideways.
  2. Keep your hips pushed downward.
