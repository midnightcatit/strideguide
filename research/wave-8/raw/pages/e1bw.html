Machine lat pulldown (Lever lat pulldown, Lat pulldown machine, Plate-loaded lat pulldown)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Sit on the Lat pulldown machine seat and adjust the thigh pads to hold your lower body firmly in place.
  2. Reach up to grasp the handles with an overhand grip wider than shoulder width.
  3. Drive your elbows down and back to pull the handles to upper chest level, squeezing your shoulder blades together.
  4. Slowly raise the handles back up until your arms are fully extended.

Form tips

  1. Maintain Shoulder depression and keep your chest lifted throughout the movement.
  2. Avoid leaning back excessively to pull the handles.
