Kneeling lunge lifts (Kneeling lunge pulses, Low lunge back leg extension, Low lunge knee lifts)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Start in a low lunge with your front knee over your ankle and your back knee on the mat. Keep your torso upright, hands on your hips or front thigh.
  2. Tuck your back toes under and press through the back foot to straighten the back leg, lifting the knee off the floor.
  3. Lower the knee back down to lightly tap the mat, then repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips low and level the whole time: don't push them up as the leg straightens.
  2. Feel the stretch in the front of the back hip ( Hip extension ).
  3. Over time, walk the front foot further forward to deepen the lunge.
