Supine chin tuck hold (Supine neck flexion hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Longus capitis
  • Rectus capitis anterior
  • Longus colli

Technique

  1. Lie on your back on the ground or on a bench.
  2. Lift your head up slightly while tucking your chin in.
  3. Hold that position for time, then bring your head back down with control.

  • Front neck bridge front and back
  • Neck flexion from deep extension
  • Headstand

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
