Technique

  1. Train with a partner on a mat: drill a technique slowly first, then spar at a controlled intensity.
  2. Work for a dominant position (guard, side control, mount, back) before attempting a submission.
  3. Use your hips and legs to control the distance: frame, shrimp and bridge rather than pushing with your arms.

Form tips

  1. Stay relaxed and keep breathing. Use leverage and timing rather than strength.
  2. Keep your elbows close to your body and your chin tucked.

Risks

  1. Tap early, and release immediately when your partner taps.
  2. Apply joint locks and chokes slowly.
