Arms circles

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart and make large vertical circles with your straight arms.
  2. Reach as far behind as you can.
  3. Circle both forward and backward; each rotation counts as one repetition.

Form tips

  1. Avoid arching your back when the arms go behind.
