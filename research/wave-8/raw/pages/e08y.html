Rings l-sit muscle-up

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Perform a strict Muscle-up on Rings .
  2. Keep your legs in a horizontal L-sit position throughout.
  3. Finish in a support position above the rings without lowering the legs.

Form tips

  1. Requires high strength to transition while keeping legs raised.
