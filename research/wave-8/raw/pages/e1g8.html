Butterfly stroke (Butterfly swimming)

Technique

  1. Lie face down with both arms stretched forward and legs together.
  2. Pull both arms back under your body at the same time, then swing them forward together over the water.
  3. Ripple your body from the chest down and kick with both legs together, two kicks per arm stroke.
  4. Lift your chin to breathe as your arms finish the pull, and put your face back in before they come forward.

Form tips

  1. Start the wave from your chest, not from your knees.
  2. Keep your arms relaxed and low over the water as they swing forward.
