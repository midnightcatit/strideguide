Smith machine romanian deadlift single leg (Smith machine RDL single leg, Smith machine hip hinge single leg)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg in the Smith machine , holding the bar in front of your thighs, with the free leg extended behind you.
  2. Hinge at the hip of the standing leg, lowering the bar down your shin while lifting the back leg.
  3. Drive through the standing heel to extend your hip and return to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Use the fixed bar track to maintain stability while hinging on one leg.
  2. Keep your hips square to the floor.
