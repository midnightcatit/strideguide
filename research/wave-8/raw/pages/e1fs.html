Banded wall shoulder flexion (Wall slide with band, Banded straight-arm wall slide)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Stand with your back against a wall. Place a Long resistance band under your feet and hold the other end with both hands. Press your lower back, upper back and head against the wall.
  2. Raise your straight arms up along the wall against the band.
  3. Aim to touch your wrists to the wall at the top, then lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your back glued to the wall: don't arch to reach higher. The greyed part of the video shows this mistake.
