Machine row (Seated row machine, Chest-supported machine row, Lever row, Seated machine row)

  • strength

  • Latissimus dorsi

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Adjust the seat height on the Seated row machine so the chest pad aligns with your sternum.
  2. Grasp the handles with arms extended in front of you.
  3. Pull the handles toward your midsection by driving your elbows back, then return with control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your chest pressed firmly against the support pad throughout the set.
  2. Focus on pulling from your elbows and squeezing your shoulder blades together ( Shoulder retraction ).
