Smith machine military press (Smith machine standing shoulder press, Smith machine overhead press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Stand tall inside the Smith machine with the bar set at shoulder height and grasp it with an overhand grip.
  2. Unhook the bar and press it straight overhead until your arms are fully extended.
  3. Lower the bar slowly back down to upper chest height and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your lower back flat and avoid overarching your spine to press.
  2. Lower the bar with control to upper chest level.
