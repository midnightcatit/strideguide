Cable biceps curl (Cable curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Attach a straight bar to a low cable pulley and stand facing the machine holding the bar with an underhand grip.
  2. Stand tall with your shoulders pulled back and arms extended down.
  3. Curl the bar upward toward your shoulders by flexing your elbows, squeeze your Biceps , and lower slowly.

Form tips

  1. Keep your elbows pinned to your sides throughout the curl.
  2. Avoid using torso momentum to swing the bar upward.
