Neck flexion from deep extension

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Longus capitis
  • Rectus capitis anterior
  • Longus colli

Technique

  1. Lie down on a banch with your head off the edge
  2. Start with your head tilted all the way back in Neck extension .
  3. Slowly pull your chin toward your chest by engaging the front neck muscles.
  4. Return to the tilted-back position and repeat with control.

Form tips

  1. Move slowly and stop immediately if you feel pain.

Risks

  1. This places significant strain on the neck; avoid if you have prior neck injuries.
