Rope climb

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Grip the Climbing rope with both hands and pull your body upward.
  2. Reach higher with one hand at a time while maintaining a secure grip.
  3. Try to keep your legs extended parallel to the ground to keep the core engaged

Form tips

  1. Take small steps upward
  2. Control the descent
