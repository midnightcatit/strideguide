Kettlebell woodchop

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid

Technique

  1. Stand with your feet wide and hinged at the hips
  2. hold a kettlebell with both hands at one side
  3. Swing the kettlebell from one hip diagonally across your body to the opposite shoulder.
  4. Control the descent and repeat

Form tips

  1. Use your hips and legs to drive the momentum.
