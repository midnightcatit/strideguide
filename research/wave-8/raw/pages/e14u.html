Weighted ledge calf raise

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on an edge holding weights.
  2. Lower your heels as far as possible below the level of the edge.
  3. Push your heels upward as high as you can and pause.

Form tips

  1. Seek maximal stretch at the bottom.
  2. Do not bounce.
