Seated leg press (Horizontal leg press, Machine seated leg press, Seated leg press machine)

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus

Technique

  1. Sit in the machine with your back flat against the backrest and feet shoulder-width apart on the sled.
  2. Disengage the safety levers and bend your knees to lower the sled toward your chest.
  3. Press through your feet to push the platform back up to full leg extension.

Form tips

  1. Avoid locking out your knees forcefully at the peak of the press.
  2. Keep your tailbone grounded on the seat pad.
