Arm screw stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand up and lift your arms out to the sides to a horizontal position.
  2. While keeping both arms aligned, twist one arm internally and twist the other one externally on their axes.
  3. Twist them as far as your flexibility allows; you should feel a special stretch in your shoulders.
  4. Then switch and keep alternating.
