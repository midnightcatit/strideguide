Box pike shoulder shrugs (Pike stand scapular pushup, Elevated handstand shrugs)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Place your feet on a box and walk your hands close to it, into a steep pike with your hips stacked over your shoulders ( Pike stand ).
  2. With straight arms, let your shoulders sink toward your ears, then press through your hands to push the floor away ( Shoulder elevation ).

Form tips

  1. Never bend your elbows: all the motion comes from your shoulder blades.
