Kettlebell pancake (Pancake good morning)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit in a Pancake stretch position.
  2. Hold a Kettlebell in front of you or behind your neck.
  3. Slowly lean forward to deepen the stretch, using the weight to pull you further.

Form tips

  1. Focus on rotating your pelvis forward rather than rounding your back.
  2. Keep your knees locked and toes pointing up.
