Treadmill running

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Step onto the treadmill belt and set your desired speed and incline.
  2. Begin walking or running as the belt moves backward.
  3. Stay positioned in the center of the belt using a natural stride.

Form tips

  1. Look forward and avoid holding onto the side rails.
  2. Maintain a natural and relaxed running posture.
