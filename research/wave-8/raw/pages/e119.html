Tuck pullup (Chair pullup, Tuck L-sit pullup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Start in a Straight-arm hang position and bring your knees up towards your chest.
  2. Engage your scapulas and pull yourself up until your chin goes past your hands.
  3. Lower yourself back down to the starting position with control.
