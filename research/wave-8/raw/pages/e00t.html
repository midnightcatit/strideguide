Stability ball plank tuck

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position, with your legs on the Stability ball .
  2. Tuck your legs towards your chest and let the ball roll forward.
  3. Then extend them back to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Do not let the back arch; keep the Abs engaged.
