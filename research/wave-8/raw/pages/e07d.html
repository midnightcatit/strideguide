One-arm one-leg plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Start in a standard Plank position on your forearms.
  2. Lift one arm and the opposite leg simultaneously off the ground.
  3. Hold this balanced position, keeping your body in a straight line for time.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips and shoulders parallel to the ground; do not let them tilt.

  • Ground (hands or body)
