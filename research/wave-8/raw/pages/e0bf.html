Straddle front lever row

  • strength

  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Start in a Straddle front lever position, hanging from rings or from a bar.
  2. While keeping your back parallel to the ground, pull on your arms and drive your elbows back to lift yourself up as high as possible.
  3. Come back to the starting position with control.
