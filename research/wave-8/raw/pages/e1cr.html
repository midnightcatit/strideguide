Machine shoulder press (Seated machine shoulder press, Shoulder press machine, Machine overhead press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Adjust the seat height so the handles align with your upper chest and shoulders.
  2. Grasp the handles with an overhand grip (palms facing away).
  3. Press the handles overhead in a smooth path until your arms are fully extended.
  4. Lower the weight back down slowly until your hands reach shoulder height.

Form tips

  1. Maintain an upright seated posture with your feet planted flat on the floor.
  2. Avoid locking your elbows aggressively at the top.
