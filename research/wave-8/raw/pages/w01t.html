Straddle roll-back to Pancake (Reclining Angle Yoga Pose to Wide-Angle Seated Forward Bend, Supta Konasana to Upavistha Konasana)

  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. From a seated position with legs extended in front, lay down on your back.
  2. Take the legs up and over.
  3. Grab the big toes and take the legs wide, about 90 degrees.
  4. Stay for a few breaths.
  5. Inhale roll up with straight legs.
  6. Land softly on the calves and fold forward in between the legs

Form tips

  1. Keep the legs engaged in the lying position and the back as vertical as possible
  2. Keep the chin away from the chest in the lying position
  3. When rolling up, pull the big toes to slow down the movement
