Hanging plank (Horizontal hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. This is the bottom position of the Horizontal row.
  2. Arms are straight and the body forms a straight line from shoulders to feet.

Similar Exercises

  • Hanging prone plank
  • Active hang
  • Straight-arm hang
  • Incline row
  • Floor T hold

Equipment

  • Rings
  • Dip station
  • Parallel bars
  • Row bar
  • Suspension straps (TRX)
