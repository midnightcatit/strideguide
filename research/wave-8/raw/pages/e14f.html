Weighted rings dip

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Perform a Rings dip with a weight attached to your body. A Weight belt is preferred to a Weight vest because it lowers your center of mass, thus helping making the exercise more stable.

Risks

  1. Make sure to have a support to step on in case you fail to complete a repetition due to the weight.
