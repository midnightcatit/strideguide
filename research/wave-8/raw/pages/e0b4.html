Standing shoulder elevation and depression- arms by the sides (Scapular elevation and depression)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. While standing or sitting, make your shoulders go up and down, without activating the rest of your body.
  2. Go slowly and push your shoulders as far as they can go, focusing on exploring the full range of motion in Shoulder elevation and Shoulder depression .
