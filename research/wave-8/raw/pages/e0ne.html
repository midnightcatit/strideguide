Partner reverse shoulder stand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Begin in Back bird .
  2. The flyer will slowly lower their back so the tops of their shoulders meet the base's hands.
  3. The base will use a pinch grip on the flyer's shoulders to balance their flyer, who maintains a hollow body.
  4. The flyer will place their hands on the back of the base's forearms with their fingers pointing down.
  5. To exit, the base will bring their feet back to the flyer's sacrum, then return to back bird.

Form tips

  1. Both the base and flyer will maintain a Hollow body for the entire exercise.
  2. The flyer will be sure to stay straight and not hinge at the hips, while the base uses their feet to assist the flyer up.
  3. The flyer will press downward and into the base's forearms to counterbalance.
