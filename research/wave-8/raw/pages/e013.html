360 pull (360 degrees pull)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Teres minor
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. From a hanging position, lift the body into Front lever , then into Inverted hang , Back lever and German hang .
  2. Then reverse the motion.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms straight and your body straight the whole time.
