90 degrees hang hold (Bent-arm hang, Isometric half-way pull-up)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang from a pullup bar with a wide grip.
  2. Pull yourself halfway up, until your elbows are bent at around 90 degrees and hold for time.

Similar Exercises

  • Chinup
  • Half pullup
  • Neutral grip pullup
  • Pullup
  • Bent-arm hang

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
