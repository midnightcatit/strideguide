Smith machine hip thrust single leg (Smith machine elevated glute bridge single leg)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Biceps femoris (short)

Technique

  1. Set up on the floor with your back against a bench and the padded Smith machine bar across your hip crease.
  2. Lift one foot off the floor and unhook the bar using the single working leg.
  3. Drive through the working heel to extend your hips, squeezing your Glutes at the top, then lower under control.

Form tips

  1. Keep the non-working leg bent or extended off the floor.
  2. Ensure the working knee tracks straight over the foot.
