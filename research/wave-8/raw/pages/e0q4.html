Kneeling forward fold (Kneeling pike stretch, Kneeling hamstring stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit down on a bent leg and extend the other leg straight in front of you.
  2. Fold forward, aiming to get your chest to your knee.
