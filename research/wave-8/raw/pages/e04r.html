Hanging knees to chest (Hanging knee raises, Straight-arm hang to tuck straight-arm hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. No swinging: keep it slow and controlled.
  2. Lift the knees up until your thighs touch your chest.
  3. Avoid bending at the shoulders to keep the focus on the Abs .
