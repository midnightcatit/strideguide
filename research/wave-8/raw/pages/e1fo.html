Sliding front split (Slider front split, Active sliding split)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Biceps femoris (short)

Technique

  1. Kneel in a half-kneeling position on a smooth floor with a Slider under your front foot, hands on your hips.
  2. Slowly slide the front foot forward into a front split as far as you can control, torso upright.
  3. Pull the front foot back toward you with your hamstrings and hip flexors to return to the start.

Form tips

  1. Never use your hands for support.
  2. Only slide as deep as you can pull yourself back out of.
