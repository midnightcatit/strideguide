Seated calf press

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus

Technique

  1. Sit on a chair or on the ground and wrap a resistance band around one foot.
  2. Adjust the band length so that it is under tension when the leg is straight in front of you.
  3. Flex at the ankle to push the foot away, then let the band bring your foot back towards yourself.
