Banded supine leg extensions (Split prep stretch, Supine knee-to-chest leg extension)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie on your back, other leg straight on the floor. Loop a Long resistance band around the arch of one foot and hold the ends.
  2. Pull that knee tight to your chest.
  3. Keeping it there, press into the band to straighten the leg toward the ceiling, then bend it again.

Form tips

  1. The knee stays close to the chest: don't let it drift away just to straighten the leg.
