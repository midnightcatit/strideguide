Top rope climbing

  • strength

Technique

  1. Climb tied into a rope that runs up to an anchor at the top and back down to your belayer.
  2. Push up with your legs and use your arms mainly to hold on and keep your balance.
  3. At the top, sit back in your harness and let your belayer lower you.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms straight when you can and your hips close to the wall.
  2. Move one hand or foot at a time.

Risks

  1. Check your knot, your harness and your partner's belay device before every climb.
  2. Learn to belay with a qualified instructor.
