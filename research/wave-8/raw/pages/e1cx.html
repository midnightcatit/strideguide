Machine-assisted chinup (Assisted chinup machine, Machine assisted chin-up, Assisted chin-up machine, Gravitron chinup)

  • strength

Technique

  1. Adjust the machine weight stack to the desired level of assistance and kneel or stand on the platform.
  2. Grasp the overhead handles with an underhand ( Wrist supination ) grip at shoulder width.
  3. Pull your elbows down and back to lift your body until your chin clears the handles.
  4. Lower yourself back down with control until your arms are fully extended.

Form tips

  1. Maintain Shoulder depression and keep your chest open as you pull up.
  2. Avoid swinging or using momentum from your lower body.
