Tuck jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Do a partial squat (about halfway or so) and explose upward.
  2. While moving upward, bring your legs close to your chest while bending at the knees.
  3. Land softly into a partial squat and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Aim to get your feet as high as possible.
  2. Absord the landing with controlled muscle elongation, instead of stiffening your joints. A good way to think about it is to make as little noise as possible when landing.
