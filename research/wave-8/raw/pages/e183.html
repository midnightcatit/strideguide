Quadruped rock back

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start on all fours with hands under shoulders.
  2. Open your legs and lower your pelvis to the floor
  3. Push your hips back toward your heels while keeping your back flat.
  4. Return to the starting position and repeat the motion.

Form tips

  1. Focus on pushing through your hands to move your hips.
  2. Do not let your feet move inward
