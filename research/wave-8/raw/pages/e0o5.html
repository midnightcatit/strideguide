One-arm assisted false grip

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor carpi radialis
  • Flexor carpi ulnaris
  • Palmaris longus
  • Abductor pollicis longus
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Place one hand in a False grip position around the ring.
  2. Place the other hand on top of the ring.
  3. Hang from that ring, aiming to use the assistance of the top hand as little as possible.
