Wrists separated circles

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand tall with arms by your side, bent at the elbows at 90 degrees.
  2. While keeping the forearms still, rotate the wrists in big circles clockwise and counterclockwise, then switch directions.
