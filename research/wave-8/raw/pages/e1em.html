Cable straight leg abduction (Cable side leg raise, Cable hip abduction)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Attach an ankle cuff to a low cable pulley and stand sideways with the working leg furthest from the machine.
  2. Hold onto the machine frame for balance while keeping your standing leg slightly bent.
  3. Step away from the anchor to put tension on the cable.
  4. Pull the working leg straight out to the side into Hip abduction , pause, and return with control.

Form tips

  1. Avoid leaning your upper body to the opposite side to cheat the weight.
  2. Focus on isolating the Glutes and outer thigh muscles.
