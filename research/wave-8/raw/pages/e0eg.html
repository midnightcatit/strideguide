Calf raise (Calf raise off the floor)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
  2. Slowly raise your heels off the ground as high as possible, balancing on the ball of your feet and keeping the knees straight.
  3. Come down slowly.

Form tips

  1. Do not bounce on the heels, keep control of the motion.
