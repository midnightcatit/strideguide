Lat pulldown neutral grip (Parallel grip lat pulldown)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Attach a neutral-grip handle (palms facing each other) to the overhead lat pulldown cable.
  2. Sit down with thighs secured under the pads and reach up to grasp the handles.
  3. Pull the handles down to upper chest level, retracting your shoulder blades, then return to full extension under control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your chest lifted and avoid rounding your upper back.
  2. Drive your elbows straight down toward your sides.
