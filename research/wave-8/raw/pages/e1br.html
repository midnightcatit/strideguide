Machine-assisted dip (Assisted dip machine, Machine assisted dip, Gravitron dip)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Select the desired assistance weight, step onto the Assisted pull-up machine , and hold the parallel bars at hip height.
  2. Lower your body by bending your elbows until your upper arms are parallel to the floor, leaning slightly forward.
  3. Press down through the handles to extend your arms and return to the top Support position.

Form tips

  1. Straighten your arms and depress your shoulders at the top ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Control the descent and keep your elbows pointing backward rather than out to the sides.
