Inverted hang (Straight Inversion hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Hang from rings or a bar with your arms straight and shoulders engaged.
  2. Tuck your knees to your chest and roll back until your hips are above your head (see Tuck inverted hang ).
  3. Extend your legs straight up so your whole body is vertical and upside down, and hold.
  4. Come down the same way: tuck, then lower your hips until you hang again.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms straight and close to your body.
  2. Squeeze your legs together and keep your body in a straight line, with no pike at the hips.

Risks

  1. Being upside down can make you dizzy; avoid it if you have high blood pressure or eye conditions like glaucoma.
