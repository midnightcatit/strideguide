Happy baby (Anada Balasana)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie on your back and lift your legs toward the ceiling.
  2. Reach up and grab the outside edges of your feet with your hands.
  3. Pull your knees down toward your armpits, keeping your shins vertical.

Form tips

  1. Try to keep your tailbone and lower back on the floor.
