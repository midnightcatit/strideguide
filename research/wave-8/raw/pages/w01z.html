Ground jump through and back

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. From Downward dog , inhale bend the knees push through the arms and jump the legs through the arms.
  2. Extend the legs, exhale.
  3. Inhale, bend the knees and cross the feet in front of you.
  4. Push through the hands, lean forward. Exhale, shoot the legs back, landing in chaturanga ( Bottom pushup hold ).
  5. Inhale, take Upward dog and then exhale, Downward dog .
