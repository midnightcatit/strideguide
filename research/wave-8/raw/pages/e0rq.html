Air pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Perform a standard Pushup .
  2. From the bottom position, push yourself up as explosively as possible.
  3. You will need enough speed to get your whole body off the ground (hands and feet), so that your are airborne for a fraction of a second.

Similar Exercises

  • Behind-the-back clap pushup
  • Chest clap pushup
  • Arms up straddle pushup
  • Clap pushup
  • Superman pushup

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
