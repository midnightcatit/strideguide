Bear walk

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Walk on your hands and feet, with your legs almost straight and your hips up.
  2. Practice until you find a regular, smooth motion.
  3. You can go forward or backward.

Form tips

  1. The knees should bend no more than they do when you walk normally; this requires sufficient Hamstrings flexibility.
