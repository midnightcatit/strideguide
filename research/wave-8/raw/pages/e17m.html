Skater jump (Side jump skater, Skater hop, Speed skater)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg with the knee slightly flexed.
  2. Jump laterally to land on the other foot.
  3. Cross the first leg behind the standing leg while maintaining balance, then immediately jump back.

Form tips

  1. Land softly and stabilize the hip before the next jump.
  2. Use your arms to balance the motion.
