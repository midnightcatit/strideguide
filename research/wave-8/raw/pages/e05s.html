Tuck ice-cream maker

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Perform a Pullup and tuck your knees to your chest.
  2. While staying tucked, lean your torso back to horizontal while straightening your arms.
  3. Pull yourself back to the vertical chin-over-bar position.
  4. Repeat the motion without lowering yourself to the starting position

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms fully straight during the lean back.
