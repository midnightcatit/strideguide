RTO support (Rings turned out support hold, Rings turned out hold, RTO hold, ROT support hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor

Technique

  1. Enter a Rings support position.
  2. Rotate the rings outward until your palms face fully forward.
  3. Hold this isometric position with a straight, tight body.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms locked straight and your shoulders down ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Actively maintain the external rotation throughout the hold.
