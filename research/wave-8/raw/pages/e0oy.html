Supine arch body hold

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Biceps femoris (short)

Technique

  1. Lie with your back on the ground and the arms lying overhead.
  2. Lift your hips up to create an arch going from your heels to your shoulder blades.

  • Neck bridge

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
