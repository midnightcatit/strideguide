Incline dumbbell chest fly

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Lie on an incline bench holding dumbbells above your chest with palms facing each other, arms with a slight bend at the elbow.
  2. Lower the weights out to the sides in a wide arc until you feel a stretch in your chest.
  3. Squeeze your chest to bring the weights back together at the top.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a slight bend in your elbows to protect the joints.
