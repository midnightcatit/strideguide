High-five pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Both partners face each other and perform a Pushup together (see the Pushup for the individual movement).
  2. At the top of each rep, both partners raise opposite hands and high-five while holding a One-arm plank .
  3. Switch the raised hand on every rep to work both sides.

Form tips

  1. Both partners keep a strong Hollow body throughout.
