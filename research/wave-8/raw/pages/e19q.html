Quarter pistol squat (Patrick step up on floor)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Iliacus
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg with the other leg extended straight in front.
  2. Lower your hips approximately one-quarter of the way by bending the standing knee.
  3. Push through the heel to return to a full standing position.

Form tips

  1. Keep the heel of the standing leg firmly planted.
  2. Keep the non-working leg straight and lifted in front.
