Fingertip plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Assume a high Plank position on your hands.
  2. Lift your palms so that your weight is supported only on your fingertips.
  3. Maintain a straight body line by activating your Core .

Form tips

  1. Distribute weight evenly across all fingertips.
  2. Keep the body rigid.
