Wide-stance seated leg press (Wide horizontal leg press, Sumo seated leg press)

  • strength

Technique

  1. Sit in the leg press machine and place your feet wide on the upper portion of the sled, toes pointed slightly outward.
  2. Release the safety latches and lower the platform until your knees bend to 90 degrees.
  3. Drive through your heels to press the platform back to the top position.

Form tips

  1. Ensure your knees track outward in alignment with your toes throughout the push.
  2. Keep your back flat against the backrest.
