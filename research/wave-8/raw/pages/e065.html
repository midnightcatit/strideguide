Knee to elbow trunk twists

  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand with your feet hip-width apart and your hands behind your head, elbows out.
  2. Lift one knee up while twisting your torso to bring the opposite elbow down to touch it.
  3. Return to standing and repeat on the other side, alternating.

Form tips

  1. Keep your back straight: bring the knee up to the elbow rather than rounding forward to reach it.
