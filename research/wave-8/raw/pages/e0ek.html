Single-leg calf raise (Single-leg calf raise off the floor)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg.
  2. Slowly raise your heel off the ground as high as possible, balancing on the ball of your foot.
  3. Pause at the top, then come down slowly to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Do not bounce on the heel; keep control of the motion.
