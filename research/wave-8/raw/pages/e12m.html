Barbell ab rollout

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Place a Barbell on the ground, with a plate attached on each side.
  2. Start from a Pike position with your hands on the Barbell .
  3. Slowly slide forward until your body is parallel to the ground, but still in a slightly Hollow body position.
  4. Pull yourself back up to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep the Abs engaged at all times, with a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Keep the arms and legs straight.

Risks

  1. This can put a lot of strain on your shoulders. Progress slowly to this level of difficulty.
  2. Do not let your back arch.
