Double Toe Hold (Both Big Toe Pose, Ubhaya Padangusthasana)

  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. From a seated position with legs extended in front, lay down on your back.
  2. Take the legs up and over.
  3. Keep the feet together and grab the big toes.
  4. Inhale roll up with straight legs.
  5. Catch the balance on the sit bones with straight arms.
  6. Take the head back, looking up.

Form tips

  1. When rolling up, pull the big toes to slow down the movement
  2. To balance, use opposite actions of pulling the big toes and push the feet back
