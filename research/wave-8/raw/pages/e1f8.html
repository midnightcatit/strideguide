Headstand pike press (Headstand straight legs lift, Sirsasana pike press)

Technique

  1. Set up a Headstand base, walk your toes in toward your head and stack your hips over your shoulders.
  2. Engage your core, lift your feet off the floor and press your straight legs together up to vertical.
  3. Lower them back down to a pike with control.

Form tips

  1. Don't use momentum on the way up or collapse on the way down.
  2. Keep pressing through your forearms so your neck stays light.
