Handstand kick-up (Kick up to handstand, Handstand kick)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Start in a short lunge with your hands on the floor shoulder-width apart, shoulders over your wrists.
  2. Lift the back leg toward vertical and spring gently off the bottom leg to kick up toward a handstand line.
  3. Bring the legs back down softly, one at a time.
  4. Do your reps on your dominant leg, then on the other.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders still over your hands throughout the kick: let the hips and legs do the work.
  2. Don't let your hips or shoulders collapse on the way down.
