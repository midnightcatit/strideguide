Lying leg extension (Double leg extension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Lie on your back with arms vertical and legs elevated with kness bent ( Dead bug ).
  2. Extend the hips and knees together until straight and slightly above the floor without resting the feet on it.
  3. Return to the starting position without arching your back

Form tips

  1. Engage your Abs to stabilize your pelvis.
