Chair l-sit (Bent leg hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Sit on the edge of a chair and place your hands on the seat or armrests.
  2. Push on your hands to lift your entire body weight.
  3. Keep your butt off the chair, your thighs parallel to the floor, and your knees bent.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders pushed down ( Shoulder depression ) .
