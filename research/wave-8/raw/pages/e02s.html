Crow pose to handstand

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Start in a Crow pose position.
  2. Lift your hips up, so that your knees come off your arms.
  3. Keep lifting up, extend your legs and extend your arms at the same time.
  4. End up in a Handstand position.
  5. Reverse the motion back to the starting position with control.
