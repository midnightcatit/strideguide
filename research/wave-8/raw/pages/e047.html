Jump-through

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position with hands on the Parallettes .
  2. Jump your feet forward between your hands while keeping your hands planted.
  3. Pull your knees to your chest and pass your legs through your arms into a reverse plank position.
  4. Low Parallettes make this much harder: start with higher ones so your legs have room to pass.

Form tips

  1. Push down hard into the floor to create maximum height for your legs to pass.
  2. Pull your knees tight to your chest and lift your hips as high as you can.
