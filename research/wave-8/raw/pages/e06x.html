Rings Hefesto

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Start from a Rings support position.
  2. Lower yourself down with control behind the rings until your arms are completely straight behind and above your back.
  3. Hold the end position of the German hang before releasing the rings.

Form tips

  1. Keep the Abs tight and legs straight
  2. Hold the rings near your body

Risks

  1. High risk of biceps and shoulder strain.
