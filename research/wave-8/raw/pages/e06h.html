L-sit one-arm hang

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Hang from a ring or a bar with one arm.
  2. Engage your scapula back ( Shoulder retraction ) and down ( Shoulder depression ).
  3. Lift your legs up until they are horizontal and straight.
  4. Hold this position for time.

  • Monkey bars
