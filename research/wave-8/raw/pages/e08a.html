Rings glute bridge bent legs

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Biceps femoris (long)

  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Lie down on your back, with your feet in the Rings and your butt under the knees.
  2. Push on your feet and activate your Glutes to lift your hips up as high as possible.
  3. Slowly come back down and repeat.
