Bilateral jedi crawl

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in an L-base with the flyer standing over the base's head, facing the base's feet.
  2. The base extends the arms in front of the face and makes a 90-degree angle at the legs.
  3. One leg at a time, the flyer places a shin into each of the base's hands while holding the base's shins just below the knees.
  4. Both partners are at a 90-degree angle in the legs, with the flyer hinged at the hips.
  5. Together, both partners extend the legs, then return to the 90-degree angle, letting the shoulders flex slightly through the movement.
  6. To exit, return to the 90-degree position and the flyer steps down one foot at a time.

Form tips

  1. Both partners keep a Hollow body the whole time.
  2. Hand placement on the shins depends on both people's height; keep the hands on the shin bones to avoid putting pressure on joints like the knees.
