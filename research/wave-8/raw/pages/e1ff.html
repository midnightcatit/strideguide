Elevated upright pigeon (Box pigeon stretch, Standing pigeon on a box)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand in front of a box and place your front leg flat across the top in a pigeon shape, knee bent outward and shin parallel to the front edge. Keep your back leg straight on the floor.
  2. Lengthen your spine upright, chest lifted, shoulders back, hips square to the front.
  3. Hold.

Form tips

  1. Keep your back straight rather than folding forward.
  2. Push your hips slightly forward and down to deepen the glute and hip flexor stretch.
