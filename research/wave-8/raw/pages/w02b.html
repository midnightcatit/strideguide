Front kick-through to one-legged dog

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Perform a Front kick-through .
  2. Upon return to the quadruped position elevate the hips and kick the same leg upward and back to finish in a One-leg downward dog position.
  3. Perform the two movements without resting the foot before switching sides

Form tips

  1. Keep the shoulders active and Core engaged to not swing sideways.
