Scapula weight push (Supine lying shoulder protraction)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Lie on your back, grab a Dumbbell and hold it straight up above your shoulder.
  2. Push your shoulder forward ( Shoulder protraction ) while keeping your back on the ground.
  3. Then bring your shoulder back down.
