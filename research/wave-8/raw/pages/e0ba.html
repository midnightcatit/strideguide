Star jump (Plyo jack, Straddle jump)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start from standing with feet hip-width apart and arms by your sides
  2. Jump explosively into the air as high as possible.
  3. While in the air, spread your arms and legs wide to form a star shape, then pull them back in to land softly.
  4. Repeat the jump continuously

Form tips

  1. Aim for maximum height and fully extend your limbs at the peak of the jump.
