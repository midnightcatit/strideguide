One-arm active hang (One-arm bar hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang from a ring or a bar with one arm.
  2. Engage your scapula back ( Shoulder retraction ) and down ( Shoulder depression ).
  3. Hold this position for time.

Similar Exercises

  • One arm towel hang
  • Climbing towel hang
  • Band-assisted partial one-arm chinup
  • One-arm bent-arm hang
  • Single towel hang

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
