Lat pulldown close grip (Close grip lat pulldown, V-bar lat pulldown)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Attach a close-grip handle (such as a V-bar) to the lat pulldown cable.
  2. Sit tall with your legs anchored under the thigh pads and grasp the handle.
  3. Pull the handle down to your sternum while arching your chest slightly upward, then return smoothly.

Form tips

  1. Pull the bar or handle toward the middle of your chest.
  2. Keep your elbows pointing forward rather than flaring wide.
