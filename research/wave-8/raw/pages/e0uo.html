Shin-support half forward bend (Straight back standing pike on shins)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. From Bent-legs forward fold to ground , inhale and lift the chest and head to lengthen the spine, bringing the hands to the shins and straighten the legs.

Form tips

  1. The idea here is to create an opposing action to the forward fold, so an extension of the spine rather than a flexion of the spine.
