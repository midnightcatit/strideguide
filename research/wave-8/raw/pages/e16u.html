Side bending pose (Urdhva Hastasana Ardha Chandrasana)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand tall with feet grounded and reach one arm overhead.
  2. Lengthen your spine, then bend sideways toward the opposite side until you feel a stretch along the long side.
  3. Breathe deeply, then return to center.

Form tips

  1. Keep both feet pressing evenly and your hips still.
  2. Bend straight sideways without twisting.
