Headstand pike to tuck (Floating pike to tuck headstand)

Technique

  1. Start in a Headstand .
  2. Hinge at the hips to lower your straight legs into a floating pike without touching the floor.
  3. Bend your knees and draw them into your chest into a tight tuck.
  4. Extend back up and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep your feet off the floor throughout.
  2. Move slowly: control in every phase builds the compression strength and balance.
