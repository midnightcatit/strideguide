One-leg stance (Dulibu)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand upright and shift your weight onto one leg
  2. Raise the knee of the other leg at hip height, pointing the foot downward.
  3. Raise the arm on the same side of the elevated leg straight toward the sky, wrist extended and palm up.
  4. Keep the other arm flexed across the torso, hand with wrist extended with palm to the side as in protecting the side

Form tips

  1. Keep your torso tall and your hips facing forward.
