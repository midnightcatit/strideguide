Back support

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Stand next to Stall bars , with your back facing the bars.
  2. Step on the first bar and grab a bar with both hands, with the arms straight alongside your body.
  3. Let your feet hang, so that all your weight is now on your hands and hold that challenging position.
