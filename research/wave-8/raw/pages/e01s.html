Arch body opposite limbs (Arch body alternates)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Lie face down on the ground, with your legs extended and your arms straight overhead.
  2. Lift one leg and the opposite arm off the ground.
  3. Then do the other side and keep alternating.
