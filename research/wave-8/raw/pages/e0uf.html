Back lever lean

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Take an Inverted hang position, then lean backward while keeping your body fully straight: your shoulders, hips, knees and feet should all be on the same line.
  2. Switch from a neutral grip to a supine one to prepare for the Back lever .
  3. The more you lean back, the harder the exercise becomes.
