One-arm toes-to-bar (One-arm hanging leg raises toes-to-hands)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Hang from a bar with only one arm.
  2. Engage your Abs and lift your straight legs upward.
  3. Continue until your feet touch the bar near your hand.

Form tips

  1. Stabilize your torso with your Core to prevent spinning.

Similar Exercises

  • One-arm chinup eccentric
  • One-arm chinup with 2 fingers support
  • Elbow-grab one-arm pullup
  • Hanging legs circle
  • One-arm scapular pull

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
