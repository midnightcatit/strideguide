Partner high rows

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Person one lies face up and person two stands over them, roughly at chest level.
  2. Person one crosses the arms and takes a Clasped hand grip .
  3. Person two performs a row by bending the elbows and pulling person one off the ground.

Form tips

  1. Person two can bend slightly at the hips.
  2. Person one keeps the shoulders engaged, holds the position throughout and avoids arching the back.
  3. Both partners keep the Core engaged.
