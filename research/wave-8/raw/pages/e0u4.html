Box jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Place yourself in front of a strong, stable surface.
  2. Jump on top of it, then jump back down to the starting position.
  3. Aim to make each landing as light as possible, absorbing the shock gracefully by bending your legs.

Risks

  1. If you miss the box, you could get hurt, so progress carefully.
