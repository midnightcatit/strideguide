Bridge pushup (Head bridge, Half bridge)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. From a Bridge position, bend your arms to let your head drop between your arms, until the crown of your head lightly touches the ground.
  2. Then push back up into a full Bridge position, with your shoulders on top of your wrists, and repeat.

  • Elbow bridge
  • Forearm bridge
  • Head tap bridge
  • Wall bridge raise
  • One-leg bridge

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
