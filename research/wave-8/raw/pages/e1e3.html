Cable crunch (Kneeling cable crunch, Rope crunch)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Kneel in front of a high cable pulley holding a rope attachment beside your head.
  2. Hinge slightly forward and hold the rope securely by your ears or forehead.
  3. Contract your Abs to curl your upper body downward, bringing your elbows toward your thighs, then slowly unroll.

Form tips

  1. Flex your spine using your Abs rather than pulling with your arms or hinging at the hips.
  2. Keep your hips stationary over your knees.
