Sliding side split (Slider middle split, Slider adductor slide)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Adductor longus
  • Adductor brevis
  • Adductor magnus
  • Gracilis

Technique

  1. Stand with one foot on a Slider , hands on your hips.
  2. Slowly slide that foot out to the side as far as you can control, lowering your hips toward a side split.
  3. Squeeze your inner thighs to pull the foot back to centre and stand up.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hands off the floor.
  2. Only slide as wide as you can pull yourself back out of.
