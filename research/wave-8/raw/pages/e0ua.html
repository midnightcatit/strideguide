Supine pike fold (Supine pike stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie on your back with the arms overhead.
  2. Lift your legs and roll them back behind you, lifting your hips over your shoulders.
  3. Aim to rest your feet on the ground behind you, around the same place your hands are.
