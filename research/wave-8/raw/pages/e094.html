Palm stretch fingers back (Fingers stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Get on all fours, with the palms flat on the ground, and rotate your hands out all the way until your fingers are facing back.
  2. Lift your plans off the ground, keeping the fingertips on the ground.
  3. You should feel a stretch in your palms and fingers.
