Cable woodchop high to low (Top-to-bottom woodchop, Downward woodchop)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Rectus abdominis
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Stand perpendicular to a Cable machine with the pulley set to a high position, holding the handle with both hands.
  2. Reach up and across your body toward the high pulley.
  3. Pull the cable diagonally downward across your torso toward your opposite knee while rotating your hips, then return under control.

Form tips

  1. Pivot on your back foot as you rotate your torso diagonally downward.
  2. Keep your arms extended and drive the motion with your Obliques and core.
