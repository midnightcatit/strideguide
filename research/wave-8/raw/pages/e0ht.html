Rocking frog

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Get on all fours and open your legs to the sides, while keeping approximately a 90 deg. angle at the knees and the hips.
  2. Get your hips as low as possible.
  3. Slowly rock back and forth to target slightly different areas with the stretch.
