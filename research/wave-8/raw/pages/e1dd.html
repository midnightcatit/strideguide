Stand-up paddleboarding (SUP, Paddleboarding)

  • strength
  • balance
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Rectus abdominis

Technique

  1. Stand in the middle of the board with your feet parallel and hip-width apart, knees slightly bent.
  2. Reach forward, plant the blade fully in the water and pull it back to your feet.
  3. Switch sides every few strokes to keep going straight.

Form tips

  1. Power the stroke by rotating your torso, with your arms fairly straight.
  2. Look at the horizon, not at your feet.

Risks

  1. Wear a leash and a buoyancy aid, and check the wind and currents before you go out.
