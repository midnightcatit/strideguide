Frontal jumping jack (Jumping jack, Skier jack)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand with feet together and arms at your sides.
  2. Jump and split your legs front-to-back while simultaneously swinging the arms up and down
  3. Alternate the motion of legs and arms synchronizing the switch with the jump

Form tips

  1. Keep your movements explosive and synchronized.
