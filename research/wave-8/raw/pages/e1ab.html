Knees ab wheel rollout

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Kneel on the floor with a Ab wheel in front of you.
  2. Slowly roll the wheel forward until your body is extended.
  3. Roll back to the kneeling position using your Abs .

Form tips

  1. Maintain a posterior Pelvic tilt at all times.
  2. Do not let your hips sag toward the floor.
