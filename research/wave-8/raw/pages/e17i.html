Flipped wrist squeeze (Fist with wrist flexion)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Press the backs of your hands on the ground in front of you, fingers pointing forward
  2. Close the fingers in a fist, raising the knuckles off the ground

Form tips

  1. Keep the wrist bent at 90 degrees
