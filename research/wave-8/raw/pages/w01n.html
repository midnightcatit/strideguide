Burpee without jump (No jump no pushup burpee, Sprawl)

  • strength
  • cardio

Technique

  1. Squat down and place your hands on the floor.
  2. Step your feet back into a Plank position.
  3. Step your feet back toward your hands and stand up without the final vertical jump.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a straight line during the plank phase.
  2. Do not let the pelvis sag toward the ground.
