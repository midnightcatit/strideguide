Straps rings planche lean (Straps rings plank)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor

Technique

  1. Attach your arms, around the elbows, to the rings straps.
  2. Get into a Rings plank position, then turn the rings out and lean forward.
  3. Keep the arms straight and the shoulders engaged ( Shoulder depression and Shoulder protraction ).
