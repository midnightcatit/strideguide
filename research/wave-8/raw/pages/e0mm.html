Reverse back bird

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in an L-base , with the flyer at the base's head, facing away.
  2. The base engages the Core to bring the feet to the flyer's Glutes .
  3. Gripping the flyer's ankles, the flyer leans back while the base lifts the arms and straightens the legs, bringing the flyer into the base's Box .
  4. Reverse the actions to exit.

Form tips

  1. The flyer keeps a Hollow body with the neck flexed forward throughout.
