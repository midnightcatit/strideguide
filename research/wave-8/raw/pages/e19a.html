Crossed legs support candle pose

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. From a seated position with legs in front of you, cross the shins.
  2. Lay down and use your hands on your back to lift the legs, sit bones up.

Form tips

  1. Take the chin away from the chest to put weight on the back of the head rather than on your neck.
