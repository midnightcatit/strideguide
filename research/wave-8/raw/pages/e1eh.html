Cable lateral raise stable grip

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Stand next to a low cable pulley and slip your hand through a triangle handle to minimize grip effort.
  2. Hold the cable handle with your outside arm hanging straight down.
  3. Raise the arm out to the side to shoulder height, pause at peak contraction, and lower slowly back down.

Form tips

  1. Lean slightly away from the machine while holding the vertical upright post with your non-working arm.
  2. Keep the working arm moving strictly in the frontal plane.
