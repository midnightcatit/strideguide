Lying rear delt stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie face down with one arm extended across your body, between your chest and the floor.
  2. Press your chest down onto the arm to stretch the back of your shoulder.
  3. Hold, then switch sides.

Form tips

  1. Use your other hand to take some weight off if the stretch is too intense.
