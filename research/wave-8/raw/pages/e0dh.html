Duck walk

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Start in a deep squat position.
  2. Walk forward while staying in the squat, taking small steps and keeping your hips low.

Form tips

  1. Keep your chest up and your back as straight as possible.
  2. Avoid letting your knees collapse inward.

Risks

  1. This exercise places high stress on the knee joints; move slowly and stop if you feel pain.
