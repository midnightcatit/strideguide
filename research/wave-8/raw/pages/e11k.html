Stability ball incline pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Place your hands on a Stability ball and get into a Plank position.
  2. Perform a Pushup , touching the ball lightly with your chest at the bottom.

Form tips

  1. Keep a straight line from the shoulders to the feet, with your Abs engaged and a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Keep your elbows close to the body on the way down; do not let them flare out.
  3. Push your shoulders forward at the top ( Shoulder protraction ).
