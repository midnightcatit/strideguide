Lying handstand line hold (Floor handstand line, Prone handstand shape)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Lie face down with your legs together, toes pointed and arms extended overhead, palms pressed together or holding a Yoga block .
  2. Tuck your pelvis under to flatten your lower back, squeeze your glutes and legs together, and elevate your shoulders toward your ears ( Shoulder elevation ).
  3. Hold.

Form tips

  1. Keep your ribs pulled in and your lower back flat.
  2. This is the exact body line you want when balancing upside down in a Handstand .
