Shoulder row (Horizontal shoulder row, Row scapular retraction)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. The body forms a straight line from shoulders to feet.
  2. Keep the arms straight the whole time and resist the temptation to bend at the elbows: this exercise helps dissociating scapular movement from elbow movement.
  3. Retract your scapulas (shoulder blades) as far as possible, then slowly let them protract again.

Similar Exercises

  • Incline shoulder row
  • Incline row
  • Hanging prone plank
  • Weighted horizontal row
  • Decline row

Equipment

  • Rings
  • Dip station
  • Parallel bars
  • Row bar
  • Suspension straps (TRX)
