Rear delt rings fly Y-shape

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor

Technique

  1. Place your body at an angle, holding the rings in front of you.
  2. Lift the ring up and back, retracting the shoulders at the end of the motion, then slowly go back to the starting position.
  3. Placing your body more vertical will make the movement easier. Choose an angle that is challenging but still allows you to keep good form: no swinging or using momentum, no arching of the back.
