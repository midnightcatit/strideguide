Leg raise circle (Alternating Leg Raises, Flat frog raises)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Lying on your back, legs together, bent at the knees, heels hovering above the floor
  2. Smoothly raise your legs until your feet are directly above your pelvis
  3. Straighten out your legs and slowly lower the feet until the heels are just above the floor

Risks

  1. Keep your back flat against the floor, do not arch your back
  2. Arching back means you need to use easier variation

Similar Exercises

  • Straddle-up
  • Straight-leg reverse crunch
  • Sitting pike compression
  • Double crunch
  • Pike compression flutter kicks

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
