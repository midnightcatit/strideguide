Sitting straddle rotating bounces (Pancake rotating bounces)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit down and open your legs straight out to the sides.
  2. Hinge at the hips to bend forward and do gentle bounces with your body while slowly rotating side to side.
