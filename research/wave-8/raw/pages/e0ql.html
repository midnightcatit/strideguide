Bottom dip hold

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Hold the bottom position of a Dip .
  2. Do not rely on your stretched tissues to hold the position: keep your muscles engaged in order to build strength in that position.
