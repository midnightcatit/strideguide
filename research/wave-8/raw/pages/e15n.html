Incline diamond pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Place your hands on a raised surface close together with fingers forming a diamond.
  2. Lower your chest toward your hands in an inclined Pushup .
  3. Push upward until your arms are fully extended.

Form tips

  1. Keep your elbows close to your body.
  2. Engage the Core to keep a straight line from shoulders to heels
