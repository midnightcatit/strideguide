Band-resisted pistol squat

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Iliacus
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Anchor a Long resistance band under your feet and place it around your neck.
  2. Perform a Pistol squat by squatting on one leg while the other is extended forward.
  3. Push through the heel down to raise yourself back up against the band resistance

Form tips

  1. Use the band to maintain a slow and controlled tempo.
