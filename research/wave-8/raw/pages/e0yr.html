Wall push calf stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Push against a wall (or any other unmovable object) and place boths legs back away from the wall.
  2. Keep the toes pointing forward and keep your heels down, to stretch your achilles tendons and calf muscles.
