Double cable chest fly upward (Double cable chest fly low to high)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Stand in the center of a dual Cable machine with the pulleys set to the lowest position, holding a handle in each hand with palms facing forward.
  2. Step forward with one foot for stability, letting your arms hang down and slightly behind your hips.
  3. Pull the handles upward and inward in a wide arc until your hands meet in front of your upper chest, then lower under control.

Form tips

  1. Keep a slight, fixed bend in your elbows throughout the upward arc.
  2. Maintain a braced Core and avoid leaning backward as you lift the cables.
