Crazy toy soldier front

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Jump on alternate legs. The non-jumping leg is lifted straight up in front of you.
  2. Lift the arm opposite to the lifted leg straight overhead.
  3. Explosively switch from one position to the next.
