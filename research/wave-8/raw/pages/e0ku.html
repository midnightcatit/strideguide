Opening 90-90

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground with the legs bent at 90 degrees ( 90-90 position) and the hands placed on the ground behind your back for support.
  2. Raise the back knee by externally rotating the hip.
  3. Move with control and do not compensate by moving the lower back.
