Preacher bench incline biceps curl (Scott bench incline biceps curl)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Sit on the Preacher bench facing away from the pad, lean your back against it and hold a Dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Let your arms hang straight down beside the pad to feel a full stretch in your Biceps .
  3. Curl the weight upward toward your shoulders, squeeze at the top, and lower back down under control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your upper arms vertical and your elbows back: don't let them drift forward as you curl.
  2. Keep your back against the pad and avoid lifting your shoulders to swing the weight up.
