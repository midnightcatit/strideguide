Inverted shrugs

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Hang upside down with your body straight.
  2. While keeping your arms straight, pull on your shoulders to lift your body up (go into Shoulder elevation ).
  3. Go back down slowly, letting your shoulders get away from your ears ( Shoulder depression ).
