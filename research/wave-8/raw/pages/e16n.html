Lying hip adduction (Side-lying leg lifts)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Adductor longus
  • Adductor brevis
  • Adductor magnus
  • Gracilis

Technique

  1. Lie on your side and cross your top leg over the bottom one, placing the foot on the floor.
  2. Lift your bottom leg straight off the ground as high as possible.
  3. Lower it with control and repeat.

Form tips

  1. Keep the working leg fully extended.
