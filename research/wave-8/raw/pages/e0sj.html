One-leg pushup (Gecko pushup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Start a Plank position: palms on the floor shoulder width apart, directly below your shoulders, legs straight, Abs engaged.
  2. Raise one leg and then slowly lower yourself down.
  3. Pause and then smoothly raise yourself back to the starting position.
