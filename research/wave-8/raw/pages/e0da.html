Wall pec stretch (Biceps stretch, Wall chest stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand next to a wall with the side of your pecs that you want to stretch.
  2. Rest your hand on the wall, then turn away from the wall and push your shoulder into the wall.
