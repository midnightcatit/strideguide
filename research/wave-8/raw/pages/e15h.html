Crossbody hammer curl

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand.
  2. Curl one weight across your body toward the opposite shoulder.
  3. Lower the weight slowly and repeat with the other arm.

Form tips

  1. Keep a neutral (hammer) grip: thumbs up, palms facing your body.
