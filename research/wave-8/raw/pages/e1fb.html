Frog pelvic tilts (Frog pose pelvic tilts)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Kneel, open your knees wide, bend them to 90 degrees with your ankles in line behind them, and place your forearms on the floor in a Frog stretch .
  2. Alternately tilt your pelvis forward (arching your lower back) and backward (tucking your tailbone under).

Form tips

  1. Keep the movement in your hips and pelvis, with steady pressure through your knees.
  2. Go slowly through each tilt to work the full range.
