L-sit pullup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Hang from a bar or rings.
  2. Lift your legs straight up so that they become parallel with the ground.
  3. Perform a pullup while keeping your legs parallel with the ground.

  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
