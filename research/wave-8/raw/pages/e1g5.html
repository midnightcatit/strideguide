Front crawl (Freestyle swimming, Freestyle, Crawl stroke)

  • strength
  • cardio

Technique

  1. Lie face down with your body straight, reach one arm forward and pull the other back under your body to your hip.
  2. Bring each arm forward over the water with a high, relaxed elbow, alternating arms.
  3. Kick steadily from the hips with nearly straight legs and pointed toes.
  4. Turn your head to the side to breathe as the arm on that side pulls back, then turn it back down.

Form tips

  1. Keep your head in line with your body and look at the bottom of the pool.
  2. Roll your body from side to side with each stroke instead of lifting your head to breathe.
