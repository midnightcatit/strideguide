Russian twist

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Rectus abdominis

Technique

  1. Balance on your butt.
  2. Keep your back straight - no rounding!
  3. Alternately twist your torso to one side and the other.
