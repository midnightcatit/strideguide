Machine-assisted pullup (Assisted pullup machine, Machine assisted pull-up, Assisted pull-up machine, Gravitron pullup)

Technique

  1. Select the desired assistance weight and step onto the Assisted pull-up machine .
  2. Grasp the overhead bar with an overhand grip slightly wider than shoulder width.
  3. Pull your chest up toward the bar, driving your elbows downward until your chin clears the bar.
  4. Lower yourself down with control until your arms reach full extension.

Form tips

  1. Initiate the pull by pulling your shoulders down and back ( Shoulder depression and Shoulder retraction ).
  2. Avoid swinging or bouncing on the platform.
