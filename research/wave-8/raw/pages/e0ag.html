Side plank knee-to-elbow

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus
  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Get into a Side plank on a straight arm, hand under your shoulder, feet stacked, and place your top hand behind your head.
  2. Bend your top knee and bring it up towards your top elbow until they touch.
  3. Extend the leg back to the start and repeat, keeping your hips up the whole time.

Form tips

  1. Do not let your shoulder shrug: push the floor away from you.
  2. Keep your hips from sagging as the knee comes in.
