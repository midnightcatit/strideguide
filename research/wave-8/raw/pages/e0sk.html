Staggered pushup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position, bring one hand about half a palm's length forward and the other hand the same distance backward.
  2. Lower yourself slowly, pause at the bottom and smoothly bring yourself back up.
