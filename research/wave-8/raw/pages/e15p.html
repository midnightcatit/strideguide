Elevated planche lean

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Place your feet on a raised surface and hands on the floor in a plank.
  2. Lean forward until your shoulders are well past your wrists.
  3. Hold this isometric position with your body parallel to the ground.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a Hollow body position and straight arms.
  2. Push your shoulders down and forward ( Shoulder protraction ).
