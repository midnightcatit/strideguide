Technique

  1. Stand with a Barbell on the floor, feet shoulder-width apart, bar over your midfoot.
  2. Hinge at the hips and knees and grip the bar slightly wider than shoulder-width, palms facing you.
  3. Explosively extend hips and knees, shrug, and pull the bar upward.
  4. Drop into a full squat and catch the bar on your shoulders, elbows forward.
  5. Stand up.

Form tips

  1. Keep the bar close to your body during the pull.
  2. Keep your back flat: no rounding or excessive arching.
