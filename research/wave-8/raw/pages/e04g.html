90-degree pushup (Handstand to elbow lever to handstand)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Posterior deltoid

Technique

  1. Start from a Handstand position.
  2. Bend your arms to lower down, as if you were going to do a Handstand pushup , except that you lean forward more.
  3. At the bottom, your body is parallel to the floor. That position is the same as the bottom position of a Planche Pushup.
  4. Reverse the motion to go back up to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep your body straight.
