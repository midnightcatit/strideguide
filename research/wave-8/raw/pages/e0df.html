Frog jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Start in a Deep squat position.
  2. Jump forward explosively, using your arms to swing forward to generate momentum.
  3. Land directly back into a squat position to absorb the force.

Form tips

  1. Land softly and absorb the impact with your legs.
  2. Aim to make as little noise as possible when landing.
