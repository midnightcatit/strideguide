Cat stance (Xu Bu, False stance, Empty stance)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Start from a standing position, shift the weight on one leg and extend the other in front of you, the foot lightly touching the ground.
  2. Squat down on the back leg in a Half pistol squat
  3. Bring the arms in front of your chest with one hand in front of the other in a guard

Form tips

  1. All the weight is on the back leg: the front foot barely touches the floor.
