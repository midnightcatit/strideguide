Curtsy shrimp squat

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg.
  2. Cross the other leg behind the support leg diagonally.
  3. Perform a Shrimp squat by lowering until the back knee lightly touches the ground.
  4. Push back up to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep the standing knee aligned with the toes and avoid caving inward.
