Stability ball knee ab rollout

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Kneel down in front of a Stability ball and place your forearms on it.
  2. Slowly roll the ball forward until your shoulders are extended overhead and your body forms a straight line from the knees to the shoulders.
  3. Then roll back to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep the Abs engaged at all times, with a posterior Pelvic tilt .
