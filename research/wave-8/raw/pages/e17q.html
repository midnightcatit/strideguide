Plank hip dips

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Start in a low Plank on your forearms.
  2. Rotate your hips to one side, dipping them toward the floor.
  3. Return to center and dip your hips to the other side.

Form tips

  1. Keep your forearms firmly planted to stabilize your shoulders.
