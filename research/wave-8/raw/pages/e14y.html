Alternating Arnold press

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Hold dumbbells at shoulder height with palms facing your chest.
  2. Press one weight overhead while simultaneously rotating the palm to face forward.
  3. Lower the weight back to the start and repeat with the other arm.

Form tips

  1. Rotate the palms 180 degrees during the movement.
  2. Keep the torso still without arching the back.
