Wall handstand walk

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Enter a Handstand with your chest facing the wall and feet resting against it.
  2. Shift your weight onto one hand and take a small step to the side with the free hand.
  3. Move your feet along the wall in sync with your hands to walk sideways.

Form tips

  1. Keep your Core tight and your arms fully locked.
  2. Maintain your chest facing the wall.
