PB walk (Parallel bar walk)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. From a support position, shift your weight to one side to move the other hand forward, switch side and repeat.
  2. Try to minimize swinging the body side to side.

Risks

  1. Be careful not to miss a step and fall on the bars.
