Rocking fists (Radial-ulnar deviation)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor carpi ulnaris
  • Extensor carpi ulnaris
  • Extensor carpi radialis brevis
  • Extensor carpi radialis longus
  • Flexor carpi radialis
  • Abductor pollicis longus
  • Extensor pollicis brevis
  • Extensor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Get into a Plank position, with your fists on the ground instead of your flat hands.
  2. Bend your wrists back and forth, shifting your whole body forward and backward.

Similar Exercises

  • Knee diamond pushup
  • Knee fingertip pushup
  • Knuckle pushup
  • High plank to low plank
  • Front monkey hop

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
