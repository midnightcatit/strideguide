Hip flexor crunch

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Sit across a bench with one foot firmly held down by a weight or horizontal bar, the other knee lifted toward the chest
  2. Slowly lean backward with your back straight until parallel to the floor at least
  3. Flex at the hips to return upright, driving the movement from the fixed leg

Form tips

  1. Keep a neutral spine the entire movement

  • Chair
  • Bench
  • Low bar
  • Stall bars
