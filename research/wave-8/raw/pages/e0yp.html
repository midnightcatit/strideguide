Tibialis raise (Tibial raise)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Tibialis anterior
  • Extensor digitorum longus

Technique

  1. Place your back against a wall and walk your feet to some distance away from the wall (the further away, the harder the exercise will be).
  2. Lift your toes up by hinging on your heels.
  3. Slowly return to the starting position and repeat.
