Hanging shoulder retraction and protraction

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Engaged your lats by pulling your shoulders down ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Alternately push your shoulders forward ( Shoulder protraction ) and pull them back ( Shoulder retraction ).

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
