Straight leg fire hydrant (Peeing dog, Quadruped hip abduction)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Go on all fours and straighten one leg to the side.
  2. Without twisting your torso but only pulling from your leg, lift the straight leg up and down.
