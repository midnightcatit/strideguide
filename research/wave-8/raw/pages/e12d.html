Banded hamstring curl standing (Resistance band knee flexion)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (short)
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Stand up, anchor an elastic band under one foot and wrap it around the other ankle.
  2. Choose a distance where there is always some tension in the band.
  3. Bend your knee to bring your heel toward your buttocks.
  4. Repeat on both sides.
