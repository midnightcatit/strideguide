Cat Cow

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Get down on all fours, shoulders over wrists and hips over knees.
  2. Inhale and slowly arch your back from the tailbone to the head and look up (cow).
  3. Exhale and round your back from the tailbone to the head and look towards your belly (cat).
