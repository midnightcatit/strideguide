Narrow-stance leg press (Close-stance leg press, Narrow leg press, Feet-together leg press)

  • strength

Technique

  1. Sit in the 45-degree leg press and position your feet close together in the center of the footplate.
  2. Disengage the safety levers and slowly lower the platform toward your chest by bending your knees.
  3. Push through the footplate to extend your legs back up to the top position, maintaining controlled tension on the Quadriceps .

Form tips

  1. Keep your lower back flat against the backrest to prevent pelvis tucking.
  2. Ensure equal pressure across both feet without letting the knees cave inward.
  3. Keep the whole foot in contact, do not elevate the heels.
