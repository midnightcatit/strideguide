Seated vertical jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

  • Gastrocnemius

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Sit on a bench or box with your feet flat on the floor.
  2. Without rocking or lifting the feet first, explode upward by extending your hips and knees to leave the seat.
  3. Jump as high as possible and land softly on both feet.

Form tips

  1. Start from a completely static seated position: no momentum before the jump.
