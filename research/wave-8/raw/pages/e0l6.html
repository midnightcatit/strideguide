Diamond frog

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Piriformis
  • Obturator externus
  • Obturator internus
  • Gemellus inferior
  • Gemellus superior
  • Quadratus femoris

Technique

  1. Lie face down on the ground and bend your knees to the side.
  2. Bring your feet together and, if possible, place a sheet of paper between your feet (to make sure you are keeping the tension while performing the exercise).
  3. While pressing your feet together and keeping the legs bent, push your feet downward.
