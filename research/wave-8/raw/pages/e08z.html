Internally rotated pushup

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position and turn your hands inward as much as possible.
  2. Perform a standard Pushup from this position.

Risks

  1. The shoulder internal rotation puts the shoulder in a position that can lead to impingement, so it is best to limit the use of this exercise, especially if you have limited experience.

Similar Exercises

  • Hand release pushup
  • Knee wrist pushup
  • Palm raise pushup
  • Fingertip pushup
  • Knee archer pushup

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
