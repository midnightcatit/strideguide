Roman chair back extension with twist (Twisting back extension, Rotational hyperextension, 45-degree back extension with rotation, Twisting hyperextension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Lock your feet in the Roman chair with your hips over the pad and hands behind your head.
  2. Lower your upper body toward the floor by bending at the hips.
  3. Lift your torso up until it forms a straight line with your legs, then rotate your shoulders to one side.
  4. Rotate back to center, lower down, and repeat while twisting to the opposite side.

Form tips

  1. Keep your neck neutral and avoid excessive rotation beyond your natural mobility.
  2. Squeeze your glutes at the top of the extension before twisting.
