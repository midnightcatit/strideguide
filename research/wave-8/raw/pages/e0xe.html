Butt balancing pike fold (Butt balancing pike stretch, Ubhaya Padangusthasana, Both Big Toe Pose)

  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Balance on your buttocks on the ground.
  2. Reach between your legs with your arms and grab the outside of each foot with each hand.
  3. Extend your legs upward until they are fully straight while holding on to your feet.
  4. Hold that position for time.

Form tips

  1. Keep your back straight.
  2. Keep your legs together.
