Cable pallof press hold (Pallof hold, Cable anti-rotation hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Stand perpendicular to the Cable machine with the pulley at chest height and hold the handle in both hands.
  2. Step away to create tension and press the handle straight out in front of your chest.
  3. Hold this extended arm position statically for time while resisting rotation, then pull back to your chest.

Form tips

  1. Resist any rotational force through active core engagement.
  2. Keep your shoulders relaxed and down away from your ears.
