Lying side leg raise (Side lying hip abduction)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Lie on your side with the legs straight and stacked on top of each other.
  2. Smoothly raise your top leg as far up as it will go, staying in the same plane as the rest of your body.
  3. Pause at the top and then slowly reverse the motion.
