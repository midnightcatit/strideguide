Dip circles (Support shoulder circles)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Support yourself on Parallel bars or a Dip station .
  2. While keeping your arms straight, perform slow shoulder circles, letting your body move around your shoulder joint.
  3. Make the circles as wide as possible, exploring the four quadrants of this position: Shoulder depression , Shoulder protraction , Shoulder elevation and Shoulder retraction .

Equipment

  • Dip station
  • Parallel bars
