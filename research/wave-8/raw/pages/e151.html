Weighted hair brush (Overhead shoulder rotation)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus

Technique

  1. Stand tall and hold a weight in each hand.
  2. Raise one hand and move the weight around your head as if you were using a hairbrush.
  3. Reach over the opposite side and behind your neck, exploring the full range of your shoulder.

Form tips

  1. Use a small, manageable weight to focus on shoulder mobility.
