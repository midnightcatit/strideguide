Hanging internal rotation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Subscapularis
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Hang from the rings with the arms straight and an overhand grip (palms facing forward).
  2. Rotate your arms inward until your palms face outward ( Shoulder internal rotation ).
  3. Hold the rotated position for the set time.
