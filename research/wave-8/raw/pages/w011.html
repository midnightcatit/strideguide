T-pushup (Pushup with rotation)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Middle deltoid
  • Supraspinatus
  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Start by performing a Pushup .
  2. After coming back up, shift your weight to one hand and open your chest to the side, aiming to form a vertical line with your arms and shoulders ( Side plank ).
  3. Rotate back to a Plank to perform a second Pushup .
  4. Then shift your weight to the other hand to get into a Side plank position on the other side.
  5. Come back to a Plank and repeat.
