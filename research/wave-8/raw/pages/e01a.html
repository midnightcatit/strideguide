Active hang (Bar hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Grip a bar or rings and hang freely.
  2. Pull your shoulders down towards your hips ( Shoulder depression ) and stay in that active position for time.

Form tips

  1. Do not bend your arms at the elbows: keep them straight, to keep the focus on the Shoulder depression . Initially, it might feel weird to dissociate Shoulder depression from Elbow flexion , but it is good to be able to control your joints separately from each other.

Similar Exercises

Equipment

  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
