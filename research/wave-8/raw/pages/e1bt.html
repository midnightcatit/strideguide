Neutral-grip machine chest press (Neutral-grip seated chest press, Close-grip machine chest press, Neutral grip chest press machine)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Sit on the Chest press machine seat with your back flat against the pad and select the desired weight.
  2. Grasp the neutral handles (palms facing each other) at chest height.
  3. Press the handles straight out until your arms are extended in front of you.
  4. Return the handles back under control until a deep stretch is felt in the chest.

Form tips

  1. Keep your elbows tucked close to your torso rather than flared out.
  2. Do not let your shoulders shrug up toward your ears ( Shoulder depression ).
