Dynamic planche lean

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Start from a Downward dog position.
  2. Lower your hips and lean forward as far as possible, moving your shoulders past your wrists.
  3. Return to the starting position and repeat the leaning motion dynamically.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms fully straight and maintain Shoulder protraction .
  2. Maintain a strong Hollow body position with a posterior Pelvic tilt .

Equipment

  • Parallettes
  • Ground (hands or body)
