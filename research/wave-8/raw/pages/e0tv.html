Neck rotation (No-no)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Obliquus capitis inferior
  • Obliquus capitis superior
  • Rectus capitis lateralis
  • Splenius
  • Semispinalis capitis
  • Upper trapezius

Technique

  1. Turn your head to one side, trying to place your chin above your shoulder.
  2. Repeat on the other side.
  3. Do this motion in a slow and controlled manner.
