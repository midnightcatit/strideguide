Incline plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Engage your Abs and keep a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Shoulders are protracted (pushed towards the support surface) to have a slightly rounded upper back.
  3. Keep a straight body or a slightly Hollow body to engage the core more.
  4. Head is neutral, looking at the support surface in front of you.
