Wall pike stretch

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start by facing the wall, a few inches away.
  2. Bend forward to get your back on the wall, placing your hands on the ground.
  3. Straighten the legs and use the wall to push into a deeper stretch.

Form tips

  1. Keep your legs straight and your back as flat as possible against the wall.
