Opposite palms pressed together

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. This is a good way to warmup the wrists and get them ready for more intense activities (such as balancing on your hands).
  2. Stand up and press your palms against each other in opposite directions.
  3. Alternate between the two ways the palms can be pressed against each other. Each position counts as one repetition.
