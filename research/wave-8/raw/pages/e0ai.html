Side to side jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg with a slight bend in the knee.
  2. Jump sideways and land softly on the other leg, bending the knee to absorb the impact.
  3. Immediately jump back and keep alternating.

Form tips

  1. Stick each landing before increasing the distance.
