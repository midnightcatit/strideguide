Iron cross

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. On rings, lower yourself from a Rings support position until your arms are horizontal at your sides, parallel to the ground.
  2. Hold this position, supporting your entire body weight with your arms out like a cross.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms perfectly straight and your shoulders depressed.
  2. Maintain a solid vertical body line.
