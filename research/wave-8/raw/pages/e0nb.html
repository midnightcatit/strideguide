Star

  • balance

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Base starts lying face up and flyer starts by standing at the base's head.
  2. Using a Hand-to-hand grip , the flyer hinges at the hips and places shoulders on the base's feet.
  3. Flyer takes a slight jump into a Tuck position , making sure to load the base's hands and feet.
  4. When stable, extend legs.
  5. To exit, base will lower their feet while the flyer goes back into a tuck, then steps down.

Form tips

  1. Tempo in with a slight rock to calibrate.
  2. Both people should maintain a Hollow body .
  3. Flyer should watch out to not land on the base's head.
