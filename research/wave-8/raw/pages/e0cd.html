Touch sky and earth

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand with feet hip width apart.
  2. Raise your hands to touch the sky, look up, and raise on tiptoes.
  3. Bend at the hips to touch the ground while keeping straight legs, this is one repetition.
