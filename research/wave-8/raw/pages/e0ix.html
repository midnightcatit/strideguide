Serratus rock

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Start from a Low plank position.
  2. Slowly lower your shoulders toward your hands until your nose almost touches the ground.
  3. Push your forearms into the ground to return to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep the core engaged the entire time

  • High plank to low plank
  • Low plank

  • Knee pushup

  • Ground (hands or body)
