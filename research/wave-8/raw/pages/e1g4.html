Technique

  1. Start in the ready position: feet shoulder-width apart, knees bent, weight on the balls of your feet, hands in front at waist height.
  2. Catch high balls with your hands in a W shape behind the ball, thumbs almost touching.
  3. For a low dive, step toward the ball with the near foot and fall sideways onto the side of your body, not onto your elbow.

Form tips

  1. Keep your eyes on the ball until it is in your hands.
  2. Bring the ball into your chest after the catch.
