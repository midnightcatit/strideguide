Kettlebell ulnar deviation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor carpi ulnaris
  • Extensor carpi ulnaris

Technique

  1. Stand tall while holding a kettlebell in your hand by the rear side of the handle, arm by your side, palm facing the thigh.
  2. Lift the kettlebell moving at the wrist as high as possible toward the rear without extending the shoulder.
  3. The bottom of the kettlebell should face backward.
