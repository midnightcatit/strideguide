Double plank

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. The base holds a Plank .
  2. The flyer takes a C-grip on the back of the base's ankles, then hooks their own ankles around the base's upper back ( Upper trapezius ).
  3. To exit, the flyer lowers one foot to the ground, then the other.

Form tips

  1. Both partners maintain a strong Hollow body with an engaged Core .
