Seated bent-over dumbbell row

  • strength

  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Sit on the edge of a bench and lean forward.
  2. Hold dumbbells with your arms hanging straight down.
  3. Pull the weights toward your hips while squeezing your shoulder blades together.

Form tips

  1. Keep your back straight and chest near your thighs.
