Trunk rotation arm swing

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand up and slightly bend your legs.
  2. While keeping your torso upright, twist it in one direction and the other.
  3. Let your arms be totally lose and make them swing when twisting so that they end up hitting your back and torso at the end of each swing.
