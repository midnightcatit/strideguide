Headstand knee lifts (Headstand one-leg lift, Sirsasana prep leg lifts)

  • strength
  • balance

Technique

  1. Set up a Headstand base on your forearms and head, and walk your feet in as close to your elbows as you can so your hips stack over your shoulders.
  2. Keeping one foot on the floor, draw the other knee into your chest.
  3. Hold briefly, lower it and switch sides.

Form tips

  1. Keep at least one foot on the floor at all times.
  2. Push the floor away with your arms to protect your neck while you get used to shifting weight into your hips.
