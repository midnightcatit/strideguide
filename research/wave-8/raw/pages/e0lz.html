Long front plank

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. L-base to start, with the flyer standing at the base's feet.
  2. The base places the feet on the front of the flyer's hips.
  3. The flyer stays in a plank and leans forward while the base brings the knees to the chest, letting the flyer fall into their feet. Connect a Clasped hand grip .
  4. The base walks one foot at a time down to the flyer's feet while the flyer holds a Plank and points the toes, so the base can place their feet on top of the flyer's feet.
  5. To exit, the base walks one foot at a time back to the flyer's hips, then lowers the flyer's feet to the ground.

Form tips

  1. The flyer rounds the shoulders ( Shoulder protraction ) and keeps the weight in the hands and feet, not just the lower body.
  2. Both partners maintain a Hollow body .
