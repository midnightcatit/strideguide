Assisted standing hip circles (Wall-assisted hip rotations)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Piriformis
  • Obturator externus
  • Obturator internus
  • Gemellus inferior
  • Gemellus superior
  • Quadratus femoris
  • Gluteus medius
  • Gluteus minimus

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg while holding onto a wall or sturdy object for balance.
  2. Lift the other knee toward your chest.
  3. Rotate the lifted leg in a large circular motion, moving the knee out to the side, then back and down.

Form tips

  1. Use a stable support to maintain balance.
  2. Move slowly to explore the full range of the hip joint.
  3. Try to not move your pelvis or lower back to compensate
