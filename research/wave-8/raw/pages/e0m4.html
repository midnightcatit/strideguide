Double plank base pushup

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. The base starts in a high Plank while the flyer sets up on top, hands on the base's ankles and feet flexed to hook around the base's Trapezius , forming a Double plank .
  2. The base performs a Pushup while the flyer stays in a high plank.
  3. To exit, reverse the entry with control.

Form tips

  1. Both partners maintain a Hollow body .
