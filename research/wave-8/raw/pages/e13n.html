Single kettlebell snatch

  • strength

Technique

  1. Stand with the feet shoulder width apart and a Kettlebell on the floor slightly in front of you.
  2. Hinge at the hips, grab the handle with one hand and hike the kettlebell back between your legs.
  3. Snap your hips forward to swing it up close to your body, pulling the elbow high.
  4. As it rises past your chest, punch your hand up through the handle so the kettlebell flips softly onto the back of your forearm, arm locked overhead.
  5. Bring it back down along the same path into the next swing.

Form tips

  1. The power comes from the hips, not from pressing with the arm.
  2. Punch through early so the kettlebell does not bang on your forearm.
