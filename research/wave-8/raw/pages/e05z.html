Jackknife pullup (Feet-elevated pullup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Rest your feet on an elevated surface, so that they are placed higher than your hips.
  2. Pull yourself up, keeping the torso upright to imitate the body position of a standard pullup.
  3. The legs can bend or stay straight, whichever works best for you.
