L-sit alternate leg lifts

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground with your legs straight in front of you.
  2. Place your hands on the floor beside your thighs and push down to lift your buttocks off the ground.
  3. While keeping your butt elevated, alternately lift each straight leg up towards your chest.

Form tips

  1. Push your shoulders down to keep them away from your ears ( Shoulder depression ).
