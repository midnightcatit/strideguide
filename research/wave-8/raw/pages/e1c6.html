Single-leg leg press calf raise (One-leg calf press on leg press, Single-leg calf press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Sit in the leg press machine with the ball of one foot on the edge of the footplate and the leg nearly straight.
  2. Flex your ankle to lower the weight, bringing your toes toward your shin.
  3. Push through the ball of the foot to extend the ankle as high as possible.

Form tips

  1. Execute the movement through a full range of motion at the ankle joint with control.
