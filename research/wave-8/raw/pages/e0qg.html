Close grip pullup (Close pullup, Narrow pullup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Place your hands close together on the bar.
  2. Pull your shoulder blades back and drive your elbows back.
  3. Go as high as you can.
  4. Extend your arms on the way down to work the full range of motion.
