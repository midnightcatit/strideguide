Bottom pushup hold (Chaturanga Dandasana, Low pushup hold, Four-Limbed Staff Pose)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Hold the bottom position of a Pushup while hovering just above the ground.
  2. Keep your Abs engaged and a posterior Pelvic tilt .
