Wobble board side to side

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand with both feet on a Wobble balance board with a slight bend at the knees
  2. Title the board side to side slowly shifting your weight from one foot to the other

Form tips

  1. Go slow to improve your proprioception and work the stabilizing muscles
