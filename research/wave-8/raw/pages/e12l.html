Weighted pullup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Attach a weight to your body using a Weight belt or vest.
  2. Hang from a pullup bar with a standard grip.
  3. Perform a Pullup by pulling until your chin is over the bar.

Form tips

  1. Ensure full elbow extension at the bottom of every rep.

Equipment

  • Weight vest
  • Ankle weight
  • Weight belt
  • Hangboard
  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
