Depth jump

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on top of a box or raised surface.
  2. Step off the box and land on the ground with both feet simultaneously.
  3. Immediately upon contact, explode upward into a maximal vertical jump.

Form tips

  1. Do not stiffen your joints upon landing
  2. Do not bend the knee too much to avoid dissipating the energy of the descent
