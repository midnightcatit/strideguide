Hacky sack (Footbag)

  • strength
  • balance

Technique

  1. Drop the footbag and kick it straight up before it reaches the ground.
  2. Keep it in the air with any part of your body other than your hands: feet, knees, thighs, chest or head.
  3. For an inside kick, turn your knee out so the inside of your foot faces up like a flat platform, at about knee height.

Form tips

  1. Kick gently: the footbag should rise to about chest height.
  2. Bend your standing knee and keep your eyes on the footbag.
