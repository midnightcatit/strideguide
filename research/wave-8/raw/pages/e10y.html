Forearm support leg raise (Captain's chair leg raise)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Support yourself on your forearms on parallel bars, with your body vertical and your shoulders pushed away from your ears ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Lift your straight legs up, then slowly back down.
  3. Don't swing: keep your body stable.
