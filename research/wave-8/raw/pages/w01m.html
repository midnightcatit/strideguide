Table top to supported L-sit

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Begin in a Table top position.
  2. Swing your hips backward, passing them between your arms.
  3. Keep your legs lifted into a Two-feet support l-sit and hold for a secondo the position.
  4. Swing between the two positions with control

Form tips

  1. Strongly push your shoulders down ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Keep the chest open.
