Single kettlebell overhead press (Kettlebell one-arm overhead press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, holding a Kettlebell in one hand at shoulder height.
  2. Press it overhead until your elbow is fully straight.
  3. Lower it back to shoulder height with control.

Form tips

  1. Don't arch your lower back to get the weight up.
  2. Keep your wrist straight, in line with your forearm.
