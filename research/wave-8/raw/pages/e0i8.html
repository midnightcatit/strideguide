Pike pull-through

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start from standing and fold forward to place the hands on the Parallettes
  2. Lift the feet and lower your hips into a L-sit with control
  3. Push down to raise your hips again into a floating forward fold

Form tips

  1. Keep your knees locked straight throughout.
