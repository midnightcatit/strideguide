Squat tap and reach

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit in a Deep squat position.
  2. Lower one elbow down to touch the floor.
  3. Then raise the arm straight up to reach the ceiling and twist your torso to look up.

Form tips

  1. Stay low in your squat and keep your weight in your heels.
