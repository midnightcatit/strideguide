Elevated glute bridge (Decline hip thrust)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Biceps femoris (short)

Technique

  1. Lie down on your back, with your feet up on an elevated surface and your butt right under the knees.
  2. Push on your feet and activate your Glutes to lift your hips up as high as possible.
  3. Slowly come back down and repeat.
