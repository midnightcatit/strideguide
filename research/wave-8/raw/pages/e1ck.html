Weighted 45-degree hip extension (Weighted Roman chair hip extension, Weighted 45-degree hyperextension, Kettlebell hip extension on Roman chair)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Position yourself on the 45-degree bench with your upper thighs on the pad and ankles secured, holding a weight plate against your chest.
  2. Hinge at the hips to lower your torso toward the floor.
  3. Squeeze your Glutes and Hamstrings to extend your hips and lift your torso back up.

Form tips

  1. Keep your spine neutral and avoid rounding your upper back.
  2. Drive the movement through hip extension rather than pulling with the lower back.
