Spiral elbow spear (Elbow spear)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Hold a staff horizontally in front of you, with a wide grip and the palms facing up.
  2. Hold one hand steady while the other one pushes the staff inward and rotate the arm in.
  3. Then extend the arm again, with the shoulder fully rotated inward.
  4. Reverse the motion to go back to the initial position.
