Tuck-up

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Start from a Hollow body position.
  2. Lift your upper body, bring your arms in and lift your legs while bending at the knees.
  3. At the top, you balancing on your butt; hold this position for a second before coming back down.
