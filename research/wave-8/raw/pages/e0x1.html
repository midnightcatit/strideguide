Sitting piriformis leg raise hold

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Get into a Sitting piriformis stretch position.
  2. Lift the straight leg off the ground, as high as possible, and hold for time.

Form tips

  1. Stay upright: do not lean back with your torso.
  2. Keep both sit bones on the ground.

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
