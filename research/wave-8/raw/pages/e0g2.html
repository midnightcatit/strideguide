Knee Copenhagen plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Quadratus lumborum
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Adductor longus
  • Adductor brevis
  • Adductor magnus
  • Gracilis

Technique

  1. Place yourself near an elevated surface, such as a Plyo Box , and turn your body sideways, holding yourself up on one extended arm.
  2. Place your top leg's bent knee on the box and hold the other leg up in the air.
  3. Hold for time, then repeat on the other side.

Form tips

  1. Do not let the bottom shoulder elevate towards your ear: push away from your ear and pull the shoulder down towards your hip ( Shoulder depression ).
  2. Do not bend at the hips: keep your upper body and thighs in one plane.
  3. Do not let your hips drop: activate your inner thigh to push them up.

Risks

  1. If the extended arm is hard on your wrist, rest on your forearm instead.
