Wide squat good morning (Standing bent-legs good morning, Horse stance hinge)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Start in the bottom position of a Wide squat (feet wide apart and pointing outward, knees bent around 90 degrees, chest up).
  2. While keeping your back straight, bend at the hips to bring your trunk as low as possible, then slowly come back up.
