Inchworm Lalane pushup

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus

  • Psoas major
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Walk the Inchworm fully out until you are flat on the ground.
  2. Lift into an Arch body hold and twist left and right ( Arch body twist ).
  3. Perform a Fingertip lalanne pushup and walk the inchworm back to standing.
