Single-leg leg press (One-leg leg press, Unilateral leg press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus

Technique

  1. Sit on the leg press unit and place one foot in the middle of the platform.
  2. Lower the platform by bending the knee toward your chest until a 90-degree angle is reached.
  3. Drive through the foot to push the platform back up to full extension.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a smooth descent and avoid letting the sled drop quickly.
  2. Keep the knee aligned with the toes.
