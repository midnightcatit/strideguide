Wall sit

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Sit against a wall, with your hips at knee level, a 90 degree angle at the knees, and your back flat against the wall.
  2. Maintain this position for time.

Form tips

  1. Let your arms hang losely by your side; do not use them to exert pressure on the wall or on your legs (that would be cheating).
