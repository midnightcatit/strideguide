Standing pike on ankles (Standing pike stretch, Standing forward fold on ankles)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand tall with your feet together.
  2. Bend at the hips while keeping the back straight as much as possible.
  3. Support yourself with the hands on the ankles

Form tips

  1. Keep an anterior Pelvic tilt to stretch the Hamstrings
