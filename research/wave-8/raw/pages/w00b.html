Technique

  1. Use your legs and hips to generate power rather than relying solely on your arm.
  2. Rotate the dumbbell smoothly into the rack position to avoid slamming it into your shoulder.
  3. Engage your core to maintain stability and prevent excessive leaning to one side.
  4. Keep your wrist neutral and avoid bending it backward under the weight.
