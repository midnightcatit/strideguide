Hanging tuck windshield wiper

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Hang from a bar or rings and pull your bent knees up in a tucked front lever.
  2. Rotate your hips and legs from side to side, like a windshield wiper.
  3. Maintain control and avoid using momentum.

Form tips

  1. Keep your shoulders down and your back flat against an imaginary plane.
