Sphinx pose leg flutters

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Start in a Sphinx pose .
  2. Lift both legs off the ground by engaging your Glutes and lower back and while maintaining your spine extension.
  3. Make small alternate movements up and down with your straight legs (flutter kicks).
