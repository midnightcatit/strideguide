Wall straddle handstand weight shift (Wall penguin shift, One-arm handstand wall prep)

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Walk up into a straddle handstand with your feet against a wall.
  2. Shift your weight smoothly onto one arm, extending through that shoulder, then back through the centre and onto the other arm.

Form tips

  1. Keep shifting continuously without holding either side.
  2. Elevate the supporting shoulder as you load it: this builds the balance control you need before holding a one-arm handstand.
