Technique

  1. Start in a glide, face down, arms stretched forward and legs together.
  2. Sweep your hands out and back, then bring them together under your chest as you lift your head to breathe.
  3. Draw your heels toward your hips, turn your feet out and kick them around and back together.
  4. Shoot your arms forward and glide before the next stroke: pull, breathe, kick, glide.

Form tips

  1. Push the water with the soles and the insides of your feet, toes turned out.
  2. Keep the arm pull in front of your shoulders.
