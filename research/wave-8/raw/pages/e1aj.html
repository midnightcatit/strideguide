Technique

  1. Hold a dumbbell at chest height and descend into a Deep squat .
  2. Roll backward onto your rounded spine keeping the feet planted, and bring the dumbbell overhead.
  3. Roll forward explosively, swinging the weight forward to help you stand up.

Form tips

  1. Use the dumbbell weight to assist the forward roll and standing transition.
  2. Keep the back rounded during the rolling phase.
