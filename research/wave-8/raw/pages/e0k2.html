Technique

  1. Begin in a standing position with your feet shoulder-width apart.
  2. Optionally, you can hold a weight in front of you, close to your body.
  3. Lower yourself into a squat position, sitting back and down, and then roll onto your back; if you're holding a weight, lower it down above your head before bringing it back to the front.
  4. Use momentum to rock forward, tucking your knees into your chest, and transition back to a standing position.

Risks

  1. Perform this exercise on a padded surface to reduce impact on the spine.
