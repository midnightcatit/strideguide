Squat reach (Squat sky reach, Deep squat upper body twist)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stay in the bottom of a deep squat.
  2. Grab one ankle with the opposite hand and drive the other arm straight up vertically.
  3. Keep your feet flat on the ground and your butt down.
