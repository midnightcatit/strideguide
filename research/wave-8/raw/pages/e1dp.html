Smith machine calf raise single leg

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Position the Smith machine bar across your upper back and stand on one leg with the other leg lifted behind you.
  2. Unhook the bar and drive up through the ball of the working foot into Ankle plantarflexion .
  3. Hold briefly at the top, then lower your heel back to the floor with control.

Form tips

  1. Control the tempo to keep maximum tension on the working calf muscle.
