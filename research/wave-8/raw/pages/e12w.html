Parallettes two-feet support L-sit (L-sit support hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground with your legs straight and the supports by your hips.
  2. Push through the shoulders ( Shoulder depression ) to elevate yourself, keeping the feet on the ground and the arms straight.
