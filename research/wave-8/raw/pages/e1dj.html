Technique

  1. For a forearm pass, join your hands, straighten your arms and contact the ball on your forearms.
  2. Bend your knees and lift with your legs to direct the ball. Do not swing your arms.
  3. For an overhead pass, contact the ball above your forehead with your fingertips and extend your arms and legs.

Form tips

  1. Move your feet to get under the ball before you play it.
  2. Keep your forearms angled toward your target.
