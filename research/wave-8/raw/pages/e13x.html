Single dumbbell front squat

  • strength

  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Hold a single dumbbell with one hand at shoulder height in a rack position
  2. Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart and lower into a squat by bending at the hips and knees.
  3. Drive through your heels to return to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Engage your Core to not tilt sideways
  2. Keep your chest lifted and your back straight as you squat down.
  3. Ensure your knees track in line with your toes.
