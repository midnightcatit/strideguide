Neck hanging rotation

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Sternocleidomastoid
  • Splenius
  • Upper trapezius
  • Rectus capitis lateralis
  • Longus capitis
  • Rectus capitis anterior
  • Longus colli
  • Obliquus capitis inferior
  • Obliquus capitis superior
  • Semispinalis capitis

Technique

  1. Lie on a bench with your head outside the support
  2. Rotate the head to look both sides while keeping the neck in line with the torso

Form tips

  1. Maintain a slow and controlled pace.
  2. Do not force the movement beyond your natural range of motion.

Risks

  1. This exercise places high pressure on the cervical vertebrae; proceed with extreme caution.
