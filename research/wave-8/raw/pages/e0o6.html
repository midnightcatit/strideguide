False grip bent-arm hang

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor carpi radialis
  • Flexor carpi ulnaris
  • Palmaris longus
  • Abductor pollicis longus
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Place your hands into a False grip position around the rings.
  2. Pull yourself into the top position of a Pullup ( Bent-arm hang ).
  3. Aim to pull your shoulders down and back ( Shoulder retraction and Shoulder depression ).
  4. Hold that position for time.
