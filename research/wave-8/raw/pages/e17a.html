Bent-arm one-leg planche (90-degree one-leg hold)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Balance on Parallettes with bent arms, leaning forward.
  2. Extend one leg straight back while tucking the other to your chest.
  3. Hold the horizontal position with your body off the ground.

Form tips

  1. Aim for a 90-degree angle at the elbows.
  2. Keep your back straight and one leg extended.
