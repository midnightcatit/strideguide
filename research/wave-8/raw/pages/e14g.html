Band-assisted rings dip

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Lower trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Kneel into (or step into) an elastic band anchored to the rings and perform a Rings dip . It might take some adjustments to find a convenient way to set up the band.
