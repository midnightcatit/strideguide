Counterweight partial one-arm chinup

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius

Technique

  1. Grab a pullup bar and with the other hand grab a rope or strap attached to a counterweight.
  2. Pull yourself up until your elbow is fully flexed and you chin is above the bar.
  3. Lower down with control until your arm is fully extended.

Form tips

  1. Control the descent the whole way; do not let yourself fall into the bottom position.

  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
