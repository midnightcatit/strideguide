L-sit muscle-up

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)

Technique

  1. Perform a Muscle-up on a bar.
  2. Maintain your legs straight and horizontal in an L-sit the whole time.
  3. Finish in a support position above the bar.

Form tips

  1. Keep your feet high to avoid them hitting the ground.
