L-sit dead hang (L-sit straight-arm hang)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major

Technique

  1. Hang from a High hanging device with a standard grip.
  2. Lift your straight legs up until they are horizontal and parallel to the ground.
  3. Hold this position while hanging freely for the required time.

Form tips

  1. Keep your legs fully straight and toes pointed.
  2. Maintain Shoulder depression to keep your ears away from your shoulders.

  • Doorway or unstable pullup bar
  • Pullup bar
  • Rings
  • Monkey bars
