Standing german hang to tuck Inverted hang

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Place the rings at about chest height.
  2. Stand right under the rings, rotate your arms back and bend your legs, to place some weight on your hands.
  3. Flip forward into a Tuck inverted hang .
  4. Return with control to the starting position.
