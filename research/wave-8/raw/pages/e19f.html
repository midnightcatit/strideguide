Cross-legged bow with arms behind (Yoga mudra progression)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. From a seated position with legs in front of you, cross the legs.
  2. Take the arms back and grab the opposite elbows.
  3. Fold forward with a straight back.

Form tips

  1. Keep grounding the sit bones as you fold forward.
