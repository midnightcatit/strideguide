Split legs handstand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Enter a Handstand .
  2. Split your legs front-to-back, similar to a front split.
  3. Balance in this position, focusing on vertical alignment through the torso.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms locked and push through your shoulders.
  2. Maintain a tight core to stabilize the staggered leg position.
