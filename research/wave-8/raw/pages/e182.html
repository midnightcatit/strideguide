Heel sit (Kiza)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Kneel on the floor with your knees together, toes tucked.
  2. Slowly sit back until your buttocks are resting on your heels.
  3. Place your hands on your thighs and hold the position.

Form tips

  1. Maintain an upright torso with a tall Spine .
