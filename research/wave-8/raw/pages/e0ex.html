Jumping half squat

  • strength
  • cardio

  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Go down until your thighs are parallel to the ground.
  2. Knees stay out: do not let them cave in when you land.
  3. Try to jump higher as you get more comfortable with the exercise.
