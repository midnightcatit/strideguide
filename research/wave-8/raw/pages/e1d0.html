Boxing (Shadow boxing)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Stand in your guard: lead foot forward, knees soft, hands up by your cheeks, chin tucked.
  2. Throw straight punches from the guard, turning your hips and shoulders into each one.
  3. Bring your hand straight back to your guard after every punch.

Form tips

  1. Stay light on the balls of your feet and keep your elbows close to your ribs.
  2. Breathe out sharply with each punch.

Risks

  1. Wrap your hands and wear gloves when hitting a bag or pads, and keep your wrist straight on impact.
