Bar typewriter

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Hang from a bar with a wide grip.
  2. Pull yourself up so that your head goes above the bar.
  3. Slide sideways to one hand, to get your shoulder near that hand, and extend the other arm so that it becomes fully straight.
  4. Then slide to the other hand and keep alternating.
