Weighted single leg calf raise

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg while holding a weight.
  2. Lift your heel as high as possible.
  3. Descend slowly with control.

Form tips

  1. Keep the knee of the working leg very straight.
  2. Do not use a support to help you ascend.
  3. Do not bounce, move with control
