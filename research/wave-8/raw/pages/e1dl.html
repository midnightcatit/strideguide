Double cable chest fly (Cable crossover, Cable chest fly)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Stand in the middle of a dual Cable machine with pulleys at chest height, holding handles with arms spread out to the sides.
  2. Step forward into a staggered stance to create cable tension.
  3. Bring your hands together in front of your chest in a wide hugging arc, pause, then slowly return to the open chest stretch.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a slight bend in your elbows and open your chest wide at the stretch.
  2. Squeeze your Chest muscles as your hands meet in front.
