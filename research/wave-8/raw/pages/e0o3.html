Squat head to floor

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stay in the bottom of a deep squat.
  2. Hinge at the hips to move your body forward between your legs.
  3. Try to get your head as close to the ground as possible, while keeping your feet flat on the ground and your butt down.
