Plie squat with tip toes

  • strength
  • flexibility

  • Adductor brevis
  • Adductor magnus

Technique

  1. Stand with a wide stance and toes pointed outward.
  2. Descend into a squat and lift your heels off the floor.
  3. Stay in the low position of the squat for all the heel lift repetitions

Form tips

  1. Maintain a vertical torso.
  2. Keep knees aligned with the toes.
