Scorpion handstand (Handstand scorpion, Backbend handstand)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Hold a stable freestanding Handstand .
  2. Open your chest, arch your back and bend your knees to bring your feet down toward your head.

Form tips

  1. Keep pressing through your shoulders and fingers to balance.
  2. Isolate the arch in your upper back and shoulders.

Similar Exercises

  • Tuck handstand
  • Forearm stand scorpion
  • Straddle handstand
  • Straight-arm pike press-to-handstand
  • Straddle to diamond handstand

Equipment

  • Parallettes
  • Ground (hands or body)
