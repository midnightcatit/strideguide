Weighted wrist flexion

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor carpi radialis
  • Flexor carpi ulnaris
  • Palmaris longus
  • Abductor pollicis longus
  • Flexor digitorum profondus
  • Flexor digitorum superficialis
  • Flexor pollicis longus

Technique

  1. Hold a weight with an underhand grip (palm facing up).
  2. Lower the weight by bending your wrist downward, then raise it back up, flexing your wrist.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a stable wrist position and isolate the movement to the wrist joint.
  2. Control the movement, ensuring a slow and controlled flexion of the wrist.
  3. Use a comfortable weight that allows you to perform the exercise with proper form.
