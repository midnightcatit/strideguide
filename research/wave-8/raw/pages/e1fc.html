Middle split pelvic tilts (Straddle pelvic tilts)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Step into a wide straddle stance, fingertips on the floor in front of you for light balance.
  2. Alternately tilt your pelvis forward (arching your lower back) and backward (tucking your tailbone under).

Form tips

  1. Keep the movement in your hips and pelvis rather than rounding your upper back.
  2. Keep your core engaged through each tilt.
