Downward dog squat (Bodyweight leg extension)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis
  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)

Technique

  1. Start in a Downward dog position.
  2. Bend your knees toward the floor until they hover just above the ground.
  3. Push through your hands and legs to return to the full Downward dog .

Form tips

  1. Keep your back flat and your hips high.
