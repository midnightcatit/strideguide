Deep pushup (Stretch pushup, Parallettes pushup)

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position, with your hands on Parallettes .
  2. Perform a Pushup , going past your hands on the way down. The lower you go, the more stretch you'll get. This strengthens and stretches your chest muscles at the same time.
  3. Push yourself back up to the starting position.

Form tips

  1. Keep your Abs engaged and a posterior Pelvic tilt .
  2. Keep your head neutral (do not crane forward).
