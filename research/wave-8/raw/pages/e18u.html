Embryo in the Womb Pose holding the feet (Garbha pindasana progression)

  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. From a seated position with legs extended in front, cross the legs at the shins in front of you.
  2. Grab the sides of the feet.
  3. Balance on the sit bones.
  4. Then roll back and forth nine times.

Form tips

  1. When rolling on the back, make sure to roll on the side of the spine.
  2. Use your breath to roll, exhale to roll down, inhale to roll up.
