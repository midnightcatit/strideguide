Partner sitting straddle stretch (Partner pancake stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground with your legs in a wide straddle, knees and toes pointing up.
  2. Your partner sits facing you with their feet against your ankles or shins, and holds your hands or wrists.
  3. Your partner slowly leans back to pull you forward and down, while you fold from the hips.
  4. Hold the position and breathe, then let your partner ease off slowly.

Form tips

  1. Tell your partner when to stop and when to go deeper.
  2. Fold from the hips with a long back rather than rounding the spine.

Risks

  1. The partner should be careful and mindful of you. Go down very slowly and avoid abrupt movements.
