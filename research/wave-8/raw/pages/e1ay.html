Running (Jogging, Sprinting)

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lean your body slightly forward from the ankles.
  2. Lift one knee and push the foot against the ground to propel yourself.
  3. Alternate legs in a rhythmic bounding motion, moving the arms opposite to the legs.

Form tips

  1. Maintain an upright torso with relaxed shoulders, gaze forward.
  2. Let the arms swing naturally by your sides.
