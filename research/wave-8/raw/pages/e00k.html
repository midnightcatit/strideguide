Rings biceps curl

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Start in an incline row position.
  2. Keep your arms perpendicular to your torso (no movement at the shoulder).
  3. Only bend at the elbows, to bring your hands towards your forehead, and your forearms towards your biceps.
