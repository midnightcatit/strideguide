Smith machine romanian deadlift (Smith machine RDL)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Stand inside the Smith machine holding the bar in front of your thighs with an overhand grip and unhook the safety catches.
  2. Keep a slight bend in your knees and hinge at the hips, lowering the bar along your shins until you feel a stretch in your Hamstrings .
  3. Squeeze your Glutes and push your hips forward to return to standing.

Form tips

  1. Push your hips backward as the bar glides down your thighs.
  2. Maintain a flat back with a neutral spine.
