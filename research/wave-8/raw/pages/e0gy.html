Foot-hold dragon squat

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Rectus femoris
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Cross one leg behind the standing one.
  2. Grab the crossing leg with the opposite hand and squat down.
  3. Aim to go all the way down, until your hamstrings touch your calf in the squatting leg.
  4. Aim to keep the held leg straight out and not touching the ground.
