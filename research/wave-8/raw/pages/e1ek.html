Cable romanian deadlift single leg (Cable RDL single leg, Cable hip hinge single leg)

  • strength
  • balance

Technique

  1. Attach a handle to a low cable pulley, stand facing the machine, and hold the handle in the hand opposite your standing leg.
  2. Hinge forward at the hips while extending your free leg straight back behind you until your torso is parallel to the floor.
  3. Drive through the standing heel and contract your Glutes and Hamstrings to return to a tall standing position.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a neutral spine and avoid rounding your back during the hinge.
  2. Keep the free leg straight and extended behind you.
