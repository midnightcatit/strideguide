Technique

  1. Sit on the bike with feet on the pedals and hands on the moving handles.
  2. Pedal with your legs while simultaneously pushing and pulling the handles.
  3. Maintain high intensity, as resistance increases with speed.

Form tips

  1. Coordinate the push and pull of the arms with the pedaling of the legs.
  2. Keep the torso still and avoid swaying side to side.
