Low plank (Elbow plank, Forearm plank)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Face the ground and hold yourself up by resting on your feet and forearms.

Form tips

  1. Engage your Abs and assume a posterior Pelvic tilt . Another way to think about it is to suck your bellybutton upward.
  2. When the Abs are properly engaged, the back should be slightly rounded.

Similar Exercises

  • Serratus rock
  • Scapular pushup
  • Hollow body hold arms down
  • Horizontal plank
  • Supported crossed legs L-sit

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
