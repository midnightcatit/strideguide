Smith machine split squat

  • strength

Technique

  1. Place the Smith machine bar across your upper back, step one foot forward and the other backward into a split stance, and unhook the bar.
  2. Lower your body vertically by bending both knees until your rear knee approaches the floor.
  3. Push through the front heel to return to the top position.

Form tips

  1. Keep your front foot flat and your back heel elevated throughout.
  2. Maintain an upright torso posture.
