Straddle one-arm handstand

  • strength
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Start from the Straddle handstand with 5 fingers support series: use 1-finger support while learning; advanced athletes can simply tent and lift the hand.
  2. Lift the arm and hold it in the same plane as the bottom leg.
  3. Keep both shoulders shrugged ( Shoulder elevation ); do not let the Upper trapezius relax as the hand leaves the floor.
  4. Balance with the fingers and forearm, making frequent small corrections.
  5. Keep the hips slightly bent for a wide, stable Straddle position , legs tense but free to correct.

Form tips

  1. Do not grip the floor hard the whole time: keep the wrist and fingers engaged but relaxed enough for quick corrections.
  2. Experiment with small changes in arm position to learn what you can balance.
  3. Expect a long learning curve; consistency with the basic drills speeds it up.
