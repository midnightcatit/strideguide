Hanging plank shoulder circles (Horizontal row shoulder circles)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor

Technique

  1. Hang under rings or a low bar in a Horizontal row start position: arms straight, heels on the ground, body in a straight line from shoulders to feet.
  2. Keeping the arms straight, slowly move your shoulders backward ( Shoulder retraction ), downward ( Shoulder depression ), forward ( Shoulder protraction ) and upward ( Shoulder elevation ) in a circular motion.
  3. Repeat, then change direction.

Form tips

  1. Resist bending the elbows: this exercise teaches you to dissociate scapular movement from elbow movement.
  2. Go slowly and reach the full range of motion in each direction.
