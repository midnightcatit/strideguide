Dumbbell fly (Dumbbell chest fly)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Lie on a bench.
  2. Grab dumbbells and lower them down to the sides.
  3. Keep a slight bent at the elbows.
  4. Only go low enough to get a slight stretch in your chest.
