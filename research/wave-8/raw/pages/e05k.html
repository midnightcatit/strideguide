Rear delt rings fly T-shape (Incline rear delt ring flys)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Place your body at an angle, holding the rings in front of you.
  2. Pull the rings apart and back to the sides, retracting the shoulders at the end of the motion, then slowly go back to the starting position.
  3. Placing your body more vertical will make the movement easier. Choose an angle that is challenging but still allows you to keep good form: no swinging or using momentum, no arching of the back.
