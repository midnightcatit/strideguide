Elbows turned-out supine plank

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Teres minor
  • Teres major
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Middle trapezius
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Rhomboid major
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Infraspinatus

Technique

  1. Lie on your back with elbows flexed at 90 degrees.
  2. Open your hands to the sides
  3. Push into the ground with your elbows and heels to lift your entire body into a straight line.

Form tips

  1. Keep your chest lifted and hips aligned with your shoulders.
