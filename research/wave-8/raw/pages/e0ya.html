Side leg swing

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Stand on one leg and swing the other one up to the side.
  2. You can swing your arms at the same time to help maintain balance.

Form tips

  1. Start with a small range of motion, then as your muscles get warmer, increase how high you swing the leg to the side to gradually reach your current maximum range of motion.
  2. Aim to keep your legs straight.
