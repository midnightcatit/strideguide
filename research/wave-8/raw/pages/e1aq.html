Swimming

  • strength
  • cardio

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major

Technique

  1. Position yourself in the water in a prone position.
  2. Use rhythmic arm strokes (e.g., reaching forward and pulling back) to move.
  3. Combine arm movements with a continuous leg kick for propulsion.

Form tips

  1. Keep the body streamlined and horizontal in the water.
  2. Coordinate your breathing with the arm cycle.
