Halo

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid

Technique

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, holding a Kettlebell by the horns in front of your chest.
  2. Circle it around your head, close to it.
  3. Alternate directions; each rotation counts as one repetition.

Form tips

  1. Keep your elbows close to your head and your torso still: do not arch your back as the weight goes behind.
