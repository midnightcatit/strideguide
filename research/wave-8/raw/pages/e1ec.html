Cable bent-over reverse fly single arm (Bayesian reverse fly single arm, Cable rear delt fly single arm)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Stand sideways to a low cable pulley, hinge at the hips into a bent-over stance, and grab the handle with the far hand.
  2. Allow your arm to hang down across your torso toward the machine.
  3. Pull the cable outward and upward in a wide arc until your arm is horizontal, squeezing your back, then lower slowly.

Form tips

  1. Keep a flat back and hinge at the hips until your torso is nearly parallel to the floor.
  2. Maintain a slight, fixed bend in your working elbow.
