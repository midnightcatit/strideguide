Smith machine ledge calf raise

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gastrocnemius
  • Soleus
  • Flexor digitorum brevis
  • Flexor digitorum longus
  • Flexor hallucis longus
  • Flexor hallucis brevis

Technique

  1. Place a step under the Smith machine bar, stand on the ledge with the balls of your feet, and rest the bar across your upper back.
  2. Unhook the bar and lower your heels as far as possible below the ledge level.
  3. Press firmly through the balls of your feet to raise your heels as high as possible, pause, and lower under control.

Form tips

  1. Use a calf block or ledge under your feet for full range of motion.
  2. Lower into a deep heel stretch before pushing up into Ankle plantarflexion .
  3. Do not bounce or use momentum.
