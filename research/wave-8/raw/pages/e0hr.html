Supported vertical frog

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit down on a slightly elevated surface (such as a yoga block).
  2. Extend one leg to the side, and bend the other and lift the knee up.
  3. Push your knee out and pull your foot closer to open your hips.
