Sitting ankle circles

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Sit on the ground or on a support.
  2. Rotate one ankle at a time, trying to draw circles as large as possible with your big toe.
  3. Rotate each foot both directions; each rotation counts as one repetition.
