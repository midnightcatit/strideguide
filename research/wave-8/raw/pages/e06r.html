Lying pec stretch (Lying chest stretch)

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Start lying prone (face down) on the floor.
  2. Extend one arm to the side, while the other hand pushes your torso away from the ground.
  3. You can increase the stretch by clasping your hands together behind your back.
