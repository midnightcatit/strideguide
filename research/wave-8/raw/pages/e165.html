Banded hip adduction

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Adductor longus
  • Adductor brevis
  • Adductor magnus
  • Gracilis

Technique

  1. Anchor a band at ankle height and stand with your side to the anchor.
  2. Loop the band around the ankle of the leg closest to the anchor.
  3. Pull the banded leg across your body toward the opposite side.

Form tips

  1. Keep the working leg straight and the standing leg slightly bent for stability.
