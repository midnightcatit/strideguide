Treadmill walking (Treadmill walk, Walking on treadmill, Incline treadmill walking)

  • strength
  • cardio

Technique

  1. Step onto the treadmill belt facing forward and start the machine at a comfortable walking pace.
  2. Walk with a natural stride, landing on your heel and rolling forward to push off your toes.
  3. Swing your arms rhythmically in opposition to your leg strides.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a natural, upright posture with your shoulders relaxed.
  2. Avoid leaning heavily on the handrails.
