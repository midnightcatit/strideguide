Flying splits pose (Eka Pada Koundinyasa 1, One-Legged Sage Koundinya Pose I)

  • strength
  • flexibility
  • balance

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. From downward facing dog, take the left leg across towards the right arm.
  2. Bend the elbows and bring the left leg on top of the right arm. Keep the right leg back.
  3. Lean forward and take the feet off the floor
  4. scissor the legs, keeping the feet pointed and knees straight.
  5. Switch sides

Form tips

  1. Bring the leg across as high as possible on the arm.
