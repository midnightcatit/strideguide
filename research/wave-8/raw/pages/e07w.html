Planche

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis
  • Serratus anterior

Technique

  1. Lean forward onto straight arms and balance with your body as parallel to the ground as possible, legs together and straight.
  2. Push the floor away: shoulders protracted ( Shoulder protraction ) and slightly depressed ( Shoulder depression ).
  3. Tuck the pelvis into a posterior Pelvic tilt and hold a Hollow body with glutes, abs and lower back squeezed.
  4. Keep the neck neutral.

Form tips

  1. On the floor, you may turn the hands slightly outward to reduce wrist strain.
  2. Pointed toes are optional.

  • Parallettes
