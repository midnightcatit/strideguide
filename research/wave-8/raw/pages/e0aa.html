Opposite arm circles

  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart and make large vertical circles with your straight arms.
  2. Rotate one arm forward while the other rotates backward.
  3. Switch directions; each rotation counts as one repetition.

Form tips

  1. Reach all the way behind with each arm.
  2. The coordination takes a few attempts to learn: start slowly.
  3. Avoid arching your back when the arms go behind.
