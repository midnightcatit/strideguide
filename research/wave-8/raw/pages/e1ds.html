Smith machine hip thrust (Smith machine elevated glute bridge)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Biceps femoris (short)

Technique

  1. Sit on the floor with your upper back against a bench and slide your hips under the padded Smith machine bar.
  2. Plant your feet flat on the floor shoulder-width apart and unhook the bar.
  3. Drive through your heels to extend your hips upward until your torso and thighs form a straight horizontal line, then lower with control.

Form tips

  1. Place a pad around the bar to protect your pelvis.
  2. Drive through your heels and squeeze your glutes at top extension.
