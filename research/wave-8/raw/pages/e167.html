Banded half-kneeling thoracic rotation

  • strength
  • flexibility

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Start in a half kneeling position beside a wall, the trailing leg near the wall
  2. Hold a resistance band in your hands and raise your arms to have them in front of you parallel to the floor
  3. Keeping the nearest arm in contact with the wall rotate and open your chest with the other arm against the resistance until it touches the wall on the other side
  4. Slowly return to the starting position resisting the band pull

Form tips

  1. Follow your hand with your gaze to ensure thoracic rotation.
