Straight-arm crow pose

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Lower trapezius
  • Middle deltoid

Technique

  1. Squat down, place your hands on the ground in front of your and place your knees on your upper arms (above the elbow).
  2. Slowly shift your weight to your hands and activate your shoulders and core to bring your legs up.

Form tips

  1. Keep your fingers spread wide on the ground to create a stable base.
  2. Keep your arms straight.
