Scapular pushup (Shoulder pushup, Plank pushup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Serratus anterior
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position.
  2. Go into Shoulder retraction , meaning your body will drop between the shoulders.
  3. Then go into Shoulder protraction , meaning your body will rise between the shoulders.

Form tips

  1. Keep your arms straight the whole time.
  2. Keep your Abs tight and a posterior Pelvic tilt .

Similar Exercises

  • Low plank
  • High plank to low plank
  • Straddle pushup
  • Lying legs raise

Equipment

  • Ground (hands or body)
