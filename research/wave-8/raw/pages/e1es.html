Cable pallof press (Cable anti-rotation press)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Stand perpendicular to a Cable machine with the pulley set at chest height, holding the handle with both hands at your chest.
  2. Step sideways to establish cable tension and assume a stable athletic stance.
  3. Press the handle straight out in front of your chest until your arms are fully extended, pause, and pull it back to your chest.

Form tips

  1. Brace your Core and avoid letting the cable pull your torso toward the anchor.
  2. Stand tall with a neutral spine and shoulders depressed.
