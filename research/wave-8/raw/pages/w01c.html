Tripod headstand forward roll

Technique

  1. This is a way to exit the Tripod headstand position.
  2. Once you are in position, round your back and tuck your chin to your chest to form a ball with your upper body, bend your legs and do a Forward roll .

Risks

  1. This exercise puts a lot of pressure on the head. Make sure to warm-up your neck thoroughly before starting.
  2. If you don't round your back sufficiently, you might fall flat back on the ground.
