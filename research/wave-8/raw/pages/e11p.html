Technique

  1. Stand sideways relative to your direction of travel.
  2. Reach down and plant your hands on the floor one after the other.
  3. Kick your legs overhead one by one, landing on the lead foot and then the trailing foot to return to standing.

Form tips

  1. Kick explosively to gain momentum to complete the movement smoothly.
  2. Try to keep your body in a single plane of motion.
  3. Keep your legs and arms as straight as possible.
