Decline deep pike pushup (Elevated deep pike pushup)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Middle deltoid
  • Triceps brachii (long head)
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)
  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Get your feet up on a elevated surface and your hands on Parallettes , with your hips bent in a Pike position .
  2. Bend your arms and lean forward as you go down, so that the elbows stays above the wrists and the forearms stay vertical.
  3. Push back up until your arms are straight and your shoulders above your wrists.
