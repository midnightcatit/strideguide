Front lever row

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Lower trapezius
  • Triceps brachii (medial head)
  • Triceps brachii (lateral head)

Technique

  1. Hang from a bar or Rings and pull into a full Front lever .
  2. While maintaining the horizontal position, pull your body to the bar by driving your elbows back.
  3. Lower back to the lever position with control.

Form tips

  1. Keep your body perfectly horizontal with no piking at the hips.
  2. Retract your scapulas at the top of the pull.
