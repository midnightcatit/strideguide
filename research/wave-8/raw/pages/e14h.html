Quads foam roll

Skill Tree

Technique

  1. Lie prone on the foam roller under your thighs.
  2. Roll up and down the roller with the help of your arms.
  3. You can lift one leg or twist toward one side to apply more pressure on the other.
  4. You can bend your knee for even more pressure.
