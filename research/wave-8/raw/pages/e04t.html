Hanging leg raises toes-to-hands (Toes-to-bar)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Lower trapezius
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Iliacus
  • Rectus femoris
  • Psoas major
  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Vastus medialis
  • Vastus intermedius
  • Vastus lateralis

Technique

  1. Hang from a pullup bar or a pair of rings with a standard grip.
  2. Lift your straight legs up in a controlled arc toward the bar.
  3. Continue until your toes reach your hands, then lower slowly.

Form tips

  1. Keep your legs fully straight and minimize any swinging.
