Plank row

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior
  • Latissimus dorsi
  • Teres major
  • Lower trapezius
  • Pectoralis minor
  • Biceps brachii (short head)
  • Brachialis
  • Brachioradialis

Technique

  1. Attach a resistance band to a low support in front of you.
  2. Get into a Plank on straight arms facing the anchor, and hold the band in one hand.
  3. Row the band towards your chest, driving the elbow back, while the other arm supports you.
  4. Extend the arm back to the front with control, then switch sides.

Form tips

  1. Keep your hips square to the floor: resist rotating towards the pulling arm.
  2. Spread your feet wider to make balancing easier.
