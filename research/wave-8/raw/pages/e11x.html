Inverted front splits (Rings inverted splits)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Upper trapezius
  • Levator scapulae

Technique

  1. Start in an Inverted hang position on rings or a bar.
  2. Open your legs into a front split, with one leg moving forward and the other backward.
  3. Hold the position while keeping your balance.

Form tips

  1. Maintain a vertical torso while upside down.
  2. Keep your legs fully extended.
