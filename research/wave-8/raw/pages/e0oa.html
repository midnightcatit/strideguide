Straps rings plank circles

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Anterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Serratus anterior
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor

Technique

  1. Attach your arms (around the elbows) to the rings straps.
  2. Get into a Rings plank position, then turn the rings out.
  3. Make circles with your arms.
