Ab crunch machine (Machine crunch, Seated crunch machine, Abdominal crunch machine, Abs machine)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique

Technique

  1. Sit in the Ab crunch machine , place your feet under the foot rollers, and hold the handles at shoulder level.
  2. Exhale and curl your upper torso forward toward your knees, flexing your spine and contracting your core.
  3. Pause at peak contraction, then slowly return to the upright starting position as you inhale.

Form tips

  1. Initiate the movement by contracting your Abs , not by pulling with your arms or pushing with your legs.
  2. Keep your neck neutral and avoid craning your head forward.
