Plank pass through (Plank drag through)

  • strength

Skill Tree

  • Rectus abdominis
  • External oblique
  • Internal oblique
  • Serratus anterior
  • Infraspinatus
  • Teres minor
  • Posterior deltoid
  • Pectoralis major (clavicular head)
  • Pectoralis major (sternal head)
  • Anterior deltoid
  • Rhomboid major
  • Middle trapezius
  • Lower trapezius

Technique

  1. Start in a Plank position, with a weight ( Dumbbell or Kettlebell ) placed on the ground slightly off to the side, at chest level.
  2. Grab the weight with the opposite hand and bring it to the other side of your body, while keeping your body facing the ground.
  3. Then repeat with the other hand, bringing the weight back to its starting position.
