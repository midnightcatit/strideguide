Single-leg 45-degree hip extension (One-leg Roman chair hip extension, Single-leg hyperextension, One-leg 45-degree hip extension)

  • strength

Skill Tree

Muscles

  • Gluteus maximus
  • Semitendinosus
  • Semimembranosus
  • Biceps femoris (long)
  • Spinalis thoracis
  • Longissimus thoracis
  • Iliocostalis

Technique

  1. Set up on a 45-degree hyperextension bench with your upper thighs on the pad, one foot secured under the roller, leaving the other leg free.
  2. Lower your upper body forward by bending at the hip of the working leg.
  3. Contract the Glutes and Hamstrings of the working leg to pull your torso back up into alignment.

Form tips

  1. Keep your spine neutral and focus the work entirely on the target leg's glute and hamstring.
  2. Keep your shoulders square throughout the repetition.
